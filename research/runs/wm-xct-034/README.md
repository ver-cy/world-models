Claude researched the model through twelve independently schema-valid bounded
passes: signature core, payload binding, canonicalization security, binding
projections, trust-chain status, algorithm verdicts, evidence preservation,
governance facts, service layers, preparation and attachment, multi-signature
lifecycle, and verification operations. Local identifiers are disjoint. The
deterministic merger remapped and deduplicated sources, routed 46 coverage
dimensions, selected the compact service-layer pass and revalidated the union.

Several broader requests reached the provider timeout without an accepted
result. Content binding was first separated into content scope and binding
projections, then content scope into payload binding and canonicalization
security. Trust validation was separated into trust-chain status and algorithm
verdicts. Governance and operations were separated into governance facts,
service layers, mutation operations and verification operations; mutation was
finally divided into preparation/attachment and multi-signature lifecycle.
Failed prompts and manifests were removed, no partial output was accepted, and
only the twelve final validated passes entered the synthesis.

The no-tools audit found zero critical conflicts and retained `mixin` as the
best entry kind: a proof has its own version and evidence lifecycle but remains
bound to a host record whose identity and business lifecycle it does not own.
The registry's parent link to WM-XCT-006 Verification / ZK Attestation remains
provisional because the frozen relationship contract is empty and the parent
specification was not supplied for cross-checking.

Primary ETSI, ISO and consolidated eIDAS texts that could not be retrieved
live remain a visible source-verification hold. Post-quantum hybrid/composite
and non-cryptographic hardware-attested proof forms remain explicit research
gaps rather than inferred semantics.

The repository owner explicitly waived Grok for this queue, so no Grok output
was fabricated or inferred. The package visibly retains that single-provider
hold and remains `reviewable-draft`; it is not a final or canonical result.

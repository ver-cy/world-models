# Codex pre-provider boundary: WM-DAT-009 Survey / Questionnaire

Frozen provisional boundary. The root is a versioned questionnaire or survey
instrument aggregate, not a response, respondent, interview, campaign, sample,
consent record, dataset or analysis. A survey study and questionnaire instrument
must remain distinguishable even when one package binds them.

Provisional source families: DDI Lifecycle and Codebook, UNECE GSIM and GSBPM,
W3C PROV-O, SKOS, DPV and WCAG, RFC 3339 and BCP 47, ISO 20252 public metadata,
HL7 FHIR Questionnaire as a health-domain profile, and official statistical
questionnaire-design guidance. Provider output remains untrusted until local
schema and semantic validation.

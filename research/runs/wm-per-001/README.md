Both providers independently classify Person as an `entity`: a referenceable
civil-identity subject. Biological organism data, households and filiation,
organisational roles, location masters, issuing authorities and service ledgers
remain sibling responsibilities. Documents and identifiers are evidence about
the person, not the person itself.

The no-tools adjudication retained Claude's tighter boundary and accepted
alternative registration routes plus an evidence-status transition operation.
Overlapping citizenship, residence, capacity, identity lifecycle, document
issuance, matching and vital-statistics structures were rejected or deferred to
focused research rather than duplicated.

Grok's substantive answer had malformed JSON transport. The immutable raw
wrapper remains outside Git; a no-tools Claude pass repaired syntax/schema only,
and the normalized result passes both schema and semantic validation.

No critical structural conflicts remain. CPV and ISO edition reconciliation,
official GDPR and CRPD verification, live source pins, non-EU profile validation,
coverage-count correction and careful scoping of alternative registration cases
remain publication holds. See `synthesis-plan.json`, `adjudication.json`,
`comparison.json` and `synthesis.validation.json`.

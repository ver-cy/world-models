# WM-DAT-007 bounded provider focus

Research one governed Data Quality Assessment definition and assessment case,
not a Dataset, Data Product, Data Contract, Data Pipeline, validation engine,
monitoring service, issue tracker, remediation task, audit log or certification.

The model owns stable assessment identity and version; assessed subject and
version bindings; purpose, audience and fitness-for-use context; quality model,
categories and dimensions; metric and rule definitions; measurement method,
sampling frame, population, time window, thresholds and tolerances; observations,
results, units, distributions and uncertainty; conformance and fitness verdicts;
evidence, provenance, conflicts and exceptions; issue and remediation references;
approval, publication, expiry, reassessment and correction lineage; access,
retention and loss-aware projections. External masters retain subject identity,
dataset lifecycle, schema or contract, execution, monitoring, issue, remediation,
certification, audit and physical records disposition.

Distinguish metric definition, rule, measurement activity, observation, result,
assessment, verdict, certificate, issue and remediation action. Quality is
fitness for a declared purpose and stakeholder; it is not an intrinsic universal
property. A passed rule, green dashboard or completed run is not proof of global
fitness, correctness, completeness, timeliness, conformance or absence of harm.

# Codex pre-provider boundary: WM-DAT-007 Data Quality Assessment

Frozen provisional boundary. The root is one governed quality-assessment
aggregate, versioned independently of the assessed subject and every execution.
It owns definitions and source-qualified assertions needed to reproduce and
interpret an assessment. It references rather than absorbs Dataset, Data Product,
Data Contract, Pipeline, Measurement Run, Telemetry, Issue, Remediation,
Certificate, Audit and Records masters.

Provisional source families: W3C DQV and DCAT 3, W3C PROV-O, W3C SHACL, JSON
Schema 2020-12, ISO/IEC 25012 public metadata, ISO 19157 public metadata, NIST
data-quality guidance, DAMA or public statistical-quality frameworks where
officially accessible, QUDT or UCUM for units, OWL-Time and RFC 3339.

Provider output remains untrusted until local schema and semantic validation.

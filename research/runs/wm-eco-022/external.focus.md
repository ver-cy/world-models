# External research focus: WM-ECO-022 Subscription

Research one governed recurring commercial relationship aggregate that binds an external agreement, subscriber, provider, plan or offering, subscribed products or services, recurring entitlement, billing cadence, usage or allowance rules, renewal and termination lifecycle. The aggregate may have consumer, enterprise, digital service, membership, content, telecom and utility profiles; do not silently universalize any one sector.

## Frozen root boundary

The subscription owns its instance identity, version and plan binding; provider and subscriber roles; subscribed items and entitlement references; quantity, seats, tiers, usage allowance and limits; term, trial, billing and renewal schedules; recurring price, discounts, taxes, credits and proration policy references; service state, pause, suspension, grace, renewal, cancellation, termination and reactivation assertions; notices, consent and provenance.

Keep these identities external: commercial contract, party, account, product or service offering, entitlement or credential, order, invoice, payment method, payment transaction, usage event or meter reading, service instance, shipment, support case and records disposition. A subscription references these masters and must not duplicate their authoritative lifecycle.

Separate contractual term, entitlement availability, service provisioning, billing schedule, invoice, payment, delinquency and observed usage. A successful payment does not prove entitlement delivery; active status does not prove service availability or legal validity; cancellation request, cancellation effective time and termination completion are distinct.

Separate signup, contract, trial, activation, service, usage, billing-period, invoice, payment, renewal, notice, cancellation-request, cancellation-effective, termination, observation, ingestion and knowledge times. RFC 3339 timestamps require seconds and explicit offset or Z; recurrence rules require timezone, anchor, interval, exceptions and end semantics.

## Required research outcomes

- Resolve subscription versus contract, plan, membership, entitlement, order, service instance, recurring invoice and payment boundaries.
- Cover fixed-term, evergreen, auto-renewing, prepaid, postpaid, usage-based, seat-based, metered, freemium, trial and sponsored profiles.
- Cover plan versions, add-ons, bundles, seats, beneficiaries, quantities, usage allowances, rollover, overage, fair-use constraints and entitlement grants by reference.
- Cover recurring pricing, billing anchors, cycles, proration, discounts, taxes, credits, refunds, dunning and payment status without owning invoice or payment masters.
- Cover renewal, notice, consent, cooling-off or withdrawal where applicable, cancellation, suspension, grace, termination, reactivation, migration, correction, access, privacy, retention and evidence.
- Provide 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 model-specific findings, at least 72 discriminating questions, artifacts and 10 safe functions.

Use primary official sources where possible and pin editions or effective dates. Do not fabricate conformance or legal conclusions. Agents may not autonomously conclude contracts, change plans or prices, charge payment methods, grant or revoke entitlements, cancel, terminate, disclose data or dispose records without delegated authority.

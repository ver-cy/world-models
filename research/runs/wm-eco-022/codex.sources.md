# WM-ECO-022 Codex source notes

The fallback uses official sources from the European Union, UNIDROIT, TM Forum, W3C, the European Payments Council, the IETF, SIX Financial Information and the OpenAPI Initiative. Schema.org is treated as a publisher-maintained discovery vocabulary, not as a universal subscription or contract standard.

EU consumer and digital-content rules support scoped requirements for billing-period prices, term, renewal, termination, continuous supply, modification and reimbursement. TM Forum separates catalogues, installed products, bills and usage observations. ODRL separates offer and agreement policies and their permissions, prohibitions, duties and constraints. EPC direct-debit material provides a payment-mandate profile without turning the subscription into a payment transaction. RFC 5545 and RFC 3339 keep recurrence and timestamp semantics explicit.

Every external mapping is version-pinned and loss-declaring. National legal, tax, consumer, payment, privacy and records requirements remain deferred to adopting-Dimension profiles and authorized review.

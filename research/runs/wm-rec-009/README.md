Both providers independently classify Application / Request Record as an
`entity`. Its boundary runs from the submission act to an explicit disposition
event and the record's later retention or disposal. Form/submission, case and
workflow, substantive adjudication, decision/approval and resulting service or
object retain sibling identities and are connected through typed references.

The no-tools adjudication retained Claude's clearer disposition-bounded scope,
identity, status, evidence, completeness, privacy, timing and handoff coverage.
It accepted Grok's eligibility-criteria and weighting, triggering event,
reported-versus-primary capture and jurisdiction/lodgement findings, plus one
missing status-transition function. Sixteen decisions were recorded and no
critical structural conflict remains.

Grok returned a complete substantive answer in an alternate provider schema.
The immutable raw wrapper stays outside Git; a no-tools pass mapped it to the
frozen Vercy contract without access to Claude, web or shell. Both provider
results pass schema and semantic validation.

Live source pins, GDPR provenance, CCCEV verification, four domain profiles,
accessibility authority, the spatial checklist correction and collection-
approval grounding remain explicit publication holds. See
`synthesis-plan.json`, `adjudication.json`, `comparison.json` and
`synthesis.validation.json`.

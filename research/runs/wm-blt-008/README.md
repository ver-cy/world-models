The adjudicated boundary classifies Site / Campus as an `entity`: a singular
managed physical holding with its own identity, lifecycle, duty holder,
authorisations and record. Recursive site composition and containment of
facilities remain relationships. Parcel, address, settlement, facility,
utility-network, organisation and reporting-boundary identities stay in their
own models and may only be referenced.

The no-tools adjudication retained Claude's stronger neighbour boundaries,
reporting-boundary safeguard, lifecycle, monitoring and emergency coverage. It
accepted Grok's engineering-to-map georeferencing, planning controls and
time-bounded construction-site overlay, plus five missing composition and
governance functions. Fifteen decisions were recorded and no critical
structural conflict remains.

Grok returned a complete substantive answer in an alternate provider schema.
Its immutable raw wrapper remains outside Git; a no-tools transport pass mapped
the answer to the frozen Vercy contract without using Claude, web or shell.
Both final provider documents pass schema and semantic validation.

Live source/version pins, clause-level verification of blocked or paywalled
standards, three materially different site profiles, a non-US jurisdiction
profile and a governed global location identifier remain explicit publication
holds. See `synthesis-plan.json`, `adjudication.json`, `comparison.json` and
`synthesis.validation.json`.

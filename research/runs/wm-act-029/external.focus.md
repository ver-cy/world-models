# WM-ACT-029 bounded external research focus

Produce one concise, complete `model-research.schema.json` result for
`WM-ACT-029 Program / Portfolio`.

## Frozen boundary

- Treat frozen `standalone-mm` as record-plane metadata and choose a subject
  kind independently. Test aggregate versus pattern explicitly.
- Do not pretend program and portfolio are synonyms. A program coordinates
  related components to realize outcomes and benefits; a portfolio selects,
  prioritizes and governs investments aligned to strategy, whose components
  need not be related. Model a required program-or-portfolio profile, shared
  governance surface and profile-specific invariants. Carry a future split hold
  if a single root cannot remain semantically safe.
- Cover identity, mandate, sponsor and governance, strategic alignment, component
  membership and dependencies, objectives and outcomes, benefits and disbenefits,
  investment and funding references, capacity and resources, roadmap and tranches,
  prioritization and balancing, risks and issues, decisions and gates, performance,
  change, lifecycle, provenance, access and retention.
- Separate the collection root and its scoped governance assertions from external
  strategy, objective, project, program, product, operation, benefit, resource,
  budget, contract, risk, issue, decision, metric, observation and audit masters.
- Preserve proposed, approved, active, paused, rebalanced, closed and terminated
  transitions, membership and priority changes and benefit reviews as attributable
  events with event, effective, observation and knowledge time.
- Do not infer delivered benefits from completed outputs, component status or
  spending. Preserve benefit owner, baseline, target, realization observation,
  attribution limits, confidence and post-closure review.

## Size and evidence limits

- Target 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 findings, 72 discriminating questions, 24
  artifacts and 10 functions.
- Use at least 8 primary official sources from at least 4 independent
  organizations. Prefer ISO 21500, 21503, 21504 and 21505, official government
  benefits-management and programme-assurance guidance, W3C PROV and Data Cube,
  OECD result-chain guidance and RFC 3339.
- Every structure and function element needs source references.
- State program-versus-portfolio split, standards access, benefits attribution,
  financial, governance, regional and external-review limitations as holds.

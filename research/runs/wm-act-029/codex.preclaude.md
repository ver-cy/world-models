# WM-ACT-029 Codex pre-provider boundary

Status: preparatory boundary analysis. This is not a provider result.

- Program and portfolio share governance, sponsorship, component membership,
  investment, performance and lifecycle concerns, but their selection and success
  invariants differ and must be profile-qualified.
- A program requires related components, coordinated change and intended outcomes
  or benefits. A portfolio requires strategy-aligned selection, prioritization,
  balancing and capacity allocation; component relatedness is not required.
- The root owns collection identity, membership history, profile-qualified
  governance, roadmap or review cadence, priority and allocation assertions and
  aggregate views. Component projects and programs keep their own masters.
- Outputs, capabilities, outcomes, benefits and disbenefits are distinct. Benefit
  realization remains an observation, not a consequence inferred from component
  completion.
- Approved baseline, target, forecast, actual observation and analyst assessment
  require separate values, periods, units, methods and provenance.
- Component admission, removal, reprioritization, pause and termination require
  decision authority and must preserve the rationale and prior collection state.

Adjudication must reject a Project clone, a Program-only definition presented as
Portfolio, a Portfolio-only investment list presented as Program, destructive
membership changes, double-counted component performance, benefits inferred from
outputs and autonomous funding or termination without delegated authority.

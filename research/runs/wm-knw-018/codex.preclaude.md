# Codex pre-provider hypothesis for WM-KNW-018

## Boundary

Taxonomy / Classification Scheme should be an aggregate for one governed
knowledge-organization scheme and its owned concept definitions, labels,
notations, relations, mappings and immutable release history. Classified
objects and their bindings, source evidence, policies and search indexes remain
external masters.

## Proposed structure

1. Scheme identity, scope, profile, namespace and release.
2. Concepts, labels, definitions, notes and notations.
3. Hierarchy, association, facets, collections and structural rules.
4. Cross-scheme mappings, provenance, confidence and disputes.
5. Governance, lifecycle, change sets, quality and validation.
6. Access, licensing, retention and interoperability projections.

Each bundle should contain two layers and four findings. Each finding should ask
three model-specific questions and define one data element and one serial
artifact. Ten functions should register, define, relate, map, validate, propose,
approve or publish, deprecate or replace, compare or migrate and project.

## Expected hard points

- Concept identity is not a label, code, notation or real-world referent.
- Broader, generic, instance and partitive hierarchies require explicit profiles.
- SKOS exactMatch and closeMatch have different inferential behavior.
- Taxonomies, thesauri, classification schemes and ontologies are related but
  not interchangeable.
- A classification binding should normally point to the scheme, so the
  WM-XCT-020 parent direction requires review.

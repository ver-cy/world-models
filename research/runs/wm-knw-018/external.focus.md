# WM-KNW-018 bounded external research focus

Produce one complete schema-valid result for `WM-KNW-018 Taxonomy /
Classification Scheme`.

Treat the root as one governed versioned knowledge-organization scheme that
defines concepts or classes, their identifiers, labels, definitions, notations,
hierarchical and associative relations, mappings and governance. Keep the scheme
distinct from a concept instance, the real-world entities classified by it, a
classification binding, ontology axioms, a metadata registry, a vocabulary
distribution and a search index.

Cover scheme identity, namespace, canonical URI, title, purpose, scope, subject,
audience, language, jurisdiction, owner, publisher and steward; scheme type and
profile; version, edition, status, release and change set; concept identity,
membership and top concepts; preferred, alternative and hidden labels with
language tags; definitions, scope notes, examples and editorial notes; notations
and code systems; broader, narrower and related relations; polyhierarchy,
facets, collections and ordered collections; allowed cycles and acyclicity
profiles; equivalence, exact, close, broad, narrow and related mappings;
mapping source, target, version, method, confidence, reviewer and provenance;
concept proposal, acceptance, deprecation, replacement, split, merge and
retirement; validation, quality, completeness, orphan, label and relation
checks; access, licensing, retention and loss-aware interoperability.

Keep classified real-world objects, assignments or classification bindings,
source documents, observations, evidence, organizations, agents, policies,
workflows, datasets and search indexes as external masters. Do not equate a
concept with its referent, a label with identity, broader with part-of, related
with hierarchy, closeMatch with exactMatch, mapping with proof of equivalence,
or publication with universal authority. Preserve competing schemes, mappings
and versions without silent last-write-wins.

Review candidate parent `WM-XCT-020 Classification Binding` but do not approve
it automatically. A binding normally refers to a scheme, so the registered
direction may be navigational or reversed and must remain a hold.

Target 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 findings, 72 questions, 24 artifacts and 10
functions. Prefer W3C SKOS Reference and Primer, RDF Schema 1.1, OWL 2,
JSON-LD 1.1, SHACL, PROV-O, DQV and DCAT 3; ISO 25964 parts 1 and 2, ISO 704
and ISO 1087; NISO Z39.19; DCMI Terms; Library of Congress MADS/RDF; BCP 47;
DataCite and RO-Crate. Pin versions or access dates and keep licensing,
language, domain, jurisdiction, reasoning and conformance limits explicit.

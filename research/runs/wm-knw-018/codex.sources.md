# WM-KNW-018 Codex source notes

The synthesis treats a Taxonomy / Classification Scheme as one governed
knowledge-organization aggregate with stable scheme and concept identities.
SKOS supplies the central graph vocabulary for concept schemes, language-tagged
labels, notations, documentation, semantic relations, collections and mappings.
Its integrity and inference rules are kept exact: broader is not automatically
part-of or formal subclassing, exactMatch is transitive, and closeMatch is not.

ISO 25964 and NISO Z39.19 add thesaurus and controlled-vocabulary construction,
maintenance and mapping profiles. ISO 704 and ISO 1087 distinguish objects,
concepts, characteristics, definitions and designations. MADS/RDF independently
reinforces the boundary between an authority record and its real-world object.
ISO/IEC 11179 supplies generic registry identity, designation, definition,
registration, classification and mapping facilities.

RDF Schema, OWL, JSON-LD and SHACL are versioned projections rather than hidden
universal semantics. PROV-O and DQV support change provenance and quality.
DCMI, DCAT, DataCite and RO-Crate support discovery and release packaging. BCP
47 governs label language tags and matching. ODRL governs rights projections;
RFC 3339 provides timestamps with seconds and an explicit offset; RFC 8785
supports deterministic JSON serialization. Licensed standards and pending ISO
revisions remain explicit conformance holds.

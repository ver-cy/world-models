# Codex pre-Claude research: WM-ECO-038 Equity / Security Holding

Boundary hypothesis: one holding is a versioned security position binding a
holder or interest holder, a security instrument, a capacity, an account or
register context and source-qualified quantities over time. It owns the
position identity, balance assertions, lot and rights references, holding-owned
correction lineage and derived eligibility snapshots. It references, but does
not own, the instrument, issuance, account, custody chain, owner or party,
trade, settlement, payment, ledger, tax lot, valuation, rating, collateral,
corporate action, entitlement, instruction, movement, tax, filing and record.

Critical distinctions to test: instrument versus holding; account versus
position; legal, registered, beneficial and economic interests; source position
versus consolidated view; quantity versus percentage, voting power and value;
trade-date versus settlement-date versus effective ownership; settled versus
available, pledged, lent, borrowed, blocked and pending balances; eligibility
versus entitlement, election, instruction, status and outcome; and holding fact
versus regulatory disclosure. The candidate extension of WM-ECO-005 is not
approved.

Primary-source discovery set: BIS, ECB, IMF, IFRS Foundation, EUR-Lex, SEC,
ISO, OMG, EDM Council, ISO 20022, IETF and W3C. External providers must verify
editions, distinguish production from development releases and avoid treating
regional disclosure or accounting rules as universal ownership law.

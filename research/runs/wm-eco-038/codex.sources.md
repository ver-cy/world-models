# WM-ECO-038 source verification notes

Codex used official primary-source pages and documents only. The BIS, ECB and
IMF handbook plus ECB securities-holdings methodology and law establish the
position, flow, holder, issuer, custody, reporting and quality distinctions.
IFRS 9 and IAS 32 support recognition, measurement and presentation boundaries,
but licensed text was not reproduced and no accounting conclusion is claimed.

EU CSDR supports book-entry, securities-account, segregation, settlement and
issue-integrity boundaries. EU shareholder-rights sources and SEC Regulation
13D-G material are regional profiles for shareholder identification and
beneficial-ownership disclosure, not universal ownership law.

ISO 6166, ISO 10962, ISO 17442, ISO 4217 and formal OMG FIGI 1.2 support
identifier and classifier projections. FIBO security-asset and corporate-action
pages support ontology alignment, but master/latest content requires an exact
production-release pin. ISO 20022 supports event, instruction, status, movement,
confirmation and reversal message projections. RFC 3339, PROV-O, DQV and ODRL
cover clocks, lineage, quality and access policies.

The result keeps a source holding distinct from instrument, account, owner,
trade, settlement, payment, ledger, valuation, corporate action and filing
masters. Jurisdiction, security class, account architecture, tax, licensing,
release and implementation conformance remain explicit holds.

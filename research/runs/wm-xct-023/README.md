Both providers independently classify Party Role as a reusable `mixin`. Its
spine is a reified, time-bounded assignment connecting a party/player, host
context and role type. Party master data, host payload and access permissions
remain sibling responsibilities.

The no-tools adjudication retained Claude's finer boundary and accepted Grok's
role-local contact/service context, ultimate-versus-immediate party in agency
chains, credential-conditioned occupancy, standing-assignment versus act-
participation discriminator and RoleClass exclusion gate.

Grok's substantive answer had malformed JSON transport. The immutable raw
wrapper remains outside Git; a no-tools Claude pass repaired syntax/schema only,
and the normalized result passes both schema and semantic validation.

No critical structural conflicts remain. ISO 20022 and FIBO primary evidence,
multi-profile validation, direct HL7/ISO checks, ODRL citation audit and explicit
registry precedence against Ownership/Stewardship and GLEIF relationships remain
publication holds. See `synthesis-plan.json`, `adjudication.json`,
`comparison.json` and `synthesis.validation.json`.

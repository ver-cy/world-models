# WM-ORG-015 independent research boundary

Supplier / Partner Relationship: buyer- or collaboration-scoped qualification,
commercial status and accountable evidence, not party identity, a purchase order
or a universal corporate partnership. Distinguish supplier, vendor, subcontractor,
reseller, alliance partner and service provider using declared role schemes.
Keep buyer scope, supplied categories, qualification evidence/expiry, approval
scope, contractual references, attributed risk/performance, incidents, ownership,
sub-tier dependencies and exit continuity separate. Registration is not approval;
approval is not a contract or permission to pay. Risk evidence is not a legal
finding, sanctions determination or universal automatic blacklist.

Use primary public technical standards and first-party supplier/partner schemas.
Aim at six subject-specific bundles, twelve findings and thirty-six useful
questions with explicit answer fields, six record functions and service layers.
Inspect procurement-party vocabulary and supply-chain due-diligence/risk guidance
without treating a sector-specific profile as universal law. Distinguish supplier
relationship scope from legal control, billing, contract and contact masters.
Do not access private vendor data, credentials or local files. Do not execute
onboarding, supplier exclusion, payments, outreach or purchase commitments.
Return complete model-research.schema.json-compatible JSON with truthful source
scope, provenance, unknowns, source/version and executable-validation limitations.

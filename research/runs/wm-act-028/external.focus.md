# WM-ACT-028 bounded external research focus

Produce one concise, complete `model-research.schema.json` result for
`WM-ACT-028 Campaign`.

## Frozen boundary

- Treat frozen `standalone-mm` as record-plane metadata and choose a subject
  kind. Test aggregate versus project, program, initiative, operation and event.
- Model a governed, coordinated set of activities directed at defined audiences,
  subjects or environments to produce intended outcomes over a bounded period.
- Cover campaign identity, mandate, owner, strategy, objectives, outcome model,
  audiences and segments, scope, phases and waves, channels and touchpoints,
  messages and offers, interventions and activities, resources and budget refs,
  schedule, dependencies, risks, controls, experiments, metrics and attribution.
- Separate the campaign plan and owned campaign-scoped coordination records from
  external projects, programs, initiatives, people, organizations, audiences,
  channels, content assets, orders, contracts, budgets, payments, events,
  incidents, observations, datasets, policy and generic audit masters.
- Represent draft, authorize, launch, pause, resume, change, terminate and close
  as attributable lifecycle events. Preserve event, effective, observation and
  knowledge time and never rewrite released history silently.
- Do not assume that exposure caused an outcome. Preserve attribution method,
  counterfactual limits, confidence, uncertainty, source and measurement window.
- Support marketing, public-information, public-health, fundraising, political,
  advocacy, security and operational profiles without treating any one as the
  universal meaning of campaign.

## Size and evidence limits

- Target 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 findings, 72 discriminating questions, 24
  artifacts and 10 functions.
- Use at least 8 primary official sources from at least 4 independent
  organizations. Prefer OASIS STIX for the campaign boundary, W3C PROV and
  ActivityStreams, Schema.org, ISO or EU project guidance, official public-health
  campaign planning guidance, and first-party campaign API schemas only as
  explicitly scoped interoperability evidence.
- Every structure and function element needs source references.
- State cross-sector vocabulary, legal, consent, political, health, measurement,
  attribution, platform interoperability and external-review limitations as holds.

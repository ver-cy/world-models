# WM-ACT-028 Codex pre-provider boundary

Status: preparatory boundary analysis. This is not a provider result.

- The root is a governed coordination aggregate with a stable identity and
  lifecycle, not a synonym for every project, operation, message, advertisement,
  event or audience interaction it references.
- Objective, intended outcome, target audience, strategy, intervention, activity,
  channel, message, offer, asset, touchpoint, observation and measured result are
  different concepts and must retain separate identities where external masters
  exist.
- The campaign may own its strategy, waves, assignments, selection rules,
  campaign-scoped variants, lifecycle and aggregation views. It references rather
  than absorbs party, content, channel, budget, contract, event and observation
  masters.
- Audience membership and eligibility are time-qualified assertions. Segments,
  exclusions, consent and suppression rules need source, purpose, confidence and
  effective interval.
- Reach, exposure, engagement, conversion, behavior change and societal outcome
  are distinct observations. Attribution never turns correlation into causation.
- Plans, forecasts, targets, actual observations and analyst estimates require
  separate values, periods, units, methods and provenance.
- Cross-channel identity resolution, frequency control, privacy, safety, political
  and health restrictions are policy-bound and jurisdiction-specific.

Adjudication must reject a marketing-only boundary, a project clone, outcomes
inferred from exposure, silent audience expansion, metric denominator loss,
double-counted cross-channel activity, destructive plan changes and autonomous
launch or targeting without delegated authority.

# WM-AI-009 verified source notes

Accessed 2026-09-06. Official public-authority, standards-body and first-party
sources were checked at their canonical pages. Provider wrappers are not sources.

- NIST AI RMF 1.0 and its Generative AI Profile ground governed measurement,
  validation, documentation, risk and accountability. NIST AI 100-2e2025 adds
  current adversarial-ML terminology for poisoning, evasion, privacy and misuse.
- The EU AI Act and GDPR are bounded European Union profiles for training,
  validation and testing data, technical documentation, lawful processing,
  minimization, rights, security and accountability.
- DCAT 3 and PROV-O provide catalog/version/distribution and provenance views.
  They do not define benchmark scoring or turn a benchmark into an evaluation run.
- Croissant 1.0 combines dataset metadata, resources, structure and ML semantics;
  Hugging Face Dataset Cards and Google Data Cards add intended use, creation,
  bias, limitation and lifecycle documentation.
- MLCommons benchmark and MLPerf policy sources distinguish benchmark definition,
  datasets, reference implementation, rules, metrics, runs, submissions and
  reviewed results. This model owns the benchmark definition and release, not runs,
  scores, leaderboards or claims.
- SPDX 3.0.1 Dataset supplies a version-pinned dataset supply-chain projection.
  SKOS supports governed classifications and RFC 3339 supplies timestamp syntax.
- Hidden tests, labels, reference answers, metrics, thresholds, split roles and
  aggregate scores require versioned identities and explicit disclosure controls.

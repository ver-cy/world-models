# WM-PER-008 Education / Qualification external research focus

Research a format-independent learner-centred record connecting education
programmes, participation, assessed learning outcomes, qualification awards,
issued credentials, recognition decisions and portability. Distinguish a
programme, course, learning activity, assessment, achievement, qualification
definition, personal award, credential and downstream authorization.

Cover learner and institution references, education system and jurisdiction,
ISCED and qualification-framework mappings, admission, enrolment, components,
credit, prior learning, outcomes, assessment evidence, grades, moderation,
award authority, conferral, credential issue, correction, reissue, revocation,
verification, transcripts, learner disclosure, recognition and retention.

Keep person and institution identity, teaching content and events, assessment
execution, skills and occupation taxonomies, payments, professional licences,
immigration and cryptographic infrastructure in their own models. Framework
level and foreign recognition are attributable comparability decisions, not
automatic equivalence.

Use current primary official standards such as UNESCO ISCED, qualification
framework authorities, W3C Verifiable Credentials, 1EdTech CLR and Open Badges.
Return model-research.schema.json with direct educational properties,
recognition criteria, governed lifecycles and agent-safe operations. State
jurisdiction, privacy, issuer-authority, terminology, identity and verification
limitations explicitly.

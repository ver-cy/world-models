# WM-PER-008 Education / Qualification independent pre-provider boundary

An Education / Qualification instance is a person's source-qualified learning
and qualification context. It links programme participation and assessed
outcomes to an authoritative award and to one or more credentials that evidence
that award. Credentials do not create the underlying achievement by
themselves, and recognition by another authority is a separate decision.

The model owns learner-programme bindings, progress assertions, outcome and
assessment references, award decisions and personal qualification state,
credential lifecycle links, transcripts, recognition assertions and governed
disclosure. People, institutions, content, teaching events, assessments,
skills, occupations, licences, payments and proof infrastructure remain
external masters.

Required checks:

- Participation, attendance, completion, achievement, qualification, award,
  credential and professional authorization are not interchangeable.
- Every programme, qualification, framework, classification and mapping pins
  authority, jurisdiction and version.
- Awarding authority, credential issuer and recognizing authority may differ.
- Grades and credits preserve their original scale and system before mapping.
- Corrections create successors; revocation preserves a resolvable status and
  never erases the learning history or downstream decision provenance.
- Public verification reveals only the minimum claim and must not expose a
  learner's full transcript, identifiers or sealed records.

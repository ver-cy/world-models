# WM-XCT-027 bounded Claude research plan

WM-XCT-027 connects externally owned risks, controls and residual assessments.
It is researched through three non-overlapping passes so the compound title
does not turn into duplicate risk and control registers.

1. `risk` owns the minimal risk projection, assessment context, criteria and
   inherent/residual comparison needed by the linkage.
2. `control` owns control references, applicability, implementation and
   operating-effectiveness assertions, evidence and coverage relationships.
3. `governance` owns authority, lifecycle, provenance, validation, access,
   retention, review and canonical service rules.

Every pass must be independently schema-valid and use its assigned local-ID
prefix. The deterministic merger remaps sources, unions structure, keeps the
complete boundary from `risk`, keeps service layers from `governance` and
validates the merged result. A separate no-tools adjudication must decide
whether the subject remains a mixin or is better represented as a relationship
or aggregate, without changing the frozen registry plane.

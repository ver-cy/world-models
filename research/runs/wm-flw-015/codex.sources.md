# WM-FLW-015 Codex source notes

The synthesis treats Resource Consumption as one source-qualified use assertion
over a declared consumer-resource boundary and interval. ISO 50001, ISO 50006
and ISO 50015 support energy management, baselines, indicators, measurement and
verification. ISO 14040, ISO 14044 and ISO 14046 bound life-cycle and water
footprint use; ISO 14051 and ISO 14052 support physical material and energy flow
quantification and allocation context.

UN SEEA supplies the main distinction among natural inputs, products, within-
economy flows, returns, residuals, stocks and uses for energy, water and
materials. UN/CEFACT Recommendation 20, QUDT and UCUM qualify units and
conversions. SOSA/SSN and OGC SensorThings keep devices, procedures,
observations, datastreams and results outside the consumption assertion.

RFC 3339, PROV-O, DQV and ODRL support qualified clocks, provenance, quality and
governed access. GRI 301, 302 and 303 are reporting projections for materials,
energy and water; they do not become universal meter, stock, invoice, emissions
or impact masters. Cost, emissions, footprint and impact remain external derived
contexts requiring their own boundary, factor, method and authority.

# WM-MED-008 provider focus

Research the format-independent logical structure for a governed Content
Provenance Credential. Prefer current primary specifications and official
maintenance pages. Keep the result compact enough for the canonical schema:
target 6 bundles, 12-16 layers, 24-30 findings, 3-5 discriminating questions
per finding, at least one concrete artifact per finding and 8-12 functions.

The central identity decision must be explicit: a provenance credential is an
issuer-attributable, integrity-protected set of assertions bound to one or more
media assets or renditions. It is not the asset, creative work, person,
copyright, factual truth, editorial endorsement, trust decision or universal
proof of authenticity. Keep claim, assertion, manifest, ingredient, signature,
certificate, trust policy, validation result and human-facing label distinct.

Investigate at least these official ecosystems where applicable:

- C2PA technical, conformance, trust-list and implementation guidance;
- W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model 2.0, Data Integrity, VC JOSE/COSE,
  Controller Documents, PROV-O and Web Annotation;
- IETF COSE, JWS, X.509 PKIX, time-stamping, RATS, digest fields, JSON
  canonicalization and RFC 3339 timestamps;
- NIST digital-signature, digital-identity and generative-AI risk guidance;
- IPTC digital-source and photo/news metadata guidance;
- durable identifiers, revocation/status, transparency, privacy, selective
  disclosure, archival verification and algorithm-agility concerns.

Separate normative rules from common practice. Pin versions or access dates,
state licensing or access constraints, and expose capture-, edit-, generative-,
redaction-, ingredient-, chain-of-custody-, trust-, revocation-, privacy-,
interoperability- and independent-review holds. Treat a valid signature as
evidence about integrity and the credentialed signer, never as proof that every
assertion or depicted event is true.

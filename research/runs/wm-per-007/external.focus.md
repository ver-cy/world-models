# WM-PER-007 Personal Health external research focus

Research a format-independent longitudinal health context for one person. It
must join clinical, self-reported and device-observed health assertions without
claiming that every assertion is a diagnosis or that one repository is the
universal legal owner. Cover identity matching, conditions, observations,
allergies, medications, immunizations, procedures, encounters, care plans,
goals, risks, functional status, provenance, consent, access, correction,
retention, emergency use and interoperable summaries.

Keep the person, providers, organizations, devices, products, appointments,
insurance, claims, public-health surveillance, research studies, genomics and
care-delivery workflows in their own models. Personal Health owns the
person-grain health assertions and links to the records or events that support
them. Clinical source records remain separately mastered.

Distinguish asserted, observed, clinically verified, refuted, entered-in-error
and unknown facts. Keep event, specimen, effective, issued, recorded,
observation and knowledge times distinct. Preserve code system, version,
display, units, reference ranges, method, body site, interpretation,
uncertainty, data absent reasons and provenance.

Use current primary official standards, including HL7 FHIR R5 and IPS, WHO
ICD-11, SNOMED CT, LOINC, DICOM where relevant, and binding privacy law only as
explicit jurisdiction profiles. Return model-research.schema.json with direct
health properties, recognition criteria, governed lifecycle and agent-safe
operations. State safety, legal, terminology, licensing, identity-matching and
clinical-validation limitations explicitly.

# WM-ACT-035 Codex source notes

The Codex fallback used official primary or first-party material from ISO and
IEC JTC 1/SC 7, W3C, NIST, OGC, NASA, Robot Framework, OpenTelemetry, the UK
National Archives and the IETF.

The ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119-3 and ISO/IEC 17025 catalogue pages expose only an
abstract or public summary. They identify neighboring standards and broad
boundaries but do not support clause-level conformance claims. The ISO 29119
series overview is used only for its explicit public description of process,
documentation and technique separation.

EARL provides the sharp assertion, subject, criterion, result and mode kernel.
OSCAL contributes plan-linked actions, subjects, assets, observations, evidence,
findings and attestations. OGC OMS separates observation act, observed feature,
procedure and result. PROV-O supports attributable plans, activities, agents,
usage, generation, revision and invalidation. NASA contributes test-article
pedigree, support-equipment and verification or validation boundaries. Robot
Framework and the development-stage OpenTelemetry test attributes provide
software execution exchange cases, not universal semantics.

Privacy, retention and timestamps use the NIST Privacy Framework, UK National
Archives guidance and RFC 3339. All mappings remain version-pinned,
source-qualified and loss-aware.

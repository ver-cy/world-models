# WM-ACT-035 bounded external research focus

Produce one concise, complete `model-research.schema.json` result for
`WM-ACT-035 Test Execution`.

## Frozen boundary

- Treat frozen `standalone-mm` as record-plane metadata and choose the subject
  kind independently. Test event, activity and aggregate interpretations.
- Define Test Execution as one attributable occurrence of running a versioned
  test procedure or test case against identified test subjects in a declared
  environment, producing observations, evidence and one or more source-qualified
  result assertions.
- Cover execution identity and lineage, test definition and procedure binding,
  subject and configuration, environment and equipment, inputs and fixtures,
  actors and automation, timing and steps, observations and measurements,
  expected versus actual results, verdicts and oracles, deviations and incidents,
  evidence, reproducibility, review, lifecycle, provenance, access, retention and
  safe agent operations.
- Preserve separate masters for test specification, requirement or criterion,
  subject, product or system version, environment, resource or instrument,
  dataset, defect, incident, assessment, verification or validation claim,
  measurement, observation, artifact and audit record.
- Preserve planned, prepared, started, paused, resumed, aborted, blocked,
  completed, invalidated, reviewed and superseded events as attributable facts.
  A rerun creates a new execution identity linked to the earlier execution.
- Do not infer success from process completion, compliance from a pass verdict,
  defect identity from a failure, or reproducibility from a single run.

## Size and evidence limits

- Target 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 findings, 72 discriminating questions, 24
  artifacts and 10 functions.
- Use at least 8 primary official sources from at least 4 independent
  organizations. Prefer ISO/IEC/IEEE test-process and laboratory boundaries,
  W3C EARL and PROV, NIST OSCAL assessment results, OpenTelemetry test semantic
  conventions, Robot Framework result documentation, NASA verification and
  validation guidance, OGC observations and measurements and RFC 3339.
- Every structure and function element needs source references.
- State cross-domain terminology, oracle semantics, laboratory accreditation,
  safety-critical evidence, privacy, records, regional and external-review
  limitations as holds.

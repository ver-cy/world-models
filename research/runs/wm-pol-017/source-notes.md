# Administrative case: selected-source preparation

Read 2026-09-09. All hierarchy and field groups are authored Vercy proposals,
not universal procedural requirements. No private case or real legal action.

- SRC-001 [OMG CMMN 1.1](https://www.omg.org/spec/CMMN/1.1/PDF), December 2016, formal/2016-12-01. Cover and sections 5.2/5.3.1 inspected: case model, plan and logical case file are distinct; physical storage is not prescribed. Browser metadata incorrectly names Business Motivation Model, while document cover/body identify CMMN 1.1. No conformance or full executable mapping claimed; document/license/errata review pending.
- SRC-002 [German VwVfG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/vwvfg/BJNR012530976.html), German federal statutory source. Selected sections 9, 22, 28, 41 and 43: scope includes administrative act or public-law contract, initiation need not always be an application, hearing has exceptions, notification and effect are qualified. German profile only; do not implement deadlines or legal consequences from these excerpts. Direct section-9 URL failed, complete HTML succeeded.
- SRC-003 [European Code of Good Administrative Behaviour](https://www.ombudsman.europa.eu/en/publication/en/3510), European Ombudsman. Selected grounds, review-route and notification provisions. EU institutional code, not universal national procedure. Its data-protection text cites an old regulation; no current data-protection-law claim is admitted from that clause.
- SRC-004 [Charter Article 41](https://fra.europa.eu/en/eu-charter/charter/article/41-right-good-administration), EU Agency for Fundamental Rights. Selected text scopes good administration to Union institutions/bodies and distinguishes hearing, file access with confidentiality safeguards, and reasoned decisions. No unrestricted public-file disclosure follows.
- SRC-005 [CPSV-AP 3.2.0](https://semiceu.github.io/CPSV-AP/releases/3.2.0/), European Union SEMIC, Recommendation 2024-05-06. Evidence, evidence type and requirements are distinct. Service-level requirements are not case-instance truth; legal resource links do not constitute executable law.
- SRC-006 [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/), W3C Recommendation. Selected qualified attribution/association supports record authors and activity roles, not factual truth, admissibility or legal competence. Dated release and update audit pending.

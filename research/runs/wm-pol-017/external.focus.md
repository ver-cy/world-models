# WM-POL-017 Administrative Procedure / Case

Research one administrative public-law case instance, distinct from court
litigation, a generic workflow template, public service definition and the
authority itself. Distinguish procedure definition and case instance; applicants,
affected parties and representatives; competence, initiation, evidence, hearing,
deadlines, decisions with reasons, notification, administrative review and
closure. Court review is a linked case, not automatically owned here.

Use primary procedural-law examples with explicit jurisdictions, public
administration schemas and case management standards such as OMG CMMN.
Do not universalize hearing rights, time limits, administrative silence,
appeal suspensive effects or finality. A task's completion is not necessarily a
legally effective decision. Filing, receipt, service, decision and effect times
are separate. Party access to a file does not authorize public publication.

Produce complete schema-valid subject-specific bundles/layers/findings/questions,
data elements, artifacts, safe record functions, composition and service layers.
Direct properties, recognition evidence, procedural capabilities and contextual
provenance are required. Local record operations cannot submit applications,
serve notices, issue decisions or determine legal rights. No private case data,
real-person lookup, external transactions or contact with other agents.

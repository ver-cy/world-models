# Proposed negative acceptance fixtures

Not implemented: ex officio opening without application; joined cases preserving
identity; officer assignment without competence; disputed evidence with valid
digest; hearing exception with explicit basis; silence not consent; deadline
without calendar or valid triggering service; workflow completion without legal
effect; sent notice not proven received; administrative appeal without assumed
suspension; court case kept separate; reopened procedure preserving history;
confidential participant access not public release; local deletion not annulment.

Require executable nested schemas, deadline and jurisdiction profiles, authorized
interpretation, expected-head writes and loss-aware round-trip tests before any
operational claim. Research-schema validity alone is not legal compliance.

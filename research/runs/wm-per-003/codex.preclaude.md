# WM-PER-003 Non-human Agent independent pre-provider boundary

## Frozen root

A Non-human Agent instance is a governed actor identity that may bear
attribution for activities and outputs while remaining explicitly distinct
from a human person, organization, software product, AI model, device, robot,
deployment, endpoint and execution session. The model does not infer
personhood, consciousness, intention, rights, duties or liability.

It owns the persistent agent identifier and kind, control and accountable-party
bindings, delegated authority envelope, declared capabilities and constraints,
operating context, activation state, attribution links, oversight controls,
assurance references and lifecycle evidence.

## External boundaries

- Human, organization and accountable-party identities remain external.
- Software, AI system, AI model and physical embodiment lifecycles remain
  external and are pinned by identifier and version.
- Tasks, workflows, activities, outputs, credentials, policies, access
  decisions, incidents and audit records retain independent master systems.
- WM-AI-002 AI Agent is a narrower specialization and must not be duplicated.

## Expected bundles

1. Identity, kind and instance boundary.
2. Control, accountability and stakeholder bindings.
3. Authority, mandate and delegation.
4. Capability, behaviour, constraints and dependencies.
5. Operation, state, attribution and oversight.
6. Assurance, evidence, privacy and lifecycle.
7. Interoperability, projections and safe agent operations.

## Non-negotiable checks

- Capability, permission, responsibility attribution and legal liability remain
  separate concepts.
- Persistent agent, deployed instance, endpoint, session, activity and output
  identifiers are not interchangeable.
- Controller, owner, provider, deployer, operator, beneficiary and accountable
  party may differ and change over time.
- Loss of control, stale delegation, unavailable dependencies and ambiguous
  identity trigger fail-safe behaviour rather than silent continuation.
- Every assertion carries provenance, valid and knowledge time, confidence and
  explicit unknown or contested state where applicable.
- Agents may not broaden their own authority, hide provider or operator
  identity, overwrite attribution history or infer subjecthood from autonomy.

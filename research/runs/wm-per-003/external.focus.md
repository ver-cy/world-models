# WM-PER-003 Non-human Agent external research focus

Research a governed non-human agent as a persistent actor identity that can be
responsible for activities without asserting human status, legal personality,
sentience, consciousness or moral agency. Cover software agents, embodied
agents and other explicitly profiled non-human actor kinds through a common
core, while keeping AI System and AI Agent specializations external.

Cover identity and kind, instance versus deployment and session boundaries,
controller, operator and accountable-party bindings, delegation and mandate,
capabilities versus permissions, tools and dependencies, operating envelope,
activation and safe-state lifecycle, activities and output attribution,
oversight and escalation, assurance and incidents, privacy, retention and
loss-aware interoperability.

Distinguish the persistent governed agent from its software, AI model, physical
embodiment, protocol endpoint, credential, task, activity and execution
session. Technical capability never implies authority. A PROV SoftwareAgent
classification supports provenance attribution but does not establish legal
personhood, intent or liability.

Use current primary official sources. Return model-research.schema.json with
direct computational or organizational properties, recognition criteria,
behaviours, governed state transitions and agent-safe operations. Physical
  properties are delegated to an embodiment model. State jurisdiction and
  deployment assumptions, conflicts, safety risks and deferred profiles.

# WM-ECO-033 Codex source set

Primary official sources: SEC EDGAR filer, submission, message and technical guidance; EU ESEF; EBA and EIOPA reporting frameworks; FCA RegData and SUP 16; Eurostat SDMX and reference-metadata guidance; XBRL 2.1, Report Packages, Taxonomy Packages and Open Information Model; GLEIF LEI data formats; eIDAS; GDPR; RFC 3339; PROV-O; DQV; ODRL; and OpenAPI 3.1.1.

Regulator, sector, jurisdiction, obligation, form, taxonomy, schema, channel, signature, confidentiality and retention rules are versioned profiles. A disclosure revision, delivery attempt, dissemination event, acknowledgement, regulator finding, decision and sanction remain independently governed objects.

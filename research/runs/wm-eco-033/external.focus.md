# WM-ECO-033 external research focus

Research one governed Regulatory Filing / Disclosure aggregate. The slash label covers a versioned regulatory declaration or report package, its filing or publication attempts, and independently attributable acknowledgements, validation outcomes and regulator or repository responses.

Keep these external masters outside the boundary: reporting entity, person, organization, regulator, obligation, legal norm, permit, application or request, investigation or case, decision, assessment, sanction, payment, domain operational fact, generic document and records management. The model may bind or project them but does not own their lifecycles.

Required distinctions:

- filing package or disclosure revision versus transport attempt, public dissemination event, acknowledgement, validation response, regulator decision and enforcement action;
- prepared versus approved versus signed versus submitted versus received versus schema-valid versus accepted-for-processing versus published versus reviewed versus closed;
- reported assertion versus regulator finding, accepted fact, legal compliance, assessment or sanction;
- original, amendment, correction, replacement, withdrawal, restatement and late filing as explicit classes with immutable lineage;
- legal or reporting period, due time, preparation time, approval time, signature time, submission time, receipt time, publication time, observation time and knowledge time;
- logical model versus XML, XBRL, iXBRL, CSV, JSON, API, portal, file or database projection.

Use only primary official sources and standards. Treat jurisdiction, regulator, sector, form, taxonomy, schema, channel, signature and retention requirements as versioned profiles. Every finding needs at least three answerable questions, typed data, an artifact or a justified inline-only rationale, source references and safe agent operations.

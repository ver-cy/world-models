# WM-ECO-028 provider focus

Research a governed Advertisement / Promotion specification, not a campaign, creative-work master, media placement, offer, price, order, contract, audience, party, consent or measurement master.

Resolve the slash boundary explicitly. Test whether advertisement and sales promotion should be represented as two discriminated root profiles in one specification family or whether one is only a reference. Never let an advertisement prove that it was served, viewed, effective, lawful or endorsed, and never let a promotion prove eligibility, redemption, discount settlement or accounting outcome.

Cover at minimum:

- stable identity, subtype, version, language, market, jurisdiction, sponsor and responsible-party references;
- proposition, claim, qualification, evidence, comparative basis, endorsement, sponsorship and required disclosure;
- creative and content bindings without absorbing the Creative Work lifecycle;
- advertised subject, offer, promotion mechanics, eligibility, benefit, limits, exclusions and redemption terms without absorbing price, offer, order or payment masters;
- media, channel, placement, inventory, format, technical delivery, brand-safety and accessibility profiles without absorbing placement or delivery observations;
- planned, approved, released, withdrawn, expired, corrected and superseded states, separated from dissemination and interaction events;
- audience intent and targeting constraints, protected-group and sensitive-attribute prohibitions, minors and vulnerable-person safeguards;
- direct-marketing purpose, lawful basis, consent, preference, objection, suppression and channel-permission distinctions;
- supply-chain transparency, seller and intermediary identities, authorization, fraud signals and provenance;
- impression, viewability, engagement, response, conversion and redemption as external source-qualified observations;
- complaint, challenge, substantiation, takedown, correction, regulator and dispute evidence;
- event time, valid time, publication time, observation time and ingestion time in RFC 3339 with seconds and an explicit offset or Z;
- access, retention, legal hold, minimum disclosure, loss-aware projections and non-destructive history.

Prefer current official IAB Tech Lab specifications, EU legal texts, FTC guidance and rules, W3C recommendations and RFCs. Treat every jurisdictional rule and every channel-specific IAB artefact as a scoped profile, not a universal law or proof of conformance.

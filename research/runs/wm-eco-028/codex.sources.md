# WM-ECO-028 Codex source basis

The Codex fallback used official sources from IAB Tech Lab, GS1, the United States Federal Trade Commission, the European Union, IETF, W3C and the OpenAPI Initiative.

The evidence supports an aggregate specification family with a mandatory discriminator between advertisement and sales-promotion instances. It does not make a channel, taxonomy, programmatic protocol, coupon mechanism, jurisdiction, legal conclusion or measurement method universal. IAB artefacts are digital-advertising profiles, GS1 artefacts are coupon profiles, and legal sources are jurisdiction-scoped. Direct automated access to two GS1 pages was publisher-blocked, but both documents were verified through official indexed content.

Claude Sonnet and Grok 4.6 each timed out on the single bounded attempt. No external provider output or unverified provider fact was admitted.

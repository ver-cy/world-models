# WM-ACT-030 Codex pre-provider boundary

Status: preparatory boundary analysis. This is not a provider result.

- An Initiative is the governed root of an intentional course of change or
  exploration when the work has not necessarily acquired a Project, Program,
  Campaign or enduring Operation identity.
- The root owns initiative identity, intent, scope hypothesis, sponsorship,
  option and experiment context, gates, initiative-scoped coordination and the
  derivation history of formal delivery objects. External work objects retain
  their own identity and lifecycle.
- Strategic objective, policy mandate, problem or opportunity, proposed output,
  desired outcome, benefit, metric and observed result are different assertions.
- Proposed, screened, sponsored, approved, incubating, active, formalized,
  absorbed, paused, cancelled, completed and superseded states must be
  profile-qualified and append-only.
- Formalization into a Project or Program creates a typed derivation and
  responsibility transfer; it never silently renames the Initiative.
- Initiative is cross-sector language with weak universal standardization.
  Source-specific profiles and explicit semantics are mandatory.

Adjudication must reject a Project clone, a Strategy or Objective clone, an
untyped list of work, inferred authorization, overwritten scope or state,
benefit realization inferred from activity, and autonomous commitment of
funding or transition into formal delivery without delegated authority.

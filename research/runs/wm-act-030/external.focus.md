# WM-ACT-030 bounded external research focus

Produce one concise, complete `model-research.schema.json` result for
`WM-ACT-030 Initiative`.

## Frozen boundary

- Treat frozen `standalone-mm` as record-plane metadata and choose a subject
  kind independently. Test aggregate versus entity and pattern explicitly.
- Define Initiative as a governed, intentional course of coordinated change or
  exploration that may exist before, outside or across a formal project,
  programme, campaign or operational change. It is not a synonym for any of
  those neighbors.
- Cover identity and type, originating need or opportunity, mandate, sponsor,
  intended scope, hypotheses and assumptions, desired outputs and outcomes,
  affected stakeholders, linked work, dependencies, options and experiments,
  roadmap and decision gates, resource and funding envelopes, risk and controls,
  progress and result evidence, transition into or across formal delivery,
  lifecycle, provenance, access, retention and safe agent operations.
- Preserve the distinction between an initiative root and external strategy,
  objective, policy, project, programme, portfolio, campaign, activity, work
  package, experiment, capability, product, service, resource, budget, risk,
  decision, metric, observation and audit masters.
- Preserve proposal, discovery, assessment, sponsorship, approval, incubation,
  activation, formalization, absorption, pause, cancellation, completion and
  supersession as attributable events. A new project or programme derived from
  an initiative does not erase the initiative or inherit its identity.
- Do not infer approval, delivery, outcome, benefit or causal impact from a
  label, budget line, task completion or linked project state.

## Size and evidence limits

- Target 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 findings, 72 discriminating questions, 24
  artifacts and 10 functions.
- Use at least 8 primary official sources from at least 4 independent
  organizations. Prefer official ISO project/programme/portfolio boundaries,
  The Open Group ArchiMate strategy and implementation concepts, IATI activity
  and results standards, UK or EU government business-case and project-delivery
  guidance, W3C ActivityStreams and PROV, OECD results-chain guidance and RFC
  3339.
- Every structure and function element needs source references.
- State terminology, cross-sector, transformation, benefits attribution,
  regional, records, privacy and external-review limitations as holds.

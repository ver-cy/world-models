# Acceptance design, not implemented runtime tests

Require later executable fixtures for: subject different from holder; namesake
award collisions; qualification without practice authority; registration without
license; valid proof with untrusted issuer; stale or unavailable status; suspension
distinct from revocation; future effective date; unknown versus open expiry;
renewal versus scheme maintenance; recognition pending in destination jurisdiction;
required scope lost in export; restricted evidence leaking in filenames; expected
head mismatch on correction. Current validator checks research structure only.

Accept publication only with valid synthesis, no critical conflicts, explicit
provider/source/runtime holds and reviewable-draft assurance. Do not infer legal
permission, issue credentials, contact regulators or access private records.

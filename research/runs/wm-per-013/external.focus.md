# WM-PER-013 Professional License / Credential

Research an awarded professional credential or license record independently.
Distinguish credential definition/scheme, individual award, holder, issuer,
regulator, cryptographic envelope and actual authority to practise. A certificate
or valid signature is not necessarily a professional license or current legal
permission. No clinical/legal advice, real-person lookup or private data needed.

Cover identity, holder binding, awarding authority and scheme, eligibility and
assessment evidence, scope/jurisdiction/conditions, issue/effective/expiry dates,
renewal and continuing requirements, suspension/revocation/reinstatement,
primary-source verification and status freshness, recognition/portability,
appeal/correction and privacy-aware presentation. Registration, qualification,
membership and license must not collapse into one active boolean.

Use primary standards and regulator examples; keep jurisdictional rules as
profiles. Consider W3C Verifiable Credentials, status lists, HL7 Practitioner
qualifications, credential vocabularies and professional registers. Design
bounded subject-specific bundles/layers/findings/questions, data elements,
artifacts, safe record functions and service layers as complete schema-valid
JSON. Never issue/revoke a real credential or interact with other agents.

# WM-ACT-036 Codex pre-provider boundary

Status: preparatory boundary analysis. This is not a provider result.

- A Research Study is a governed inquiry aggregate, not the organizational
  Research Project, one contained Experiment or Trial, a Dataset, a Publication,
  a Participant, a Specimen, an Observation or an Analysis Procedure.
- The root owns study identity and lineage, research purpose, question and
  hypothesis assertions, protocol and design releases, study-scoped role and
  ethics bindings, sampling and cohort assertions, collection and analysis
  plans, result and finding assertions, registration history, amendments,
  output links, limitations and provenance. Referenced masters retain identity.
- Protocol intent, actual conduct, source observations, analysis executions,
  estimates, statistical evidence, interpretations and findings are distinct.
- Proposed, designed, preregistered, registered, approved, recruiting,
  collecting, active, paused, closed to participation, analysing, completed,
  terminated, withdrawn, published, corrected, retracted and archived events
  are profile-specific, attributable and append-only.
- Study terminology differs across natural science, engineering, health,
  social science, humanities and arts. The shared kernel must remain usable
  without treating a clinical-trial registry as a universal research schema.

Adjudication must reject a Research Project clone, an Experiment or Trial
clone, copied participant or specimen masters, unversioned protocols, consent
inferred from participation, causality inferred from association, significance
treated as truth, findings detached from methods and evidence, silent protocol
rewrites, and autonomous protected-data disclosure or destructive disposition
without delegated authority.

# WM-ACT-036 bounded external research focus

Produce one concise, complete `model-research.schema.json` result for
`WM-ACT-036 Research Study`.

## Frozen boundary

- Treat frozen `standalone-mm` as record-plane metadata and choose the subject
  kind independently. Test activity, aggregate and program interpretations.
- Define Research Study as one governed, versioned inquiry aggregate that
  pursues declared research questions or hypotheses through a protocol and
  design, a defined population, material or phenomenon, data collection,
  observations, analysis and bounded findings.
- Cover study identity and lineage, classification and purpose, questions and
  hypotheses, protocol and design releases, sponsor and roles, funding and
  sites, ethics and oversight, consent and privacy, population and eligibility,
  sampling, cohorts and arms, interventions or exposures, instruments and
  variables, collection events, observations and measurements, data management,
  quality, analysis plans and executions, estimates and uncertainty, findings,
  limitations, preregistration and registration, amendments, outputs,
  reproducibility, lifecycle, provenance, access, retention and safe agents.
- Preserve separate masters for Research Project, Grant or Award, Experiment or
  Trial, Test Execution, Person, Research Subject or Participant, Population,
  Sample or Specimen, Site, Instrument, Observation, Measurement, Dataset,
  Software, Scholarly Publication, Ethics Decision, Artifact and Audit Record.
- A study owns study-scoped assertions and immutable bindings to those external
  masters. It does not silently copy or control their independent lifecycles.
- Do not infer hypothesis support from significance alone, causality from an
  observational association, generalizability from one sample, consent from
  enrolment, data quality from completeness, or reproducibility from one study.

## Size and evidence limits

- Target 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 findings, 72 discriminating questions, 24
  artifacts and 10 functions.
- Use at least 10 primary official sources from at least 6 independent
  organizations. Prefer OECD Frascati, DDI Lifecycle, WHO trial registration,
  ClinicalTrials.gov protocol elements, OGC Observations Measurements and
  Samples, W3C PROV-O and DCAT, DataCite, NIH data management and privacy,
  human-subject protection rules, NIST privacy guidance and RFC 3339.
- Every structure and function element needs source references.
- State cross-domain terminology, disciplinary methods, ethics, statistics,
  clinical-trial specificity, regional law, sensitive-data, controlled-access,
  archival, interoperability and independent-review limitations as holds.

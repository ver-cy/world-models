# WM-ACT-036 Codex source notes

The Codex fallback used official primary material from OECD, the DDI Alliance,
WHO, ClinicalTrials.gov, OGC, W3C, DataCite, NIH, the United States eCFR, NIST,
the UK National Archives and the IETF.

OECD Frascati supplies a cross-disciplinary statistical boundary for research
and experimental development. DDI Lifecycle supplies the broadest structured
study and data-collection decomposition used here. WHO TRDS and
ClinicalTrials.gov sharpen registration, design, sponsor, recruitment, outcome
and data-sharing fields, but remain clinical-trial profiles rather than
universal research requirements.

OGC OMS separates observation, observed feature, procedure, sampling and
result. PROV-O supplies attributable plans, activities, agents, derivation,
revision and invalidation. DCAT and DataCite support discoverable, separately
versioned datasets and outputs. The property-level DataCite mapping remains
pinned to 4.6 while the official release history identifies 4.7 as current, so
the delta and round-trip behavior remain a publication hold.

NIH data-management and participant-privacy guidance, 45 CFR Part 46 and the
NIST Privacy Framework inform prospective management, controlled sharing,
human-subject and privacy safeguards. They do not establish universal legal
compliance outside their stated jurisdiction and use. UK National Archives
guidance informs policy-bound retention and disposition. RFC 3339 supplies
timestamps with seconds and explicit offsets.

Product type and configuration, lot or batch, stock position, handling unit,
as-built assembly, asset register, passport, shell and digital twin remain
classifiers, relationships, views or projections over the same physical thing;
none may mint a competing master identity.

accepted Grok's non-serial identification evidence, digital-representation
identifiers, exceptional non-terminal states, lot-as-provenance relation and
event-dimension or observation-grain checks. Natural and initially unmarked
objects are admitted as a scope clarification; legal title remains owned by the
ownership and party models while this model governs custody references.

than Vercy's frozen contract. The immutable raw wrapper remains outside Git. A
no-tools pass converted source slugs, composition links, questions, artifacts
and functions into the canonical schema without adding research or losing the
reported node counts. Both final provider results pass schema and semantic
validation.

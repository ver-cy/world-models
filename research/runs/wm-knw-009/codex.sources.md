# WM-KNW-009 Codex source notes

The synthesis treats a Hypothesis as a versioned provisional proposition made
evaluable through scope, operationalization, predictions and criteria. NIST,
ISO 3534-1 and JCGM provide statistical, measurement and uncertainty context;
none supplies a universal truth rule. ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO TRDS and OSF are
prospective registration profiles whose timing and deviations must remain
visible rather than becoming generic hypothesis semantics.

OBI, STATO and ECO connect investigations, statistical methods, evidence and
conclusions, with biomedical-origin caveats. PROV-O, Web Annotation, DQV, ODRL
and SHACL support lineage, scoped evidence annotations, quality, access and
validation. DataCite, RO-Crate and FAIR support identity, packaging, discovery
and reuse; Schema.org Claim supports broad claim discovery.

The model therefore preserves a hypothesis separately from questions,
assumptions, theories, predictions, studies, results, evidence and decisions.
P-values, intervals, Bayes factors, model scores, citations, peer review and
authority produce qualified assessments, not context-free truth flags.

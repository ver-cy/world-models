# Codex pre-provider hypothesis for WM-KNW-009

## Boundary

Hypothesis should be an entity for a versioned provisional proposition that is
made evaluable by scope, operationalization, predictions and criteria. It owns
hypothesis identity, formulation, type, evaluation contract and assessment
lineage. Generic claims, assumptions, theories, research questions, protocols,
studies, tests, data, observations, results, evidence, publications and
decisions remain external masters.

## Proposed structure

1. Identity, formulation and hypothesis kind.
2. Scope, variables, conditions and logical structure.
3. Rationale, assumptions, provenance and prior evidence.
4. Operationalization, predictions, criteria and study bindings.
5. Evaluation, evidence, uncertainty, lifecycle and revision.
6. Governance, preregistration, access and interoperability.

Each bundle should contain two layers and four findings. Each finding should ask
three model-specific questions and define one data element and one serial
evidence artifact. Ten functions should register, formulate, classify, scope,
operationalize, preregister, bind evaluation, record assessment, revise or
supersede and project an authorized view.

## Expected hard points

- Hypothesis, research question, assumption, prediction, theory and generic claim
  are related but not interchangeable.
- A statistical null/alternative pair is one profile, not the universal form of
  every hypothesis.
- Evidence supports or challenges through a method and scope; it does not set a
  context-free truth flag.
- Rejection, non-rejection, corroboration, refutation and practical significance
  have different meanings.
- Preregistration time and deviations must remain distinct from later results.

# WM-ORG-019 Organization Policy

Research one complete model for an organization's approved normative rule,
its scope and lifecycle. Distinguish policy identity and version from text
artifacts, procedures, laws, control implementations and enforcement results.
Drafts may be tracked but are not approved policy. An agent must not interpret
this descriptive record as authorization to execute its requirements.

Cover authority and approval evidence, purpose/scope, normative statements,
applicability/conditions, duties/prohibitions/permissions, precedence and
conflicts, exceptions with authorizer and expiry, publication/acknowledgement,
effective time, supersession/review/withdrawal, implementation mappings and
assurance evidence. Unknown applicability is not permit. Receipt is not consent
or compliance. Separate organization policy from law and access policy.

Use primary official sources, identifying each source's sector and limits.
Do not claim generic prose compiles losslessly to executable policy. Consider
W3C ODRL, NIST policy/procedure controls, OASIS XACML and provenance/versioning
only where relevant, distinguishing semantic alignment from conformance.
Design subject-specific bundles, layers, findings and discriminating questions
plus data elements, artifacts, functions and Vercy service layers. Aim for a
bounded complete schema-valid result, not a placeholder or generic template.
Do not read local files or contact other agents. Return your independent JSON.

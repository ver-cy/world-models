# WM-ORG-019 acceptance design

These are planned semantic fixtures, not executed tests or conformance proof.

1. A draft bearing a policy title but no approval evidence is not approved.
2. A translation with a different digest must retain its language and authority
   relationship; it cannot silently replace the authoritative version.
3. Approval, issue, effective date and review due date differ; a missed review
   does not automatically imply legal expiry.
4. Unknown applicability cannot be converted to permission. Not-applicable,
   indeterminate and denied evaluation outcomes remain distinct where a named
   machine policy profile uses them.
5. An expired exception cannot authorize a new case; a request is not approval.
6. Conflicting clauses with no approved precedence are flagged, not resolved by
   newest-file-wins or universal deny-wins assumptions.
7. Acknowledgement records receipt, not automatically understanding, consent,
   compliance or enforceability.
8. A procedure or control mapping is not successful implementation evidence.
9. Historical versions and withdrawals retain provenance and permitted evidence;
   personal acknowledgement details are excluded from public projections.
10. A model record never executes access changes, discipline, sanctions or legal
    determinations without a separately authorized validated adapter.
11. ODRL/XACML projections identify profile/version and unmapped meaning; they
    are not full conformance or lossless prose compilation claims.
12. Root policy is nonphysical. Direct properties include modality, scope and
    temporal standing; physical measurements belong to referenced subjects.

Before publication: strict schema validation, no-tools frozen-result self-audit,
source and external-review holds, exact package/API digest and website checks.
No executable nested-field fixture suite has yet been authored for this model.

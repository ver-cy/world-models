# WM-POL-016 Public Authority / Institution

Independently research a public institution with evidenced legal competence,
not a sovereign state, officeholder, building, single service or government-wide
aggregate. Distinguish organization identity and legal personality, legal basis,
competence and jurisdiction, functions and services, governing organs and posts,
delegation and accountability, institutional succession and records custody.

Use primary standards and authority sources such as W3C ORG, Core Public
Organisation Vocabulary and official government institutional classifications.
Do not generalize one country's public-body definition to all jurisdictions.
Public ownership, funding, statistical government-sector classification and
authority to exercise a particular power are separate facts.

Include direct institutional properties, evidence for recognition, bounded
record functions, contextual relationships and lifecycle. Questions must elicit
concrete attributable answers with effective time and source rather than assign
legal powers from names or taxonomy. Service delivery does not prove competence;
website links do not prove delegated authority. Changes to the local record never
change legal mandates. No real-person lookups, private data or external actions.

Return schema-valid subject-specific bundles/layers/findings/questions,
data elements, artifacts, composition and all service layers. Scope any legal
examples precisely and keep uncertain authority, conflicts and missing mappings
visible. Do not contact other agents or modify any repository.

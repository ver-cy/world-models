# WM-POL-016 selected-source preparation

Inspected 2026-09-09. These are bounded primary-source readings, not full legal
or standards verification. The hierarchy and proposed fields will be authored
Vercy design; no assertion about a real institution's current powers is made.

- SRC-001 [Core Public Organisation Vocabulary 2.1.1](https://semiceu.github.io/CPOV/releases/2.1.1/), European Union SEMIC, Recommendation 2024-05-06. Selected organization boundary, identifiers, names, territory, reference framework and change-event passages. Services and personnel are linked resources, not detailed contents of this vocabulary. CC-BY 4.0 stated with exceptions; exact reused material still needs license check.
- SRC-002 [W3C Organization Ontology](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-org/). Selected Post/Membership distinction, sites, reporting and ChangeEvent. A vacant post can exist independently of its holder. Site is not institutional identity. No universal legal-authority semantics inferred from reportsTo. Exact dated pin/update check pending.
- SRC-003 [CPSV-AP 3.2.0](https://semiceu.github.io/CPSV-AP/releases/3.2.0/), European Union SEMIC, Recommendation 2024-05-06. Selected competent-authority relation: outsourcing does not change the public organization ultimately responsible for the service. This is a service responsibility mapping, not proof of every coercive/legal power. CC-BY 4.0 statement with exceptions noted.
- SRC-004 [Public bodies](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/public-bodies-reform), UK Cabinet Office. Selected economic versus administrative classification distinction. A statistical central-government classification may be followed by separate administrative classification. UK profile only.
- SRC-005 [Statistical classification to the public sector](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/governmentpublicsectorandtaxes/publicsectorfinance/methodologies/statisticalclassificationtothepublicsector), Office for National Statistics. Selected control-indicator and independence distinctions: statistical public-sector control is not a determination that an institution lacks independence. No mechanical funding/shareholding test adopted.
- SRC-006 [Publication schemes: a guide](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/foi/publication-schemes-a-guide/), Information Commissioner's Office. Selected structured publication guide, accessibility and disclosure exception passages. This is jurisdiction-specific guidance; it does not authorize public release of all institutional records.

# Proposed acceptance fixtures

Not implemented tests. Later fixtures must distinguish name change from legal
succession; vacant post from nonexistent institution; provider from competent
authority; sponsorship from legal control; statistical control from loss of
independence; address from jurisdiction; classification from coercive powers;
delegation from reporting; published source from disclosure authorization;
conflicting mandates from resolved facts; local record retirement from abolition.

Nested schemas, source authority policy, jurisdiction-specific interpretation,
loss-aware export refusal and expected-head concurrency need executable proofs.
Research-schema validation alone cannot establish legal or operational readiness.

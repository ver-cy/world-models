# WM-AI-007 verified source notes

Accessed 2026-09-06. Official public-authority, standards-body and first-party
sources were checked at their canonical pages. Provider wrappers are not sources.

- NIST AI RMF 1.0, the Generative AI Profile and SP 800-218A establish lifecycle
  risk, accountable development, documentation, provenance, evaluation, incident
  and release evidence. AI RMF 1.0 is pinned because its revision is in progress.
- The EU AI Act and GDPR are bounded European Union legal profiles for technical
  documentation, transparency, records, risk, privacy and accountability.
- PROV-O and OpenLineage provide activity, entity, agent, run, dataset and event
  lineage concepts; they do not make the registry entry a training-run master.
- DCAT 3 explicitly distinguishes a catalog record from the cataloged resource
  and provides a bounded profile for discovery, versioning and distributions.
- Current MLflow documentation distinguishes registered models, model versions,
  aliases, tags and environments; fixed model stages are deprecated since 2.9.0.
- Hugging Face model cards and the original Google Research paper support
  intended-use, limitation, dataset, evaluation, license and discovery metadata.
- SPDX 3.0.1 AI and CycloneDX 1.7 provide version-pinned AI package and ML-BOM
  views. OCI Distribution 1.1.1, SLSA 1.1 and Sigstore provide complementary
  distribution, provenance and signature-verification profiles.
- A digest or verified signature is evidence, not proof of safety, quality,
  ownership, authorization or fitness. RFC 3339 supplies timestamp syntax.

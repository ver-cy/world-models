# WM-LIV-021 Codex pre-provider position

The defensible aggregate root is a condition occurrence concerning one organism
plus immutable assertions about it. The disease concept comes from a pinned
classification or ontology; the occurrence identity is local to the master
system; and a diagnosis is an attributable assertion with evidence and
verification state. These three identities must not collapse.

Direct properties of the occurrence include subject, applicable concept,
anatomical site, pathological morphology or process, onset and course only when
supported. Signs, symptoms, measurements and functional limitations remain
external observations. Etiology, severity, stage, remission and prognosis are
method- and time-qualified assertions rather than timeless labels.

The most dangerous errors are treating a code as proof, treating a rule-out as
present, mixing current clinical status with epistemic certainty, silently
merging duplicate diagnoses, interpreting an association as causal, confusing
relapse with a new occurrence, and disclosing protected condition context
without purpose and consent checks.

Human clinical profiles cannot govern all organism conditions. ICD, SNOMED CT,
Mondo, Disease Ontology, Orphanet, HPO, Phenopackets, FHIR and OMOP serve
different classification, terminology, exchange and analytics roles; mappings
must pin releases and disclose semantic loss.

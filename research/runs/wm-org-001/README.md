Both providers independently classify Organization as an `entity`: a
persistent, externally addressable subject bearing identity, classification,
standing and lifecycle. Legal registration, internal units, sites, positions,
party roles and person records remain sibling responsibilities.

The no-tools adjudication retained Claude's tighter boundary and accepted home
legal jurisdiction, organization formality spectrum, national legal form,
terminal dissolution, and record stewardship. Duplicate standing, activity,
contact, classification and function structures were rejected. Ownership and
control remain organization-side endpoints pending a dedicated relationship
sibling.

Grok's substantive answer had malformed JSON transport. The immutable raw
wrapper remains outside Git; a no-tools Claude pass repaired syntax/schema only,
and the normalized result passes both schema and semantic validation.

No critical structural conflicts remain. Live source pins, multi-profile
validation, EUID/BRIS verification, beneficial-ownership grounding, multilingual
legal-name correction, licensed ISO text and ISO 5009 verification remain
publication holds. See `synthesis-plan.json`, `adjudication.json`,
`comparison.json` and `synthesis.validation.json`.

# WM-PLC-002 Land Parcel external research focus

Research a format-independent cadastral or land-administration parcel as a
governed spatial unit with stable identity, jurisdiction, lifecycle, boundary
evidence and links to rights, restrictions and responsibilities. Distinguish
the parcel from land, terrain, address, building, site, planning zone,
ownership, title, legal interest and survey observation.

Cover authoritative identifiers, parcel type and status, administrative and
registry context, geometry and topology, boundary points and segments, area
and dimensionality, survey and source quality, subdivision, consolidation,
reparcellation and successor history, rights and party references, land use
and valuation references, provenance, access, retention and interoperability.

Use current primary official land-administration and geospatial standards,
including ISO 19152 LADM, OGC LandInfra/InfraGML and GeoSPARQL, INSPIRE
Cadastral Parcels and UN-GGIM land-administration guidance. Return
model-research.schema.json with direct spatial properties, recognition
criteria, lifecycle and agent-safe operations. State jurisdiction, coordinate,
topology, legal-effect, accuracy and source-authority limitations explicitly.

Both providers independently classify Work Assignment as a `relationship`: a
reified, time-bounded occupancy or authorization binding an agent, organization
or unit, role or post, bounded scope and validity period. Position, engagement
or contract terms, person and organization master data, task ownership,
permissions, rosters and timecards remain neighbouring responsibilities.

Grok's unnamed or pre-allocated occupancy, role-scoped contact and endpoint
projection, jurisdiction-labelled personnel-action typology, temporary or
time-limited forms and two non-duplicative functions. The non-human-agent branch
is retained only with an explicit evidence gap and required natural-person
oversight.

only. Both provider results pass schema and semantic validation without manual

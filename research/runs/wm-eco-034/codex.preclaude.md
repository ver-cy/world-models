# Codex pre-provider boundary: WM-ECO-034

Status: provisional preparation, not a provider result or publication input.

The aggregate is an organization- or reporting-group-level sustainability disclosure, not a generic filing, financial statement, product environmental claim, raw measurement dataset, risk register, action plan or assurance engagement. It owns the bounded disclosure and its append-only revisions while referencing those neighboring masters.

Agent-native coverage must include identity and class, direct abstract properties, recognition and evidence, permitted operations, and context and governance. Physical properties are not applicable to the disclosure itself. Any physical subject is referenced through an external model. Assertions remain source-qualified, versioned and falsifiable; absence of data is not zero, and publication is not proof of truth, compliance or assurance.

Candidate structure: identity, mandate and boundary; sustainability matters and materiality; assertions, metrics, targets and evidence; preparation, controls and assurance; publication, filing and lifecycle; governance, provenance, access and interoperability. Exact content remains subject to official-source research, validation and adversarial audit.

# WM-ECO-034 Codex source set

Frameworks remain versioned profiles with distinct users, materiality lenses and legal effects. Interoperability guidance is not equivalence. Reported assertion, observation, calculation, estimate, scenario, target, approval, assurance conclusion, filing, publication, compliance and regulator conclusion remain independently governed objects.

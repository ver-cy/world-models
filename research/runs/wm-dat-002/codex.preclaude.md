- Registry ID: `vr.wm-dat-002`

- Approved outgoing relation ledger: empty. Composition proposals from the
  previous card remain unapproved until relation reconciliation.

The model owns the governed statistical product: programme and authority,
statistical concepts and measures, populations and frames, classifications,
data structures, series and observations, methods, estimation, quality,
uncertainty, revisions, release and confidentiality controls. It does not own
respondent or unit identity, operational source records, generic dataset and
distribution lifecycle, geography or time registries, documents, policy or
publication channels.

Official status is not the same as numerical truth. Every value must retain
its producing authority, statistical concept, population, reference period,
unit, dimensions, method, source, vintage, status, uncertainty and revision
lineage. Alternative estimates may legitimately coexist.

## Existing conceptual structure to test

The previous N10 card proposed collection, series and dissemination. Research
should deepen and test:

1. Statistical authority, programme, purpose and product identity.
2. Concepts, measures, units, populations, frames and domains.
3. Census, survey, administrative and other source operations.
4. Sampling, weighting, imputation, estimation and adjustment.
5. Classifications, code lists, data structures and series keys.
6. Observations, status, confidentiality flags, uncertainty and vintage.
7. Quality dimensions, comparability, breaks, revisions and corrections.
8. Release calendar, embargo, pre-release access, dissemination and archive.
9. Provenance, access, retention, federation and SDMX, DDI or GSBPM mappings.

## Adversarial checks

- Do not confuse indicator definition, series, observation, release, dataset
  and chart.
- Keep reference time, observation period, collection time, processing time,
  release time and knowledge time distinct.
- Do not treat provisional, estimated, seasonally adjusted, revised, corrected
  or final values as interchangeable.
- Do not promise anonymity from removing direct identifiers. Microdata and
  small aggregates remain in controlled source systems.
- Preserve numerator, denominator, unit, scale, price base, seasonal status,
  classifications, source and method needed for interpretation.
- Never overwrite a released value without a new vintage and revision reason.
- Do not claim SDMX or DDI round-trip conformance without pinned versions,
  constraints and loss declarations.

# WM-ACT-054 verified source notes

Accessed 2026-09-06. Official primary and first-party sources were checked at
their canonical pages. Provider wrappers are not sources.

- ICH E6(R3), ICH E8(R1), the 2024 WMA Declaration of Helsinki, 45 CFR 46,
  the Belmont Report and EU Regulation 536/2014 establish the human-research
  protection, permission, review, responsibility, safety and records frame.
- WHO TRDS 1.3.1 and ClinicalTrials.gov describe public study-level registry
  data. They do not provide an individual participant master.
- HL7 FHIR R5 ResearchSubject explicitly separates the subject, study,
  study-specific identifiers, progress, period, assigned and actual groups and
  consent references. Its Trial Use maturity is retained as a visible hold.
- FHIR R5 Consent, CDISC ODM 2.0 and CDASHIG 2.2 provide bounded permission,
  clinical-data interchange and acquisition profiles. Detailed CDISC material
  requiring sign-in was not treated as inspected normative content.
- ISO 14155:2026 supersedes the 2020 edition. Only the public official catalogue
  metadata was used because the normative text is access-restricted.
- HHS HIPAA guidance, GDPR, W3C PROV-O and RFC 3339 support bounded
  de-identification, privacy, successor provenance and timestamp rules.

The no-tools adjudication classified AI System as an `aggregate`. The governed
object is the composite of model artifacts, software, data interfaces,
configuration, operator roles and oversight arrangements. ML model internals,
AI agent actor semantics, individual inference or agent runs, incident case
files and the organisation-level AI management system remain sibling models.

Claude was retained as the structural base. Accepted Grok additions record the
adopted AI-system definition and definitional divergence, an OECD
socio-technical classification profile, and the boundary between an AI system
and a general-purpose AI model. Sixteen decisions were recorded and no critical
conflict remains. AI literacy, sandbox and real-world-testing states,
provider-cessation escrow, agent-surface links, environmental footprint and
additional jurisdictions or sectors remain deferred research.

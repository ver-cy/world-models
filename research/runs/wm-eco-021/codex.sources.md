# WM-ECO-021 Codex source notes

The fallback uses official primary materials from UNCITRAL, UNIDROIT, the European Union, OASIS Open, GS1, UNECE, ISO, W3C, the IETF, JSON Schema and the OpenAPI Initiative. Schema.org is treated as its publisher-maintained discovery vocabulary, not as legislation or proof of legal effect.

The CISG and UNIDROIT Principles establish the need to distinguish offer intent, effectiveness, withdrawal, revocation, rejection, modified acceptance, receipt and formation. They are reference profiles, not universal governing law. EU consumer rules contribute jurisdiction-specific pre-contract disclosure fields. UBL provides the principal quotation document structure and separately models the RFQ. Schema.org and GS1 contribute public discovery projections; ODRL contributes policy offer semantics but explicitly does not make an Offer policy a grant.

Every external projection is version-pinned and loss-declaring. National contract, tax, signature, privacy and records law remains deferred to adopting-Dimension profiles and authorized review.

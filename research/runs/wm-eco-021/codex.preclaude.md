# Codex pre-provider boundary: WM-ECO-021 Offer / Quote

Status: frozen provisional boundary prepared before external results. It is not a provider result or publication input.

Offer / Quote is treated as a versioned pre-contract commercial proposal aggregate with explicit legal-offer, quotation, estimate and informational profiles. It owns proposal-specific terms and provenance. Party, product, service, catalogue, price list, RFQ, negotiation, tender, contract, order, invoice, payment, fulfilment, response events and legal formation determinations remain external.

The research must preserve line, lot and whole-offer structure; alternative and dependency logic; fixed, estimated, ranged, tiered, recurring and usage-based prices; currencies, taxes, allowances and charges; delivery, payment, validity, assumptions and exclusions; attributable lifecycle events; multiple temporal axes; evidence; access; retention; and loss-aware projections.

Neither recipient action nor a local status may be treated as proof of contract formation or enforceability. Autonomous pricing approval, tax determination, acceptance, signature, dispatch, publication, rights expansion and disposition are prohibited without delegated authority.

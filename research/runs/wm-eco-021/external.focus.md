# External research focus: WM-ECO-021 Offer / Quote

Research one governed, versioned pre-contract commercial proposal aggregate. It may use explicit profiles for a legally operative offer, a price quotation, a non-binding estimate or an informational proposal. Do not silently assert that all profiles are offers in the legal sense.

## Frozen root boundary

The root owns proposal identity and version, issuer and addressed recipient roles, offered items or services by reference, quantities, prices, currencies, taxes, allowances and charges, delivery or performance terms, payment terms, validity conditions, dependencies, exclusions, assumptions, status, provenance and governed response routes.

Keep these identities external: party, product or service master, catalogue and price list, request for quotation, negotiation or message thread, tender or bid, contract, order, invoice, payment, shipment or performance, acceptance event and legal determination that a contract formed. References to them do not import their lifecycle or authority.

Acceptance, rejection, counter-offer, withdrawal, revocation, lapse and expiry must be separately attributable events. A technical status or recipient action does not by itself prove legal effectiveness, contract formation or enforceability. Preserve jurisdiction, governing rules, communication or receipt semantics and evidence.

Separate issue, effective-from, valid-until, delivery-window, response, withdrawal, revocation, rejection, acceptance, expiry, observation, ingestion and knowledge times. RFC 3339 timestamps require seconds and an explicit offset or Z.

## Required research outcomes

- Resolve offer, quotation, estimate, proposal, catalogue price, tender, counter-offer, order and contract boundaries.
- Model fixed, estimated, ranged, tiered, recurring and usage-based prices without collapsing amount, tax, discount, allowance, charge, exchange rate and total.
- Cover line, lot and whole-offer dependencies; alternatives; minimums; bundles; substitutions; optional items; partial acceptance; and all-or-none constraints.
- Cover parties, authority, channels, delivery and payment terms, jurisdiction, language, confidentiality, rights, privacy, retention, provenance, validation and amendments.
- Cover human, PDF, HTML, API, JSON, UBL, schema.org, GS1 and other loss-aware projections as representations, never the canonical model.
- Provide 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 model-specific findings, at least 72 discriminating questions, artifacts and 10 safe functions.

Use primary official sources where possible. Pin editions and effective dates. Do not quote restricted standards or fabricate conformance. Keep contract formation, pricing approval, tax determination, acceptance, signature, dispatch, publication and deletion outside autonomous agent authority unless explicitly delegated.

# Codex pre-provider hypothesis for WM-KNW-016

## Boundary

Assumption should be an entity for one stable, attributable premise used under
declared uncertainty in a bounded reasoning, decision, planning or execution
context. It owns the premise role, acceptance state, scope, review and revision
lineage. Proposition, fact, hypothesis, evidence, requirement, risk, decision,
plan, test and source document remain external masters.

## Proposed structure

1. Identity, expression, kind and epistemic status.
2. Purpose, scope, actors, authority and applicable context.
3. Rationale, evidence, uncertainty and competing assessments.
4. Dependencies, consequences, risks and affected reasoning products.
5. Validation, challenge, lifecycle, review and revision lineage.
6. Governance, access, retention and interoperability.

Each bundle should contain two layers and four findings. Each finding should ask
three model-specific questions and define one data element and one serial
evidence artifact. Ten functions should register, classify, contextualize,
accept, link dependencies, challenge, validate, assess consequences, revise or
supersede and project an authorized view.

## Expected hard points

- An assumption is accepted for use but is not thereby a verified fact.
- The proposition content and its assumption role may require separate identity.
- Confidence, probability, uncertainty, materiality and risk are distinct.
- Validation outcome is contextual and time-qualified, not universal proof.
- Consequences if false and dependent decisions must remain queryable.

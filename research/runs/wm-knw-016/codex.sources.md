# WM-KNW-016 Codex source notes

The synthesis treats an Assumption as one versioned and attributable premise
accepted for a declared use while verification remains incomplete or contextual.
OMG SACM supplies the clearest semantic boundary: an assumed claim is declared
without supporting evidence or argumentation. SysML, ISO life-cycle and
requirements standards, NASA guidance, the UK AQuA Book and the GAO cost guide
place assumptions inside governed engineering and analytical work.

The draft does not turn acceptance into truth. It separates the proposition,
premise role, acceptance assertion, confidence, probability, uncertainty,
evidence assessment and validation outcome. ISO 31000, IEC 31010, NIST risk
guidance and the AI RMF ground consequences, sensitivity, limitations and
ongoing review. The AQuA Book specifically supports recording effect,
reliability, time, rationale, author and sign-off.

PROV-O, DQV, Web Annotation, ODRL and SHACL support attributable change,
quality, annotation, access and validation projections. DataCite, RO-Crate and
Schema.org provide portable discovery and packaging views. DMN covers dependent
decision context without owning the assumption. RFC 3339 provides timestamps
with seconds and an explicit offset, while RFC 8785 supports deterministic JSON
serialization. Every mapping remains version-pinned and loss-declaring.

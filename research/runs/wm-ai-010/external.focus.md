## Boundary questions

- Separate the underlying incident from its initial, intermediate, final and
  corrected reports, submissions, acknowledgements and public summaries.
- Keep AI system, model, dataset, deployment, endpoint, person, organization,
  harm, security event, privacy breach, investigation, regulatory case,
  remediation action, evidence and records masters external.
- Represent jurisdiction-qualified reportability, classification, deadline,
  recipient and disclosure decisions without making autonomous legal findings.
- Preserve occurrence, detection, discovery, awareness, notification, report,
  update, correction, closure, recorded, ingested and knowledge times.
- Distinguish observed fact, reporter statement, hypothesis, causal assessment,
  authority finding, impact estimate and verified outcome.
- Require append-only amendments and attributable approvals; never overwrite a
  submitted report or silently change severity, affected scope or causal claims.

## Required breadth

Target 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 findings, 72 questions, 24 serial artifacts and
10 governed functions. Cover identity and jurisdiction; affected systems and
parties; occurrence and timeline; classification, severity, impact and harm;
causal, security and privacy analysis; containment, recovery and remediation;
notification duties and deadlines; submissions and authority interactions;
evidence, confidentiality, access, retention, correction and projections.

## Safety constraints

Do not authorize autonomous legal classification, regulatory submission,
public disclosure, admission of liability, attribution, access expansion,
evidence destruction or closure. Preserve uncertainty, privilege and restricted
data markings. Use RFC 3339 timestamps with seconds and explicit offset or Z.

# WM-AI-010 pre-provider boundary note

The root should be a governed reporting-case aggregate. It owns report identity,
version, status, submission and correction lineage, plus source-qualified facts,
classifications, impact summaries, causal assessments, response summaries and
authority exchanges needed to report an AI incident. It does not own the
incident, AI system, model, deployment, investigation, remediation work,
regulatory case, harm, vulnerability, evidence or records masters.

Candidate source families for later independent verification include the EU AI
Act serious-incident and reporting provisions, OECD AI incident monitoring and
reporting work, NIST AI RMF and GenAI Profile, NIST incident-response guidance,
GDPR breach-notification provisions, FIRST incident-response service guidance,
PROV-O and RFC 3339. No source is admitted until checked at its official page.

# WM-ORG-005 Employment independent pre-provider boundary

## Frozen root

An Employment instance is a governed relationship in which a worker party
performs or is meant to perform work for an employing party under an asserted
legal, contractual, statutory or factual basis. It owns relationship identity,
party-role bindings, jurisdiction-qualified classification assertions and
determinations, effective periods, status, term references, assignment links,
continuity and separation events, competing assertions and provenance.

Membership is excluded. A worker may have several simultaneous Employment
instances, including with related organizations, agencies, clients or public
bodies. Employer, host, client, agency, paymaster and platform may differ.

## External boundaries

- Person and organization own party identity.
- Organizational unit, position, occupation and skill models own their concepts.
- WM-ORG-016 owns assignment identity, scope, execution and lifecycle; this
  model owns the typed link showing which assignment realizes this relationship.
- Contract, collective agreement, policy and legislation own normative texts.
- Payroll, compensation, attendance, leave administration, benefits, tax,
  social insurance, qualification and recruitment own their transactions.
- Evidence, access grants, audit events and generated verification documents
  retain independent identity and lifecycle.

## Expected bundles

1. Relationship identity, parties and authority.
2. Classification, jurisdiction and recognition.
3. Commencement, terms and working context.
4. Assignments, occupation and organizational placement.
5. Lifecycle, continuity and separation.
6. Assertions, disputes, evidence and privacy.
7. Interoperability, governance and agent operations.

## Non-negotiable checks

- Labels, pay, control indicators and statistical categories are evidence, not
  a universal legal determination.
- Recorded, asserted, agreed, effective, observed and legally determined times
  remain distinct and support retroactive correction without erasure.
- Bilateral provenance does not grant either party unilateral control over the
  other party's lawful record, access, correction, portability or retention.
- Current relationship status is not inferred from an active assignment,
  payroll event, workplace access or historic contract alone.
- Agent operations preserve authority, purpose limitation, minimum disclosure,
  immutable history and explicit unknowns.

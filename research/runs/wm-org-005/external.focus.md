# WM-ORG-005 Employment external research focus

Research one governed employment relationship between a worker party and an
employing party. Keep membership entirely outside this model. Treat employment
status as jurisdiction-bound and evidence-based, not as a conclusion derived
from a contract label, job title, payroll entry or one universal classifier.

Cover relationship identity, worker and employer bindings, host or client and
agency arrangements, legal and factual basis, jurisdiction, classification
assertions and determinations, commencement, continuity, term bindings,
working-time and work-location context, occupation and assignment references,
suspension and leave, transfer and employer succession, separation, disputes,
retroactive correction, minimal verification, privacy, retention, provenance
and exchange.

Keep person, organization, organizational unit, position, occupation, contract,
work assignment, payroll, attendance, benefit, tax, social insurance,
qualification, recruitment and work-product lifecycles in their owning models.
The approved candidate COMPOSE relation to WM-ORG-016 means assignments realize
employment, but this draft must not import assignment or task execution.

Use primary official sources. Distinguish statistical ICSE status categories
from legally dispositive national determinations. Address employee,
worker-like, apprentice, public-service, agency, dispatched, platform-mediated
and genuinely self-employed boundaries without inventing global legal effect.

Return a complete model-research.schema.json result with direct relational
properties, recognition criteria, governed transitions and agent-safe
operations. No physical-property fields are applicable to the relationship
itself. State regional assumptions, power-asymmetry risks, disputed assertions,
uncertainty and deferred jurisdiction profiles explicitly.

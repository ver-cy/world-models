partnership, affiliation, supply or control/consolidation, with explicit endpoint
roles, direction, validity and evidence. Do not equate accounting consolidation,
equity ownership, beneficial ownership, contract existence or public affiliation.

bilateral assertions from mutual recognition and inferred graph edges.

at least 6 primary sources from 3 organizations, all service layers, concrete
artifact data elements and 6 subject-owned operations. Keep unresolved coverage
explicit. Include kind-specific direction/cardinality/transitivity constraints,
multi-party arrangements without lossy pairwise flattening, controlling basis,
effective versus recorded time, dispute, evidence and disclosure limits.

supplier/partner qualification lifecycles belong to WM-ORG-014/015. Contract and
ownership records remain separate. No universal legal conclusions or executable
commercial actions. References to WM-ORG-001 are proposed composition, not an
already-approved ledger edge. Examine W3C ORG, GLEIF RR-CDF, Open Ownership BODS
and procurement parties/roles for loss-aware mappings; verify exact sources.
All five object facets apply as organizational/relational properties, not mass
or geometry. Return a complete canonical-schema object only, without padding.

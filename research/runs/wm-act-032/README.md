The repository owner explicitly waived Grok for this run. No Grok output was
fabricated or inferred. A separate no-tools Claude adversarial audit challenged
the validated result against its frozen registry record and relationship
contract. The audit could reject or defer claims but could not add facts,
findings or functions.

The audit accepted Change Request as an `entity`-rooted aggregate: a persistent,
addressable request record remains meaningful when rejected, deferred, withdrawn
or never implemented. Decision/Approval owns the disposition decision, while
execution, release and realised-outcome records remain external references. No
critical conflict was found.

Open holds include live source and version verification, two artifact
cardinality corrections, an incomplete relation ledger, reconciliation of a
cached disposition with its decision record, coverage-checklist overclaim and
the explicit single-provider waiver. The result therefore remains
`reviewable-draft`, `publishable` is false, and every public artifact exposes the
waiver and holds. See `provider-policy.json`, `comparison.json`,
`synthesis-plan.json`, `adjudication.json`, `source-verification.csv` and
`synthesis.validation.json`.

# WM-ACT-052 verified source notes

Accessed 2026-09-06. These are official primary or first-party sources used by
the Codex fallback. Provider wrappers are not sources.

- OECD Tax Administration 2025 and Tax Administration 3.0 provide comparative
  administration and digital-transformation context. The ICAP programme is a
  bounded cooperative-assurance profile, not a universal filing process.
- OECD International VAT/GST Guidelines supply cross-border VAT principles;
  domestic legal effect and procedure remain jurisdiction-specific.
- IRS MeF schemas and business rules, Publication 4164 for Processing Year
  2026, and Publication 1345 Rev. 12-2025 separate versioned return content,
  validation, transmission, electronic postmark, acknowledgement, acceptance,
  rejection, signature, record-keeping, payment and refund concepts for United
  States profiles.
- HMRC VAT (MTD) API 1.0 beta and VAT Notice 700/22 provide a United Kingdom
  profile for obligations, digital records, return submission, declaration,
  receipt, liabilities, payments and penalties.
- The EU VAT Directive supplies a legal VAT frame whose transposition and
  current national procedure still need jurisdiction-specific resolution.
- XBRL 2.1, UBL 2.4 and XML Signature 1.1 provide versioned reporting,
  commercial-document and signature projections. None is a universal tax-case
  schema, and each mapping needs conformance and information-loss evidence.
- PROV-O, GDPR and RFC 3339 support revision lineage, protected personal-data
  processing and explicit event and knowledge times.
- IRS appeal material is a United States procedural profile. It supports the
  distinction between assessment notices, disagreement, evidence, deadlines,
  administrative review and later proceedings without generalizing deadlines.

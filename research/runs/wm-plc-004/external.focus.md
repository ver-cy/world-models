# WM-PLC-004 Administrative Area external research focus

Research a format-independent administrative area as a governed territorial or
statistical unit whose identity, authority, hierarchy, names, codes, geometry,
effective dates and successor history are separately represented. Distinguish
the area from a place, address, settlement, electoral district, postal zone,
service area, cadastral parcel, jurisdiction, government organization and map
geometry.

Cover authoritative codes and registries, unit type and level, parent and
containment hierarchies, multilingual and historic names, legal instruments,
competent authority, boundary geometry, coordinate reference system, enclaves,
exclaves, maritime or disputed status, population and other statistics as
external observations, creation, merger, split, annexation, renaming,
reclassification and abolition, provenance, access and interoperability.

Use current primary official standards and registries such as ISO 3166, UN
M49, INSPIRE Administrative Units, OGC geospatial standards and GeoNames only
where its authority is explicitly qualified. Return model-research.schema.json
with direct spatial properties, recognition criteria, lifecycle and agent-safe
operations. State jurisdiction, dispute, recognition, coding, topology,
accuracy and legal-effect limitations explicitly.

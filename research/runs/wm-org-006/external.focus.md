# WM-ORG-006 Membership external research focus

Research a qualified Membership relationship between a member agent and an
organization or governed group. Employment is entirely outside this model.
Membership may carry one or more roles, scopes and validity intervals, but it
does not own the identities or lifecycles of the member, organization, role,
post, activity, payment, credential, policy or access entitlement.

Cover relationship identity and parties, membership type and governing profile,
application or nomination, eligibility evidence, sponsorship and approval,
role bindings, admission, active standing, suspension, renewal, reinstatement,
resignation, termination and revocation, terms, duties, fee and benefit
references, competing assertions, correction, privacy, retention, portable
attestations and loss-aware interoperability.

Distinguish membership from employment, office-holding, participation,
affiliation, subscription, citizenship, licensing, credential possession and
access authorization. A credential or directory entry is evidence or a
projection, not the relationship itself. Treat visibility of relationship
existence, role, dates, standing and evidence as separately permissioned.

Use current primary official standards. Return model-research.schema.json with
direct relational properties, recognition criteria, governed transitions and
agent-safe operations. Physical properties are not applicable to the
relationship. State jurisdiction and domain assumptions, conflicting sources,
power asymmetry, due-process risks and deferred profiles explicitly.

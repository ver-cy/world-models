# WM-ORG-006 Membership independent pre-provider boundary

## Frozen root

A Membership instance is a governed relationship connecting a member agent to
an organization or group under a named membership scheme or constitutive rule.
It owns stable relationship identity, party bindings, type and profile,
admission basis, role bindings, validity and standing, terms, lifecycle events,
competing assertions and provenance.

Employment is excluded. The same member and organization may have several
memberships under different schemes or terms, and a membership may have several
concurrent or non-contiguous role bindings without changing its identity unless
the governing profile says otherwise.

## External boundaries

- Member and organization or group own their identities and lifecycles.
- Roles, posts, units, activities and projects remain external references.
- Applications, nominations, decisions, payments, subscriptions, credentials,
  access grants and policy evaluations keep their own identities and histories.
- Citizenship, licensing, office-holding, affiliation, participation and
  employment are non-equivalent neighboring relationships.

## Expected bundles

1. Relationship identity, parties and scheme.
2. Admission, eligibility and authority.
3. Roles, scope and participation context.
4. Standing, validity and lifecycle.
5. Terms, duties, fees and benefits.
6. Evidence, privacy and dispute handling.
7. Interoperability and agent operations.

## Non-negotiable checks

- A memberOf edge, directory listing, payment, credential or access grant alone
  does not prove a current qualified Membership.
- Event, valid, decision, knowledge and observation times remain distinct.
- Organization control does not erase member-side evidence, lawful correction,
  appeal, portability or retention rights.
- Private membership must not leak through identifiers, counts, timing or
  credential-status endpoints.
- Agent operations preserve authority, minimum disclosure, immutable history,
  non-equivalence and explicit unknowns.

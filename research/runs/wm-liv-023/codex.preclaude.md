# WM-LIV-023 Codex pre-provider position

The defensible root is one physical material entity with stable identity and an
append-only derivation lineage. A collection event creates a specimen from a
source. Splitting, aliquoting, pooling, extraction, culture or material
transformation creates one or more new specimen identities linked to every
input and output. Labels, accessions, containers and storage positions are
qualified identifiers or placements, never the material itself.

Direct properties include material kind, physical state, quantity and measured
composition only when source-qualified. Taxon, donor, patient, body site,
environment, disease, encounter, protocol, analysis, observation and result are
external masters. Quality, integrity, contamination and fitness for use are
method-, time- and purpose-qualified assessments rather than permanent truth.

The model must preserve actual versus planned collection, preanalytical and
transport history, custody and location transitions, quantity depletion,
destructive use, retention and disposition. Current location does not prove
custody or ownership. Human, genetic, sensitive location, endangered-species
and access-and-benefit-sharing context requires deny-by-default disclosure.

The slash-name represents interoperable clinical and broader biological
profiles, not two objects in one record. Clinical standards, biobank practices,
biodiversity vocabularies and sequence-sample metadata serve different scopes
and must not be silently collapsed.

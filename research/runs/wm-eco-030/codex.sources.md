# WM-ECO-030 Codex source basis

The Codex fallback used official sources from Grants.gov, the United States eCFR, the European Union, IATI, 360Giving, the Charity Commission for England and Wales, the United States Internal Revenue Service, the IFRS Foundation, OECD, ISO, IETF, W3C and the OpenAPI Initiative.

The evidence supports one non-exchange contribution specification family with mandatory grant-award and donation-or-gift profiles. It does not make a grant regime, charity rule, tax treatment, accounting policy, aid classifier, reporting format or result method universal. Grants.gov pages, eCFR, OECD and ISO had automated-access limitations or redirects but were verified through official indexed content; all limitations remain visible.

Claude Sonnet and Grok 4.6 each timed out on the single bounded attempt. No external provider output or unverified provider fact was admitted.

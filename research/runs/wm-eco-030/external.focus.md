# WM-ECO-030 provider focus

Research a governed Grant / Donation specification, not a public-benefit program, procurement, loan, investment, sponsorship, sale, contract, bank transfer, invoice, accounting ledger, tax return or generic project master.

Resolve the slash boundary explicitly. Test a common non-exchange contribution family with mandatory discriminators for grant award and donation or gift, while preserving distinct agreement, pledge, restriction, disbursement, expenditure, result and reporting identities. A grant or donation record must never prove that money moved, costs were eligible, a result occurred, a tax benefit applies or a report was accepted.

Cover at minimum:

- stable identity, subtype, version, status, program or appeal reference, jurisdiction and master system;
- funder, grantor, donor, sponsor, recipient, beneficiary, intermediary, fiscal sponsor, implementing partner, subrecipient and auditor roles;
- application, nomination, solicitation, pledge, award decision, acceptance, agreement and amendment boundaries;
- cash, in-kind, service, asset, data and rights contributions, valuation basis and uncertainty;
- restricted versus unrestricted purpose, earmark, condition, eligibility, prohibited use, matching, co-funding, cost-share and indirect-cost rules;
- award ceiling, committed amount, obligated amount, disbursed amount, recognized amount, spent amount, refunded amount and available balance as separate assertions;
- schedule, tranche, milestone, deliverable, performance period, reporting period, closeout and post-close obligations;
- external payment, banking, currency, exchange-rate, tax, accounting, procurement, payroll and asset-register references;
- output, outcome, indicator, baseline, target, measurement, evaluation, attribution and causal limits;
- monitoring, report, review, approval, exception, suspension, termination, clawback, refund, recapture, dispute and audit evidence;
- donor anonymity, dedication, consent, recognition, fundraising-channel and privacy safeguards;
- event, effective, decision, acceptance, transfer, observation, reporting, ingestion and knowledge times in RFC 3339 with seconds and an explicit offset or Z;
- access, segregation of duties, retention, legal hold, correction, minimum disclosure and loss-aware projections.

Prefer current official public-grants rules and data standards, charity or tax authorities, IATI and 360Giving specifications, ISO and W3C standards. Treat accounting, tax, public-sector, aid and charity rules as scoped profiles rather than universal law.

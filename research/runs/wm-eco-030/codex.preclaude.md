# WM-ECO-030 Codex pre-provider boundary note

This is preparatory framing, not provider evidence.

- Registry boundary: award, gift or contribution identity, restrictions, commitments, disbursement bindings, use, reporting, monitoring and closeout.
- Candidate research-plane kind: aggregate specification family with mandatory grant-award versus donation-or-gift discriminator.
- Provisional parent signal: WM-POL-012 Public Benefit / Program from the unified registry only; the frozen relation ledger has no settled WM-ECO-030 edge.
- Mandatory exclusions: public program, campaign, appeal, application workflow, party, project, agreement, pledge, bank transfer, procurement, payroll, invoice, tax, accounting ledger, asset, dataset, evaluation, dispute, audit and records masters.
- Critical non-equivalences: application is not award; decision is not agreement; commitment is not obligation; obligation is not disbursement; disbursement is not receipt; receipt is not eligible expenditure; report submission is not acceptance; attribution is not causality; donation is not grant; grant is not procurement, loan, investment or sponsorship.
- Required output remains Bundle to Layer to Finding to Question to Artifact with source-qualified direct properties, recognition criteria, behavior, allowed operations, constraints, failure modes and context evidence.

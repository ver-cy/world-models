Both providers independently classify Ownership / Stewardship as a reusable
`mixin`. The synthesis retains the control-register boundary: it records the
claimed basis, holders, stewardship, delegation, transfer, evidence and
governance of control, but does not decide legal validity or duplicate party
identity, runtime authorization, audit logging, licences or dispute procedure.

The no-tools adjudication pass accepted three independently grounded Grok
additions: trust arrangements and separate funds; governing law, situs and
mandatory overrides; and involuntary deprivation, insolvency and freeze
effects. Tenure typologies, FATF concealment patterns and transfer-watch
notifications remain deferred where they need a clearer placement or stronger
evidence.

Grok's substantive answer completed with duplicated and malformed JSON
transport. Its raw wrapper is retained unchanged outside Git. A no-tools Claude
pass performed syntax/schema normalization only; the normalization manifest
records input/output hashes, disabled tools and successful validation.

There are no unresolved critical structural conflicts. Source-version checks,
multi-profile validation and several clause-level standards checks remain
explicit publication holds, so the result is labelled `reviewable-draft`.
See `synthesis-plan.json`, `adjudication.json`, `comparison.json` and
`synthesis.validation.json` for the evidence trail.

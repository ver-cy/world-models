# WM-ACT-040 Codex source notes

The Codex fallback used official primary material from NIST, CIS, the IETF,
the United States Office of Personnel Management, CISA, the United Kingdom
National Cyber Security Centre and National Archives, ISO, the European Union
and W3C.

NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 and CIS Controls v8 ground governed account creation,
review, disabling, revocation, separation of duties and timely action when a
person joins, changes role or leaves. SCIM RFC 7643 and RFC 7644 supply portable
User, Group, active-state and provisioning exchange concepts. They do not
define employment authority or authorization semantics, so the model keeps
requested, executed, verified and effective states distinct.

OPM provides a concrete onboarding profile for integrating new employees. The
CISA Interagency Security Committee guide focuses on adverse and involuntary
separations, and NCSC identity and access guidance covers joiners, movers and
leavers. These are jurisdictional or operational profiles, not a universal
employment process. ISO 30414:2025 contributes the public human-capital
reporting frame, but only its public catalogue abstract was used and no
normative conformance is claimed.

GDPR and the NIST Privacy Framework ground purpose limitation, minimization,
rights, protection and privacy-risk treatment. PROV-O grounds attributable
plans, activities, evidence, revisions and invalidations. WCAG 2.2 supports
accessible onboarding and offboarding interactions. UK National Archives
guidance informs retention and disposition, while RFC 3339 supplies timestamps
with seconds and explicit offsets.

Claude Sonnet and Grok 4.6 each timed out after their single bounded 120-second
attempt, so no external result was admitted. No approved relation rows were
supplied; the Employment parent and all proposed master-model relations remain
candidate holds. The source-grounded Codex result passed local validation and
proceeds only as a reviewable draft subject to a separate no-tools adversarial
audit.

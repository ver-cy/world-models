# WM-ACT-040 Codex pre-provider boundary

Status: preparation only, not a provider result.

Onboarding / Offboarding is a governed transition-case aggregate, not the
Employment relation, role assignment, person, account, credential, access
grant, asset, training record, agreement, benefit or audit log. A mandatory
profile separates joining, moving, leaving and rejoining. The case coordinates
authorized desired changes and source-qualified completion evidence while each
master system retains identity and enforcement authority. Planned, requested,
executed, verified and effective states remain distinct.

Adjudication must reject checklist-only completion, copied master records,
standing access without need, access inferred from a SCIM group, revocation
inferred from a request, deletion inferred from disabling, missing asset or
data-custody reconciliation, overwritten history and autonomous grants,
termination, disclosure or deletion outside delegated authority.

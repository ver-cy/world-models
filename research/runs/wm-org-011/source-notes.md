## SRC-001 US Census: Service Annual Survey glossary
https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/sas/about/glossary.html
Public glossary, selected sections opened 2026-09-09. Establishment and firm
have different grains; firm may comprise several locations. Reporting scope
and disclosure are distinct from an ordinary commercial listing. Exact mutable
page revision pin remains open; do not universalize a US statistical definition.

## SRC-002 Insee: Etablissement
https://www.insee.fr/fr/metadonnees/definition/c1377
Page date 2020-11-10. Full short definition read. A geographically distinct
production unit depends on a legal unit. Organizational restructuring and
establishment continuity can differ. France-specific terminology, not a global
assertion that every kind of branch has one site or lacks legal standing.

## SRC-003 Insee: Siret number
https://www.insee.fr/en/metadonnees/definition/c1841
Displayed date 04/12/2019, retained literally to avoid date-locale inference.
Full short definition read: establishment identifier links to legal-unit Siren
through its structure. Do not invent a universal identifier shape from Siret.

## SRC-004 W3C: Organization Ontology
https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-org/
W3C Recommendation; selected sections 2.1, 5.2.3 and 5.4.1 inspected.
Organizations, units and sites differ; registered/primary site roles are
explicit and Site does not exclude virtual premises. ORG does not fully model
delegated authority; Vercy mandate fields are proposed extensions, not ORG
conformance. Exact dated publication URI remains to pin.

## SRC-005 GS1: Global Location Number
https://www.gs1.org/standards/id-keys/gln
Official indexed page inspected 2026-09-09. GLN can identify legal entities,
functions and physical/digital locations. Therefore identifier type and scope
must accompany matching. Allocation/change rules are linked, not fully audited;
no universal relocation or non-reuse rule is inferred from this overview.

## SRC-006 GLEIF: State Transition and Validation Rules
https://www.gleif.org/lei-data/access-and-use-lei-data/level-2-data-reporting-exceptions-2-1-format/2025-07-03_state-transition-validation-rules_2.8.5_final.pdf
Version 2.8.5 Final, 2025-07-03; 173-page PDF. Selected passages on PDF pages
52-53 and 99 inspected through extracted text. International branch LEI can
represent a network of offices in one host jurisdiction. This is the decisive
counterexample to branch equals single premises. Full LEI rules, current
replacement releases and registry round-trip conformance remain unverified.

## SRC-007 Schema.org: LocalBusiness
https://schema.org/LocalBusiness
Public type page opened 2026-09-09; selected definition/property passages read.
Local business projection can expose branch relation, contacts and regular or
exception opening times. This discovery vocabulary is not proof of legal
standing, service permission or current physical presence. Release pin pending.

## SRC-008 W3C: PROV-DM
https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/
Recommendation 2013-04-30; public page opened. Attribution and derivation are
candidate mappings, not access grants or a mechanism for choosing true claims.
Detailed establishment-specific mapping and runtime validation deferred.

## SRC-009 IETF: RFC 3339
https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3339
RFC 3339, July 2002; public page opened. Exact instants need seconds and offset;
opening-hour recurrence still needs a local timezone/calendar contract, and
timestamps are not unique identity or automatic succession evidence.

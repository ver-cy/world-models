Both providers independently classify Organizational Unit as an `entity`: a
persistent, identified part of an organization with governed lifecycle,
hierarchy, mandate and accountability. Legal personality remains with
Organization; Position is an independently occupiable post; Team, Work
Assignment, Site and statistical units remain neighbouring responsibilities.

The no-tools adjudication retained Claude's stronger boundary and concurrent
named-hierarchy model. It accepted Grok's W3C ORG collaboration typing and the
ChangeEvent conformance threshold, plus four non-duplicative operations.
Unit-to-site association remains an explicit boundary question rather than an
invented placement layer.

Grok's substantive answer arrived as an unrecognized transport envelope. The
immutable raw wrapper remains outside Git; a no-tools pass normalized transport
only. One data-element ID repeated across two distinct findings was namespaced
with its parent finding ID. The authoritative directory identifier received the
explicit `master-system` label required by the semantic contract. Both provider
results pass schema and semantic validation.

No critical structural conflicts remain. Live source/version checks, paywalled
ISO clauses, unit-grain staffing standards, additional organizational profiles,
the EU statistical-unit source and regional-scope qualifications remain
publication holds. See `synthesis-plan.json`, `adjudication.json`,
`comparison.json` and `synthesis.validation.json`.

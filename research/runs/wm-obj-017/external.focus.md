# Product Configuration / Variant research boundary

Research WM-OBJ-017 as a governed product configuration or reusable variant
between Product Type / Catalog Item (WM-OBJ-002) and Physical Object / Item
(WM-OBJ-001). Preserve the registered CLASSIFIES relationship to instances.
Distinguish configurable family, option space, partial selection, resolved
configuration, released variant and actual serial-numbered instance. Require
an explicit discriminator when their states differ. A sellable SKU, GTIN,
offer, configuration session, engineering BOM and design revision are related
identities, not interchangeable roots.

Investigate official ISO STEP AP242/product configuration and effectivity,
ISO 10007 configuration management, GS1 identifiers and Consumer Product
Variant, Schema.org ProductGroup/ProductModel/Product/isVariantOf/variesBy,
GS1 Web Vocabulary, IDTA Asset Administration Shell, ECLASS and IEC CDD,
OASIS UBL catalogue items, and official configurable-product implementations
where they reveal operational gaps. Pin sources and distinguish standards
from vendor examples.

Cover option/feature definitions, typed values and units, defaults, mandatory
and optional features, dependencies, exclusions, cardinalities, rule versions,
partial/complete/invalid/unsatisfiable selections, validation explanations,
effective dates and markets, revisions and immutable baselines, classification,
identifiers, selected BOM/design references, physical and functional properties,
recognition, compatibility, availability context, authorized substitutions,
provenance, access, retention and loss-aware crosswalks. Pricing, orders,
inventory, manufacturing execution and instance lifecycle remain external.

Build subject-specific bundles, layers, findings, discriminating questions,
typed candidate properties, artifacts and functions. Avoid repeating a generic
question template under every finding. Explicitly record unresolved boundaries
and specialist profiles; do not claim universal completeness.

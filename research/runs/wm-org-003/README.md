The synthesis classifies Team as an `aggregate`. Both providers model
membership as a separately identified, time-bounded record with role, state and
provenance, but that record has no meaning outside its team root. Organization,
Organizational Unit, Position, Work Assignment, rule-defined cohorts and
subject-specific care teams remain neighbouring responsibilities or profiles.

The no-tools adjudication retained Claude's stronger boundary and accepted
Grok's names and labels, containing/management organization split, nested-team
membership semantics, provisioning, team-post establishment, visibility and
outbound entitlement relations. Competing hierarchy, visibility and typing
vocabularies remain declared profile conflicts rather than a collapsed code
list.

Claude contained two distinct questions with the same local ID. The second was
deterministically namespaced by its parent finding and the result then passed
schema and semantic validation. Grok's substantive answer arrived as an
unrecognized transport envelope; the immutable raw wrapper remains outside Git
and a no-tools pass normalized transport only. Both final provider results are
valid.

No critical structural conflicts remain. Live version pins, clinical,
directory, emergency-response and agile profile round trips, paywalled ISO
limitations, vendor-specific additions and citation provenance remain explicit
publication holds. The accepted RFC 3339 claims were re-pointed to the base
registered source during synthesis and pass local validation. See
`synthesis-plan.json`, `adjudication.json`, `comparison.json` and
`synthesis.validation.json`.

# WM-FLW-004 bounded external research focus

Research a vendor-neutral aggregate model for planned and observed movement of physical goods. Preserve distinct identities for transport movement, shipment, consignment, cargo item, package, handling unit, transport equipment, route, leg and logistics observation. Keep sale, title, inventory, customs-declaration, vehicle, facility, party, contract, invoice, payment, sensor-stream and generic provenance lifecycles in their owning systems.

Prefer a compact exact structure: 7 bundles, 16 layers, at most 32 findings, exactly 3 materially distinct questions per finding, and 10 to 12 functions. Cover identity and transport arrangement; cargo and handling-unit composition; route, schedule and multimodal legs; operational events and milestones; custody, condition and proof; exception, replanning and claims reference; regulatory interfaces, access, retention and standards mappings. Distinguish planned, requested, estimated, predicted, actual, corrected and ingestion time. Treat EPCIS events as observations, not automatic proof of legal custody, ownership or acceptance.

Use 7 to 10 accessible primary official sources from at least four organizations. Prefer UNECE UN/CEFACT MMT RDM and UN/LOCODE, GS1 EPCIS 2.0 and CBV 2.0, WCO Data Model, DCSA Track and Trace, IATA ONE Record, IMO FAL, W3C PROV-O and RFC 3339. Do not claim conformance to inaccessible or unverified material. Entry kind should be aggregate. Direct physical properties of referenced goods, vehicles and equipment remain external; direct informational properties of the movement record are in scope. Proposed relations remain unapproved when no outgoing relation exists in the approved ledger.

# WM-XCT-016 bounded external research focus

Produce one concise, complete `model-research.schema.json` result for
`WM-XCT-016 Identity Register`.

## Frozen boundary

- Treat this as a registry pattern that anchors durable, source-qualified
  identities for persons, organizations, devices, services and things.
- Keep the subject's descriptive profile, credential, account,
  authentication factor, cryptographic key, authorization decision, source
  document and business lifecycle in their external master models.
- Distinguish the subject, registry entry, identifier, alias, credential,
  account, key and evidence. Never infer that identifier equality alone proves
  subject equality.
- Preserve namespace and scheme authority, identifier syntax and uniqueness,
  assignment and binding evidence, lifecycle and status, temporal validity,
  source and assurance, resolution semantics, privacy, subject rights and
  federation mappings.
- Represent candidate matches, equivalence, merge, split, correction,
  supersession and retirement explicitly. Never silently collapse entries or
  erase provenance.
- Make public, relying-party, registrar and subject-self projections
  purpose-bound and minimal. A successful lookup is not authentication,
  authorization, legal identity or current eligibility.

## Size and evidence limits

- Target 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 findings, 72 discriminating questions, 24
  artifacts and 10 functions.
- Use at least 8 current primary official sources from at least 4 independent
  organizations. Prefer NIST digital identity guidance, W3C DID, Controlled
  Identifiers, Verifiable Credentials and PROV, IETF URI, UUID and timestamp
  standards, and official identifier-register standards such as GLEIF or GS1.
- Every structure and function element needs source references.
- State jurisdiction, proofing, privacy, federation, interoperability,
  implementation and external-review limitations as holds.

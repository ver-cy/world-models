# WM-XCT-016 Codex pre-provider boundary

Status: preparatory boundary analysis. This is not a provider result.

- The model is an identity-register pattern, not a universal person,
  organization, device, credential, account, authentication, authorization or
  key model.
- A registry entry anchors a subject reference under a named authority and
  namespace. It says as little as possible about the subject itself.
- Identifier assignment, identifier binding, subject equivalence and
  credential issuance are different assertions with different evidence and
  lifecycle.
- Duplicate candidates and conflicting identity assertions remain visible
  until an authorized, evidenced resolution. Merge, split and supersession
  preserve tombstones, redirects and provenance.
- Resolution returns a scoped, current projection with explicit source,
  effective time and assurance. It does not prove control, authorization,
  eligibility or real-world truth by itself.
- Namespace rules, uniqueness scope, canonical form, comparison rules,
  persistence, reassignment policy and resolver behavior are versioned.
- Public and federated views minimize disclosure. Sensitive identifiers,
  proofing evidence and subject-rights records are deny by default.

Adjudication must reject universal-identity claims, silent record merging,
identifier reuse without policy, destructive correction, credential or key
material inlining, lookup equated with authentication, and federation mappings
that hide loss, ambiguity or conflicting authority.

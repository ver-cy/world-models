# External research focus: WM-ECO-025 Return / Refund

Research one governed return-and-refund case aggregate that links an external fulfilment, entitlement, order or obligation to a requested or required reversal and records authorization, reverse movement or access revocation, inspection, disposition, financial adjustment, refund execution, exception and closure evidence. Support goods, services, digital content, deposits, partial returns, non-return refunds and multi-party profiles without making one profile universal.

## Frozen relation and root boundary

The unified registry gives WM-ECO-024 as a provisional parent signal but the candidate relation ledger has no settled edge for WM-ECO-025. Treat that signal and every proposed relationship as an unresolved hold, not as cascade authority.

The return/refund root owns case identity and version; reason and remedy request; return eligibility and authorization assertions; returned subject, quantity and condition observations; reverse-fulfilment references; receipt, inspection and disposition evidence; refund obligation, calculation and adjustment references; refund execution and reconciliation evidence; exceptions, appeal or dispute bindings; closure, provenance and loss-aware projections.

Keep external: order and contract, fulfilment and delivery, party, product or service, inventory, shipment and carrier execution, package and location, entitlement and digital access, invoice and credit note masters, payment transaction and account, tax ledger, warranty, recall, support case, dispute and adjudication, waste disposition and records retention. A return/refund case stores typed references and subject-specific assertions; it does not remaster those lifecycles.

Separate return request from eligibility determination, authorization, physical handover, carrier custody, receipt, inspection, acceptance of return, item disposition, credit adjustment, refund obligation, refund instruction, payment reversal, settlement and customer receipt. A returned item does not prove refund entitlement; a refund approval does not prove payment; a credit note is not a cash refund; payment reversal is not necessarily cancellation of the commercial obligation.

Separate purchase, fulfilment, defect discovery, request, authorization, label issue, handover, receipt, inspection, decision, refund due, instruction, execution, settlement, notification, observation, ingestion and knowledge times. RFC 3339 timestamps require seconds and explicit offset or Z; legal periods need jurisdiction, rule version, start event and calendar interpretation.

## Required outcomes

- Cover consumer and business returns; goods, services, digital content and access; unwanted, defective, damaged, incorrect, late, recalled and goodwill cases; exchange, repair, replacement, price reduction, credit, refund and no-remedy outcomes.
- Resolve return case, return merchandise authorization, reverse shipment, receipt, inspection, disposition, refund obligation, credit note, payment return or reversal, chargeback, warranty claim, recall and dispute boundaries.
- Cover order or fulfilment binding, returned subject, quantity, unit, lot or serial, condition, reason, evidence, channel, location, custodian, eligibility rule, authorization, deadline and instructions.
- Cover reverse movement, custody, receipt, inspection, acceptance or rejection, restock, refurbish, repair, quarantine, recycle or destroy references without owning warehouse, carrier, inventory or waste masters.
- Cover price, tax, fee, shipping, discount, promotion, deposit, restocking deduction, currency, rounding and allocation inputs without remastering invoice, tax or payment systems.
- Cover partial and multiple returns, combined refunds, split tenders, original and alternate payment routes, store credit, failed or delayed refunds, duplicate requests, fraud signals, disputes, appeals and reconciliation.
- Provide 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 model-specific findings, at least 72 discriminating questions, artifacts and 10 safe functions.

Use primary official sources and pin editions. Include OASIS UBL return or adjustment documents, UN/EDIFACT return messages, ISO 20022 payment-return semantics, consumer and sales remedies, accounting treatment, provenance, time, quality and API projections. Agents may not autonomously determine legal eligibility, authorize a return, issue a shipping label, accept custody, judge condition, select disposition, create a credit note, instruct or execute a refund, reverse payment, disclose protected commerce data or dispose records without delegated authority.

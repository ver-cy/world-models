# WM-ECO-017 Codex source record

Accessed 2026-09-06. Only primary official publications were admitted. Exact
verification and licensing boundaries are in `source-verification.csv`.

The research uses the IFRS Conceptual Framework, IAS 1, IFRS 13 and the IFRS
Accounting Taxonomy 2025; ISO 20022 cash-management messages; XBRL 2.1 and its
Link Role Registry; IMF BPM7 and GFSM 2014; the ECB MFI balance-sheet manual;
SEC 2026 taxonomy support; GLEIF LEI-CDF 3.1; the ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency;
W3C PROV-O and DQV; and RFC 3339.

IFRS material is used only within public access and licensing boundaries. BPM7
is explicitly pinned to the March 2025 white-cover pre-edited edition. These
sources support the position, account, instrument, transaction, valuation and
statement boundaries; stock and flow distinctions; recognition, ownership,
quantity, amount, unit, valuation, restriction, time, reconciliation, quality,
correction and projection semantics. They do not delegate authority for posting,
settlement, netting, valuation approval, impairment, restatement, disclosure,
certification, access widening or records disposition.

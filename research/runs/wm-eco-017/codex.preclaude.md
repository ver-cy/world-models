# Codex pre-provider boundary: WM-ECO-017 Financial Position / Balance

Frozen provisional boundary. The root is a versioned quantitative position or
balance assertion at an as-of time, not an account, instrument, transaction,
journal entry, valuation process, risk calculation or financial statement.
Quantity, monetary valuation, accounting classification, availability,
encumbrance and risk exposure are separate qualifiers. Event, trade, settlement,
booking, valuation, reporting, observation, ingestion and knowledge times must
not be collapsed.

Provisional source families: IFRS Conceptual Framework and Accounting Taxonomy,
IAS 1 and IFRS 13 public materials, ISO 20022 business components and account
reporting messages, XBRL 2.1, IMF statistical manuals, ECB balance-sheet
statistics, SEC Regulation S-X and taxonomies, GLEIF identity data, ISO 4217
maintenance material, PROV-O and DQV. Provider output remains untrusted until
local schema and semantic validation.

The model owns governed value assertions: observed prices, administered or
quoted prices, subject-specific appraisal conclusions and statistical price
or index observations. Every assertion identifies its subject or basket,
interest or right, quantity and condition, currency or unit of account,
market or context, effective or observation time, source, method, status,
uncertainty and revision lineage. It does not own offer, transaction, invoice,
payment, contract, asset, currency, commodity, statistics dataset or document
lifecycle.

A requested price, agreed consideration, invoiced amount, payment, accounting
measurement, professional appraisal and price index are not synonyms even if
their numeric amounts coincide. The previous card groups price observations,
appraisals and indices; research must test that they form one aggregate with
explicit assertion families and compose index production with Official
Statistics rather than copying its full lifecycle.

## Adversarial checks

- Identify whether a value is an observation, request, transaction term,
  accounting measurement, appraisal opinion, model result or index.
- Require subject, interest, quantity, condition, location, market, currency,
  effective time, source, method and epistemic status where applicable.
- Preserve original and converted amounts, rate source, rate time, direction,
  precision and rounding.
- Keep gross, net, tax-inclusive, subsidized, discounted, delivered and
  financed values distinguishable.
- Keep uncertainty, sensitivity, scenario, confidence and quality separate.
- Never overwrite corrected, revised, superseded or withdrawn values.
- Do not interpret a price index automatically as cost of living, inflation or
  an individual item's price.
- Do not absorb offers, transactions, invoices, payments, assets, statistics,
  datasets or evidence documents when governed references are sufficient.

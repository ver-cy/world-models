# WM-MED-003 provider focus

Research the format-independent logical structure for a governed publication or
edition record. Prefer current primary specifications and official maintenance
pages. Keep the result compact enough for the canonical schema: target 6
bundles, 12-16 layers, 24-30 findings, 3-5 discriminating questions per
finding, at least one concrete artifact per finding and 8-12 functions.

The central identity decision must be explicit: a Publication / Edition is one
publisher-authorized release identity bound to a work or expression, not the
work itself, the publication workflow, a physical copy, a digital file or an
access URL. Model manifestations, serial issues, regional or language editions,
revisions and corrected releases without inventing one universal hierarchy.

Investigate at least these official ecosystems where applicable:

- IFLA Library Reference Model and RDA Registry entities and relationships;
- Library of Congress BIBFRAME 2.0, MARC 21 and MODS;
- Dublin Core Terms and Schema.org bibliographic and periodical types;
- DataCite and Crossref metadata and relation vocabularies;
- ISBN, ISSN, DOI and URN identifier authority guidance;
- EDItEUR ONIX for Books and NISO JATS for publishing metadata;
- W3C EPUB, Web Annotation, PROV-O, ODRL and accessibility guidance;
- IPTC NewsML-G2, EBUCore, PBCore and IIIF Presentation where useful;
- PREMIS for preservation events and immutable lineage;
- RFC 3339 timestamps and RFC 8785 canonical JSON.

Separate normative rules from common practice. Pin versions or access dates,
state licensing or access constraints, and expose media-, serial-, scholarly-,
legal-deposit-, jurisdiction-, accessibility- and independent-review holds.

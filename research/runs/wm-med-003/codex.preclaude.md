# WM-MED-003 pre-provider research notes

## Provisional boundary

Treat the subject as an edition-level aggregate or entity whose durable identity
survives changes of catalogue record and access URL. Do not decide aggregate
versus entity solely from the registry's `standalone-mm` packaging label; the
no-tools audit must adjudicate the subject-model kind.

The model may carry ordered constituent references and manifestation bindings,
but article, chapter, work, file, copy, person, organization, publication event,
workflow, holding and identifier-agency lifecycles remain external. A serial
issue may be a profile or nested publication record only when its independent
identity, chronology and enumeration are preserved.

## Evidence plan

Use official conceptual bibliographic models for identity, publisher exchange
standards for operational fields, identifier-agency documentation for scope and
cardinality, preservation and provenance standards for correction lineage, and
web standards only for loss-aware projections. Record source authority,
version/date, URL, access constraints and the exact decision each source supports.

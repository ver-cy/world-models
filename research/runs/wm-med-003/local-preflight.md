# WM-MED-003 local preflight

## Frozen subject

`WM-MED-003 Publication / Edition` describes one governed publication-edition
record: an identifiable, publisher-authorized release of intellectual content
for a stated audience, territory, language, medium and release context. It owns
the edition or release identity, publication statement, lifecycle, revision and
correction lineage, identifiers, constituent ordering and declared availability.

The slash in the catalogue name is a scope label. It does not permit one record
to collapse the abstract creative work, its evolving expression, the edition,
each physical or digital manifestation, every distributed copy and the act or
workflow that released them. The edition is the stable governed subject; format,
channel and interface are projections or external manifestations.

## External masters and adjacent models

- `WM-MED-001 Creative Work / Content` owns abstract work and intellectual
  content identity, authorship and content-level derivation.
- `WM-MED-002 Media Asset / Rendition` owns byte-bearing media assets,
  renditions, fixity, encoding and technical preservation.
- `WM-ACT-044 Content Publication` owns editorial approval, release execution,
  distribution actions, takedown and correction workflows; its candidate parent
  link to this model requires cardinality review.
- `WM-MED-004` through `WM-MED-007` own medium-specific recording, image, 3D,
  broadcast and streaming semantics.
- People, organizations, publishers, identifiers and identifier agencies,
  rights instruments, contracts, channels, platforms, places, events, orders,
  holdings, inventory, copies, citations and evidence remain external masters.

## Required coverage

Cover edition and publication identity; work and expression bindings; edition,
release, version, issue, volume, series and collection semantics; publisher and
contributor responsibility; publication statements; language, audience,
territory and accessibility; constituent ordering and pagination; identifiers
and authority; manifestations and format projections; dates and lifecycle;
corrections, errata, retractions, withdrawals, supersession and lineage; rights,
embargo, availability and access; cataloguing, citation and discovery metadata;
provenance, evidence, quality, validation, retention and machine transforms.

## High-risk conflations to reject

- work, expression, edition, manifestation, issue, release, item and copy;
- publication resource, publication event and publishing workflow;
- edition statement, version string, release number and stable identity;
- ISBN, ISSN, DOI, URN, catalogue key and universal identity;
- publisher, imprint, distributor, platform, repository and rights holder;
- publication date, release date, availability date, copyright date and deposit;
- correction notice, corrected edition, silent replacement and retraction;
- format, carrier, media type, rendition, manifestation and access channel;
- serial title, volume, issue, article and collection membership;
- public availability, legal publication, deposit, dissemination and discovery.

# WM-ACT-045 Codex source notes

The Codex fallback used official primary material from IAIS, NAIC, the FCA,
EIOPA, the European Union, United Kingdom legislation, HL7, ACORD, W3C and the
IETF.

IAIS ICP 19 in the December 2024 ICP and ComFrame release provides the neutral
conduct baseline: timely, fair and transparent handling; documented procedures
from claim raised to settlement; claimant guidance and status; clear reasons;
competent and conflict-free assessment; balanced dispute review; and insurer
responsibility for outsourced handling. ICP 21 supports controlled fraud-risk
management without turning an indicator into a fraud finding.

NAIC Models 900 and 902 provide United States model-law and property/casualty
profiles for reconstructable files, notification, acknowledgement,
communication, investigation, decision reasons, deadlines, offers and fair
settlement. Model 910 supplies a market-conduct record and retention profile.
These are model texts, not automatically applicable law in every state. FCA
ICOBS 8 is a United Kingdom profile for prompt and fair handling, guidance,
progress, reasonable rejection and settlement. The EU motor-insurance
directive supplies a reasoned-offer or reply and claims-representative profile.

EIOPA guidance and the Insurance Distribution Directive preserve the boundary
between claim handling, complaints and independent redress. United Kingdom
late-payment legislation is used only as a regional timing and remedy profile.
GDPR supplies privacy constraints for personal, financial and health data.

FHIR R5 Claim, ClaimResponse and ExplanationOfBenefit supply a Trial Use health
insurance exchange profile separating request, adjudication response and
patient-facing projection. ACORD P&C XML 2.13.0 is cited only from its public
overview; licensed schemas were not used and no ACORD conformance is claimed.
PROV-O grounds accountable activity and delegation, and RFC 3339 supplies
timestamps with seconds and explicit offsets.

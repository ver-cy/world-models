# Codex pre-Claude research: WM-ACT-045 Insurance Claim Handling

## Frozen boundary

This model owns one governed handling case and its attributable process events,
decisions and handoffs. The Insurance Claim master owns the claim identity and
substantive claim assertions. Policy, loss, parties, evidence, payments,
complaints, disputes, fraud investigations and records remain external masters.

## Critical distinctions

- Notification, registration, completeness, admissibility and coverage are
  separate assertions.
- Policy existence, policy in-force state, applicable coverage, liability,
  causation, damage and payable benefit require separate evidence and authority.
- Requested, received, validated, relied-on, challenged and superseded evidence
  states are not interchangeable.
- Estimate, accounting reserve, approved amount, reasoned offer, accepted
  settlement, payment instruction and cleared payment remain distinct.
- A fraud indicator starts a controlled referral; it is not a fraud finding.
- Claim handling, complaint handling, internal review, external dispute,
  litigation, subrogation, salvage and recovery are linked but separate cases.
- Closure does not erase obligations or extinguish appeal, limitation, retention
  or reopening rights.

## Candidate structure

Six bundles cover: case identity and authority; intake, triage and coverage
framing; evidence, investigation and assessment; decisions, settlement and
financial handoffs; communications, disputes and recoveries; lifecycle,
quality, provenance, retention, interoperability and agents.

## Evidence plan

Use official regulator, standards-body, legislature and protocol sources. Pin
versions, separate universal process concepts from jurisdiction or insurance
line profiles, and do not elevate public abstracts into clause-level claims.

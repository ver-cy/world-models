# WM-ACT-051 verified source notes

Accessed 2026-09-06. These are official primary or first-party sources used by
the Codex fallback. Provider wrappers are not sources.

- ISO 37301:2021 provides the primary compliance-management-system frame;
  ISO 31000:2018 provides risk-process concepts; ISO 19011:2018 provides audit
  guidance. Their official catalogue pages were access-restricted, so exact
  normative clauses are not asserted by this draft.
- NIST SP 800-53 and SP 800-53A Release 5.2.0, 2025, separate control catalog,
  customizable assessment procedures, assessment plans and result analysis.
- NIST OSCAL 1.2.3, released 2026-08-07, supplies machine-readable catalogs,
  profiles, implementation plans, assessment plans, results and remediation
  tracking. Its security and privacy scope is not generalized silently.
- US DOJ Evaluation of Corporate Compliance Programs, updated September 2024,
  and USSC 2025 section 8B2.1 provide United States profiles for programme
  design, resourcing, risk assessment, reporting, investigation, incentives,
  discipline, response and improvement.
- OECD Recommendation on Public Integrity, 2017, and the UNODC business guide,
  2013, support integrity systems, leadership, risk assessment, controls,
  reporting, enforcement, monitoring and review without creating binding law.
- OMG BPMN 2.0.2 supplies process notation, and OMG SACM 2.3 supplies claims,
  argumentation and evidence structures for assurance cases.
- OASIS SARIF 2.1.0 supplies a portable automated-analysis result format only
  for applicable tool findings; it is not a general audit or legal schema.
- W3C ODRL 2.2, PROV-O, EU GDPR and RFC 3339 support policy-expression,
  provenance, protected processing and explicit event and knowledge times.

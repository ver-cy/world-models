# WM-ACT-051 Codex pre-provider note

This is preparatory boundary evidence, not provider research and not a
publication artifact.

- Registry identity: `vr.wm-act-051`, model `WM-ACT-051`, name Regulatory
  Compliance Process.
- Registry record plane: `world-model`; catalogue entry kind:
  `standalone-mm`; candidate subject-schema kind: `aggregate`.
- Purpose: connect obligations, controls, evidence, findings and remediation
  for a defined subject and scope without representing compliance as a timeless
  binary fact.

  candidate boundary notes and permit no cascade behavior.
- Expected external masters: law and regulation, obligation, policy, risk,
  control, organization and party, asset and system, audit or assessment,
  evidence, incident, task or work order, attestation, regulator submission,
  provenance, access audit and records policy.
- Required direct properties: programme identity and scope, jurisdiction,
  source revision, applicability rationale, obligation-control mapping,
  implementation and operating-effectiveness assertions, evidence coverage,
  finding and exception disposition, remediation and verification state,
  reporting, attestation, review and successor lineage.
- Required safety boundary: no autonomous legal interpretation, certification,
  regulator filing, control enforcement, exception approval, evidence deletion
  or external status change without explicit delegated authority.
- Required time format: RFC 3339 with seconds and explicit offset or `Z`, with
  source effective, assessment, evidence coverage, finding, remediation,
  verification, attestation, submission, recorded, ingested and knowledge times
  separated whenever they differ.

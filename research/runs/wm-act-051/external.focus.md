# WM-ACT-051 bounded provider focus

Research the governed compliance-cycle aggregate for one defined subject,
regulatory scope, jurisdiction and effective period. Cover obligations,
applicability decisions, risk and control mappings, implementation evidence,
monitoring, assessments, findings, exceptions, remediation, attestation and
reporting without claiming that the process itself creates law or proves
compliance.

Use current official primary sources. At minimum inspect ISO 37301:2021,
ISO 31000:2018 and ISO 19011:2018 catalogue material; current NIST SP 800-53,
SP 800-53A and OSCAL materials; the September 2024 US Department of Justice
Evaluation of Corporate Compliance Programs; the 2025 US Sentencing Guidelines
section 8B2.1; the OECD Recommendation on Public Integrity; UNODC corporate
ethics and compliance guidance; OMG BPMN 2.0.2 and SACM 2.3; OASIS SARIF 2.1.0;
W3C ODRL 2.2 and PROV-O. Record sectoral and jurisdictional limits.

Stress-test these semantics:

- a legal or regulatory source, requirement and obligation retain external
  authority; the process owns an attributable applicability interpretation and
  mapping, not the law itself;
- control definition, implementation assertion, test procedure, test result,
  evidence item, finding, exception, remediation action and attestation are
  different records with independent identities, sources, states and times;
- compliance status is a bounded claim for a scope and evaluation time, never
  an unqualified fact about an organization;
- audit, inspection and assessment execution remain external specialist
  processes; this aggregate records commissioning, evidence exchange, result
  intake, response and closure context;
- evidence must preserve provenance, collection method, period covered,
  integrity, access limits, freshness and contradictions;
- exceptions and waivers require authority, scope, rationale, compensating
  controls, expiry, monitoring and review; they do not erase the obligation;
- remediation closure is separate from action completion and requires
  attributable verification against defined acceptance criteria;
- reporting, attestation, certification and regulator acceptance are distinct;
  submission does not prove acceptance or ongoing compliance;
- changes create successor lineage and do not rewrite released evidence.

No relation-ledger edges are registered. Treat plausible links to obligation,
risk/control, audit, assessment, assurance, incident, task, policy, evidence and
records models as relationship-completeness holds rather than approved
composition. Keep the result reviewable-draft until independent review and
release-pinned mappings clear the recorded holds.

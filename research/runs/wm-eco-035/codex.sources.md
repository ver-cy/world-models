# WM-ECO-035 Codex source set

Primary official sources: IAASB standards and handbook for audit, review, assurance, sustainability assurance and agreed-upon procedures; the IESBA Code; IIA Global Internal Audit Standards; PCAOB auditing standards; GAO Yellow Book; INTOSAI principles; ISO 19011; EU statutory-audit law; RFC 3339; PROV-O; DQV; and ODRL.

These authorities define different engagement families, assurance levels, objectives, evidence duties, report semantics and jurisdictions. They are versioned profiles rather than interchangeable labels. Subject matter, subject-matter information, criteria, assertion, evidence, finding, misstatement, deficiency, management response, practitioner conclusion, opinion and regulator decision remain independently governed objects.

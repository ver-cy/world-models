# Codex pre-Claude research: WM-ECO-037 Debt Instrument

Boundary hypothesis: one debt instrument is an identity-bearing, versioned
contractual definition of a debtor's obligation and a creditor's claim. It owns
the instrument's economic and legal terms, scheduled cashflow formulas,
optionality, ranking, covenant terms, credit-support references and amendment
lineage. It references, but does not own, parties, legal documents, issuance,
holdings, trades, settlement, payments, ledger entries, observations,
valuations, ratings, collateral, guarantees, defaults, disputes, enforcement,
insolvency, tax, filings and records.

Critical distinctions to test: instrument versus issuance versus position;
asset and liability perspectives versus identity; scheduled, due, paid and
posted amounts; original, issued and outstanding principal versus valuation;
coupon terms versus observations; secured assertion versus collateral and
perfection; covenant versus compliance and breach; rating opinion versus fact;
and modification versus novation, restructuring, refinancing and
derecognition. Candidate extension of WM-ECO-005 is not approved.

Primary-source discovery set: IFRS Foundation, BIS, ECB, IMF, ISO, OMG, EDM
Council, ISO 20022, XBRL International, IETF and W3C. External providers must
verify editions, distinguish formal from development releases and avoid
claiming universal regulatory or accounting conformance.

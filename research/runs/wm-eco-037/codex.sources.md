# WM-ECO-037 source verification notes

Codex used official primary-source pages and documents only. The evidence set
combines financial-reporting, securities-statistics, identifier, ontology,
message, reporting, temporal, provenance, quality, policy and vocabulary
authorities because no one source defines a universal storage-independent Debt
Instrument model.

IFRS pages support classification, presentation, contractual cash-flow,
impairment, modification, derecognition and disclosure boundaries, but licensed
standard text was not reproduced and no accounting conclusion is claimed. The
BIS/ECB/IMF handbook and BIS statistics pages support debt-security identity,
coupon, principal, maturity, issue, position and valuation distinctions.

ISO 6166, ISO 10962, ISO 4217, ISO 17442 and formal OMG FIGI 1.2 support
identifier and classifier projections. FIBO debt, bond and asset-backed
security pages support ontology alignment; the referenced master/latest pages
are development artifacts, so production-release pinning remains a hold.
ISO 20022 and XBRL are versioned transport/reporting projections rather than
semantic masters. RFC 3339, PROV-O, DQV, ODRL, OWL-Time and SKOS cover clocks,
lineage, quality, access policies, intervals and controlled vocabularies.

The resulting boundary keeps the instrument definition separate from issuance,
holding, payment, ledger, market observation, valuation, rating, collateral,
default and legal-case masters. Exact jurisdiction, debt class, licensed
content, external release and implementation conformance remain deferred.

The no-tools adjudication classified ML Model Artifact as an `entity`. Its
governed identity survives repackaging and serialization changes; content
digests remain integrity and pinning keys rather than the business identity.
AI systems, datasets, training runs, evaluation campaigns, inference runs,
ordinary software packages and individual files or OCI blobs remain sibling
records connected through typed evidence and lineage edges.

Claude was retained as the structural base. The accepted Grok addition records
mutable, environment-scoped deployment aliases without adopting deprecated
stage vocabulary. Three Grok operations were also accepted for interchange
projection, alias assignment and authority-facing documentation release.
Fifteen decisions were recorded and no critical conflict remains.

Both provider runs used the same frozen contract and clean input commit
`c6599d6a77b0f7aea0361910e4ce04d0c2896478`. Grok returned substantive content
using a non-canonical response envelope. The raw wrapper remains outside Git; a
no-tools pass normalized only the contract shape, made no semantic repairs and
required no cross-grain identifier repairs. Both provider results and the
synthesis pass schema and semantic validation.

Live source and version verification, EUR-Lex reconciliation, limitations of
paywalled ISO evidence, four-profile validation, re-checking current MLflow
alias semantics and independent confirmation of signing, transparency-log and
build-attestation sources remain publication holds. Artifact sameness across
repackaging, continual learning, specialised model forms and unsettled
watermarking or fingerprinting standards remain deferred research. See
`synthesis-plan.json`, `adjudication.json`, `comparison.json` and
`synthesis.validation.json`.

# WM-ECO-027 Codex source basis

The Codex fallback used publisher and public-authority sources from TM Forum, IAB Tech Lab, the European Union, the UK Information Commissioner's Office, the United States Federal Trade Commission, Schema.org, IETF, W3C and the OpenAPI Initiative.

The evidence supports a vendor-neutral marketing-campaign aggregate and its boundaries. It does not make a funnel, audience taxonomy, KPI, attribution method, experiment design, advertising channel, privacy framework or legal regime universal. IAB specifications cover important digital-advertising profiles but do not define all campaigns. Several TM Forum resource pages were verified through official indexed content while direct automated access was publisher-blocked.

Claude Sonnet and Grok 4.6 each timed out on the single bounded attempt. No provider output or unverified provider fact was admitted.

## Boundary hypothesis

The model represents an authorization application, assessment and decision case plus later administrative actions concerning the grant. It does not own the permission-bearing grant. A favorable decision references the separately mastered right or credential. Applicants, authorities, legal sources, public-service definitions, criteria, evidence files, payments, inspections, enforcement and appeals also retain external masters.

## Required distinctions

- process case versus resulting authorization grant;
- application status versus grant status;
- criterion definition versus case-specific assessment finding;
- evidence artifact versus evidence submission and verification assertion;
- administrative decision versus legal instrument or court judgment;
- permit, licence, approval, registration, exemption and certificate as jurisdiction-qualified classes;
- renewal or variation process versus mutation or replacement of the external grant;
- automatic expiry versus suspension, revocation, annulment and surrender;
- capability, state, transition, affordance, operation, permission, constraint and failure mode.

No workflow outcome may be presented as legal authority or current grant validity without an authoritative decision and external grant reference.

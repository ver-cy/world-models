# WM-POL-004 bounded research focus

Treat this file as frozen scope, not as evidence. Verify substantive claims against primary official sources.

Research the governed administrative process and case by which a person or organization applies for a permit, licence or authorization, an authority assesses requirements and evidence, and a reasoned decision grants, refuses, varies, renews, suspends, revokes, annuls or closes the request. The model owns the case identity, requested scope, submissions, criterion evaluations, consultations, decision records, reasons, appeal and review routes, and process events. The resulting permission-bearing grant or credential remains an external rights or attestation master referenced by the decision.

Do not treat permit, licence, registration, approval, exemption and certificate as universal synonyms. Do not infer a valid grant from a successful workflow, file, payment, evidence submission or status page. Keep applicant, authority, legal instrument, public service, criterion definition, evidence artifact, payment, inspection, enforcement action, appeal case and granted right in external masters.

Prioritize primary specifications and legislation from EU CPSV-AP, Core Criterion and Core Evidence Vocabulary, the Single Digital Gateway framework and evidence-exchange regulation, OASIS LegalRuleML, UN/CEFACT licence and permit exchange material, W3C PROV-O and OWL-Time, and RFC 3339. Sector examples are profiles, not universal permit law.

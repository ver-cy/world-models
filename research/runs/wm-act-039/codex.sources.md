# WM-ACT-039 Codex source notes

The Codex fallback used official primary material from ISO, the ILO, the EEOC,
the United States Office of Personnel Management, the European Union, the
European Data Protection Board, the United Kingdom Information Commissioner's
Office, the European Commission, HR Open Standards, W3C, NIST, the UK National
Archives and the IETF.

ISO 30405 supplies the cross-sector recruitment frame of planning, attracting,
sourcing, assessing, employing and learning, but only its public catalogue
abstract was available. The ILO fair-recruitment guidance grounds worker-rights,
intermediary, fee, transparency and remedy controls. The EEOC Uniform
Guidelines material separates the total selection process from its components,
requires job-related validation in its stated circumstances and makes clear
that the four-fifths rule is an enforcement rule of thumb rather than a legal
definition. The OPM roadmap provides a concrete United States federal process
profile, not a universal workflow.

GDPR, EDPB automated-decision guidance and the EU AI Act inform purpose,
minimization, transparency, rights, human oversight and high-risk recruitment
automation within their legal scope. The ICO recruitment and selection material
adds detailed candidate-data handling, verification, vetting and retention
guidance, but remains draft guidance after a closed consultation. These sources
are retained as regional profiles rather than universal rules.

Schema.org JobPosting informs public opportunity projection. ESCO supplies
versioned multilingual identifiers for occupations and skills. HR Open
Standards identifies recruiting exchange vocabularies, but downloads require
registration or terms acceptance and no round-trip conformance was tested.
PROV-O grounds attributable plans, evidence, decisions, revisions and
invalidations. WCAG 2.2 supports accessible digital candidate journeys. The
NIST Privacy Framework and UK National Archives guidance inform privacy risk,
retention and disposition. RFC 3339 supplies timestamps with seconds and
explicit offsets.

Claude Sonnet and Grok 4.6 each timed out after their single bounded 120-second
attempt, so no external result was admitted. The registry parent link to
Vacancy / Matching and all proposed inter-model relations remain candidate
holds because no approved relation rows were supplied. The source-grounded
Codex result passed local validation and proceeds only as a reviewable draft
subject to a separate no-tools adversarial audit.

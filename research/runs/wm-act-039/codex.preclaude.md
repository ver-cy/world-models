# WM-ACT-039 Codex pre-provider boundary

Status: preparatory boundary analysis. This is not a provider result.

- Recruitment / Hiring Process is a governed process aggregate, not a Person,
  Vacancy / Matching record, Candidate, Application, selection instrument,
  Interview, Decision, Offer, Contract, Employment or onboarding record.
- A mandatory process profile distinguishes single-vacancy, pooled or
  evergreen, internal mobility, contingent, agency, public-sector and
  cross-border recruitment.
- The root owns its process identity and lineage, authorized purpose and scope,
  exact requisition, criterion and policy bindings, stage design, participating
  role assignments, controlled candidate-case references, process events,
  bounded selection and offer outcomes, closure and aggregate quality evidence.
  Referenced masters retain identity, ownership and lifecycle.
- Sourcing, application, eligibility, qualification evidence, assessment
  result, interviewer judgment, ranking, shortlist, accountable selection,
  offer, acceptance, contract and employment are distinct assertions.
- Draft, authorized, open, paused, closed, cancelled, filled, corrected,
  superseded and archived events are attributable and append-only. Reopening or
  materially changing the hiring need creates a governed successor or new root.

Adjudication must reject an applicant-tracking database clone, copied person or
vacancy masters, undocumented criteria, retrospective criterion changes,
silent candidate deduplication, inferred consent, score-only selection,
fairness claims based on one ratio, hidden automation, overwritten evidence or
decisions, recruitment fees charged to workers as a default, employment
inferred from an accepted offer, and autonomous rejection, disclosure or
disposition outside delegated authority.

# WM-ACT-039 bounded external research focus

Produce one concise, complete `model-research.schema.json` result for
`WM-ACT-039 Recruitment / Hiring Process`.

## Frozen boundary

- Treat frozen `standalone-mm` as record-plane metadata and choose the subject
  kind independently. Test aggregate, process and event interpretations.
- Resolve the slash name with a mandatory process profile. Model one governed
  recruitment process aggregate for a bounded hiring need or campaign, from an
  authorized requisition and sourcing plan through applications, selection,
  offer and closure. Keep each person, vacancy, candidate case, application,
  assessment, interview, decision, offer, contract and resulting employment as
  a separately identified master or linked child record.
- Cover process identity and lineage, hiring need and requisition, organization
  and position bindings, workforce and budget authority, job analysis,
  competency and criterion revisions, posting and channels, sourcing and
  referrals, agencies and fees, candidate notice and consent, applications,
  eligibility and accommodations, screening, assessment, interview, reference
  and background checks, ranking and shortlisting, conflicts and recusal,
  accountable decisions, offer and negotiation, acceptance or rejection,
  closure, metrics, fairness and adverse-impact observations, complaints and
  remedies, lifecycle, provenance, interoperability, privacy, retention and
  safe agent operations.
- Preserve separate masters for Organization, Organizational Unit, Position,
  Vacancy, Person, Candidate, Application, Qualification, Competency,
  Assessment Definition, Assessment Result, Interview, Evidence, Decision,
  Offer, Contract, Employment, Communication, Dataset, Artifact and Audit.
- Do not infer qualification from a resume, eligibility from sourcing,
  suitability from a score, consent from application, fairness from an 80
  percent ratio, employment from offer acceptance or job performance from an
  opaque model. Do not let agents autonomously reject candidates or expose
  protected data without delegated authority and applicable policy.

## Size and evidence limits

- Target 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 findings, 72 discriminating questions, 24
  artifacts and 10 functions.
- Use at least 10 primary official sources from at least 6 independent
  organizations. Prefer ISO 30405, ILO fair-recruitment guidance, EEOC UGESP,
  OPM end-to-end hiring, GDPR and EDPB automated-decision guidance, the EU AI
  Act, ICO recruitment guidance, Schema.org JobPosting, ESCO, HR Open
  Standards, W3C PROV and WCAG, NIST privacy guidance, records guidance and
  RFC 3339.
- Every structure and function element needs source references.
- State the limits of paywalled ISO text, draft ICO guidance, US federal and
  EU or UK legal profiles, protected-class analytics, small samples, automated
  decision systems, cross-border recruitment, interoperability mappings and
  the missing independent provider review as visible holds.

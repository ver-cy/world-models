# Codex pre-Claude research: WM-FLW-009 Journey / Trip

Boundary hypothesis: one journey is a versioned movement episode or intended
movement instance with declared subject, origin, destination, temporal basis
and ordered stages. It owns journey-scoped identity, assertions, state and
correction lineage. It references route, itinerary, service, schedule, places,
vehicles, parties, bookings, tickets, shipments, incidents and observations
without absorbing their master lifecycles.

Critical distinctions to test: personal trip versus vehicle journey; trip
versus wider travel or tour; journey versus reusable route; actual movement
versus itinerary or routing result; leg or stage versus boarding; scheduled,
predicted, estimated, observed and actual time; origin or destination activity
versus passing call; transport mode versus vehicle identity; position fix
versus trajectory; interruption versus completion; distance measured along the
travelled track versus straight-line distance; and reported trip versus
operational service truth. The candidate WM-FLW-007 parent is not approved.

Primary-source discovery set: CEN Transmodel, MobilityData GTFS, OGC, UNECE,
Eurostat, ITF, UN Statistics, European Commission, UK Department for Transport,
Schema.org, IETF and W3C. External providers must verify releases, expose
terminology conflicts and avoid treating public-transit or tourism profiles as
universal journey law.

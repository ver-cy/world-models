# WM-ACT-049 verified source notes

Accessed 2026-09-06. These are official primary or first-party sources used by
the Codex fallback. Provider wrappers are not sources.

- HL7 FHIR R5 CarePlan 5.0.0, maturity 2, Trial Use: a planning request that
  groups goals, participants and intended activity references for a subject.
- HL7 FHIR R5 EpisodeOfCare 5.0.0, maturity 2, Trial Use: the association and
  responsibility of a provider for a patient over a period, distinct from both
  CarePlan planning and Encounter activity.
- HL7 FHIR R5 Goal 5.0.0, maturity 2, Trial Use: a desired state with its own
  lifecycle, achievement status, priority, targets, source and outcomes.
- HL7 FHIR R5 CareTeam 5.0.0, maturity 2, Trial Use: dynamic participants,
  roles, periods and on-behalf-of relationships for coordination and delivery.
- HL7 FHIR R5 RequestOrchestration and PlanDefinition 5.0.0, maturity 4,
  Trial Use: subject-specific related requests versus reusable protocol and
  order-set definitions with hierarchy, conditions, options and dependencies.
- HL7 FHIR R5 Task 5.0.0, maturity 3, and Workflow maturity 2, both Trial Use:
  work execution state remains separate from request authorization and data
  exchange alone does not standardize workflow execution.
- HL7 FHIR R5 ClinicalImpression maturity 1, Condition maturity 5 and
  Observation Normative: assessment reasoning, concerns and measurements are
  separately governed evidence used to create or evaluate a plan.
- WHO Continuity and Coordination of Care, 2018, and UHC service-planning
  guidance: person-centred relational, informational and management continuity
  across providers, organizations, settings and transitions.
- NICE NG197, 2021: people and professionals work together on treatment and
  care decisions, including communication of benefits, risks and consequences.
- IHE Dynamic Care Planning, revised 2019: a profile for interoperable care-plan
  sharing and updates, not universal clinical or legal policy.
- openEHR Task Planning PROC Release-1.7.0 is RETIRED. Its planning, teamwork,
  recurring-task, decision-path, cancellation and tracking concepts are useful
  research evidence but are not a current conformance target.
- EU GDPR, W3C PROV-O and RFC 3339: purpose limitation, protected processing,
  attributable revisions and interoperable event and knowledge timestamps.

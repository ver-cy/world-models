# WM-ACT-043 Codex pre-provider boundary

Status: preparation only, not a provider result.

Business Continuity / Recovery is a governed continuity-capability aggregate,
not an incident, response case, risk register, organization, service, activity,
resource, supplier, contract or audit master. It owns capability identity and
released continuity specifications, BIA and strategy assertions, plan versions,
exercise and activation evidence, recovery verification and improvement lineage.
External masters retain their own identity and operational state.

Adjudication must reject unqualified "critical" labels, copied master data,
targets presented as measured outcomes, plan approval treated as activation,
invocation treated as execution, backup existence treated as recoverability,
exercise completion treated as readiness, restoration treated as return to
normal, silent overwrites and autonomous activation or disclosure without
delegated authority.

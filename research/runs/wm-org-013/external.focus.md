# WM-ORG-013 independent bounded research

Research Stakeholder / Interest: a party's source-qualified stake in a specified
organization, project, decision or impact, not a person/organization identity
and not equity ownership. Distinguish affectedness, expressed interest,
influence, rights, representation and engagement evidence. Low influence must
not erase materially affected parties. Silence or attendance is not consent.

Target six subject-specific bundles, twelve findings, thirty-six discriminating
questions, six scoped record operations, all service layers and five object
facets. Use at least six primary sources from three independent organizations.
Examine GRI stakeholder definitions, World Bank ESS10, OECD participation and
UN rights-based engagement for bounded applicability. Do not assume universal
legal consultation obligations or turn stakeholder records into political or
personal profiling. Reference grievances, commitments and engagement events
without remastering their workflows. Respect privacy, contested representation,
disability/language access, collective interests and changing valid times.

Return canonical-schema JSON. Keep source/license/release and executable gaps
visible. Roles/rights are attributed, not inferred from influence scores.

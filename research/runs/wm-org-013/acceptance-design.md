These are design rationales, not claims that every field is a normative term.

Negative examples for eventual fixture implementation:

- A highly affected community has little influence: it stays included; rating
  must not suppress its rights or affectedness.
- A spokesperson claims to represent everyone but some members dissent:
  keep bounded mandate and dissent, do not invent collective consensus.
- A stakeholder attended a meeting or did not reply: consent remains unknown.
- Analyst inferred a concern: label attributed hypothesis, not self-expression.
- The group leaves consultation: retain unresolved impact and distinguish
  engagement withdrawal from the end of a stake.
- An outdated power score exists: require scale, date and uncertainty; never
  convert it to a stable personal characteristic or legal entitlement.
- A public report links a vulnerable group: retain protected-source controls;
  no automatic sensitive-attribute inference or contact-list publication.
- A correction arrives concurrently: expected-head conflict, append reconciliation
  only under policy rather than discard the older attributable assertion.

Five facets: relationship identity/class, direct scope/interest/roles/time,
recognition from evidence with uncertainty, permitted record operations and
context/evidence are required. Physical mass/geometry/material not applicable.
Endpoint party, organization, project, impacts, grievance, commitments and
engagement-event identities remain mastered elsewhere; proposed references must
not be portrayed as existing approved relation-ledger edges.

Service layers must specify owner package, namespaces, pinned canon/extensions,
master-ID/URI/UUID priority, RFC 3339 seconds+offset for precise instants,
policy-scoped CRUD, contributor/steward/reviewer/custodian roles, field-scope
access, correction/retention and AGENTS.md entry/read order.

# WM-ECO-031 Codex source set

Primary official sources: ILO C095, C100 and C131; EU Directives 2019/1152 and 2023/970 plus GDPR; US DOL wage recordkeeping and IRS Publication 15; HMRC PAYE and FPS guidance; CRA T4001; ATO Single Touch Payroll Phase 2; New Zealand IRD payday filing; OECD Taxing Wages; Eurostat Structure of Earnings Survey metadata; ISO 20022 and ISO 4217; XBRL 2.1; RFC 3339; PROV-O; DQV; ODRL; OWL-Time; and OpenAPI 3.1.1.

Jurisdiction-specific labour, tax, benefit, garnishment, reporting, privacy and accounting rules are profiles, not universal semantics. Search-index verification was used only where the official site rejected or timed out automated direct retrieval.

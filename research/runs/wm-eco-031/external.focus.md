# WM-ECO-031 provider focus

Research a governed Payroll / Compensation specification, not a worker, employment contract, organization, job, timekeeping, attendance, benefit plan, tax account, bank account, payment rail, accounting ledger or generic document master.

Resolve the slash boundary explicitly. Test a payroll-run and worker-compensation aggregate in which compensation terms, earned results, statutory and voluntary deductions, employer contributions, payment instructions, pay statements, corrections and reporting evidence remain separate identities. A payroll result must never prove that time was worked, a payment settled, a tax authority accepted a filing, a benefit was delivered or an accounting entry posted.

Cover at minimum:

- stable payroll-run, payee result, earning-period, jurisdiction, employment or engagement, pay-group and master-system identities;
- employer, worker, contractor, payroll administrator, time approver, compensation authority, tax authority, benefit provider, garnishment authority and payment-provider roles;
- compensation agreement, salary, wage, hourly rate, piece rate, commission, bonus, allowance, reimbursement, benefit, equity and employer-contribution boundaries;
- gross earnings, taxable and pensionable bases, pre-tax and post-tax deductions, employer costs, net pay, currency, units, rounding and balancing;
- regular, supplemental, retroactive, off-cycle, final, advance, reversal, correction and void processing;
- hours, units, overtime, leave, absence, timekeeping, expense, sales and performance references without absorbing their masters;
- tax withholding, social insurance, pension, benefit, union, court order, child support, loan, charitable and voluntary deductions with authority and priority;
- eligibility, effective dating, proration, ceilings, floors, thresholds, accumulation, year-to-date values and period-to-date values;
- calculation inputs, rules, versions, intermediate results, overrides, approvals, anomalies and reproducibility evidence;
- payment allocation, destination, instruction, execution, rejection, return, recall and settlement references;
- pay statement, filing, declaration, remittance, reconciliation, correction, audit and retention evidence;
- event, earning, effective, cutoff, calculation, approval, payment, settlement, filing, observation, ingestion and knowledge times in RFC 3339 with seconds and explicit offset or Z;
- least-privilege access, segregation of duties, worker privacy, legal hold, correction lineage and loss-aware projections.

Prefer current official labour, tax and payroll authorities, ISO 20022, OECD or ILO sources, XBRL or relevant statutory reporting standards, and W3C or IETF governance standards. Treat jurisdiction, worker classification, labour, tax, benefit and accounting requirements as scoped profiles rather than universal law.

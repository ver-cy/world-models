# External research focus: WM-ECO-026 Sales Lead / Opportunity

Research one governed commercial-pursuit aggregate that can begin as a lead signal and, after explicit qualification, become an opportunity case. It links external parties, customer or account relationships, needs, offerings, interactions, activities, quotes and outcomes while preserving stage, score, probability, value, evidence, privacy and forecast provenance. Support business-to-business, business-to-consumer, public-sector, partner-led, inbound, outbound, renewal, expansion and tender profiles without making one sales method universal.

## Frozen relation and root boundary

The unified registry gives WM-ORG-014 Customer / Account Relationship as a provisional parent signal but the candidate relation ledger has no settled edge for WM-ECO-026. Treat that signal and every proposed relationship as an unresolved hold, not as cascade authority.

The root owns pursuit-case identity and version; lead or opportunity profile; origin signal and source attribution; stated or inferred need with confidence; qualification assertions; offering-fit and value estimates; pipeline stage and gate evidence; probability and forecast-category assertions with method version; stakeholder roles within the pursuit; next-action and interaction references; conversion, merge, split, progression, hold, reopen, win, loss, no-decision and closure evidence; governance and loss-aware projections.

Keep external: person, organization, party and contact identity; customer or account relationship; consent and preference master; marketing campaign and audience; product, service, offering, catalog and price; activity, task, calendar, message and communication content; territory, channel and partner; quote, proposal, tender, order and contract; invoice and payment; project or fulfilment; competitor; sales target, quota and aggregated forecast; identity resolution, access, audit and records policy. The case stores typed references and assertions; it does not remaster those lifecycles.

Separate a raw lead signal from a known person or organization, qualified lead, customer/account relationship and opportunity. Lead conversion does not merge identities or erase lineage. Separate pipeline stage, qualification decision, score, probability, forecast category, expected close date and actual outcome. Probability is an assertion produced by a named actor or model under a versioned method, not a fact about the future. Estimated value is not price, quote, order, contract, revenue or cash.

Separate source-event, capture, consent, qualification, stage-entry, last-contact, next-action, forecast-as-of, expected-close, quote, decision, win/loss, closure, observation, ingestion and knowledge times. RFC 3339 timestamps require seconds and explicit offset or Z; local business dates and deadlines need timezone, calendar and source context.

## Required outcomes

- Cover lead signal, qualified lead and opportunity without collapsing them into party, contact, account or customer identities.
- Cover inbound, outbound, referral, campaign, event, partner, renewal, expansion, cross-sell, upsell, tender and public-sector profiles.
- Cover source and attribution, need or demand, offering fit, scope, quantity, timing, location, budget, value, currency, stakeholders, competitors, risks, dependencies and next actions.
- Cover qualification dimensions, score and reason, stage and gate criteria, probability and confidence, forecast category and as-of date with method version and evidence.
- Cover assignment, team, stakeholder influence and decision roles, interactions and activities without owning people, accounts, campaigns, messages, calendars or task masters.
- Cover capture, deduplication, merge, split, qualification, conversion, nurture, progress, hold, reopen, win, loss, no-decision, disqualification and closure with immutable lineage.
- Cover privacy, lawful basis, consent or objection references, purpose limitation, minimization, access, retention and deletion without embedding one jurisdiction as universal.
- Provide 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 model-specific findings, at least 72 discriminating questions, artifacts and 10 safe functions.

Use primary official sources and pin editions. Include an open sales-opportunity API, party and customer boundaries, quote and product-catalog boundaries, UBL quotation semantics, public web vocabulary, privacy and electronic-marketing law, provenance, time, quality, policy and API projections. Agents may not autonomously identify or enrich people, contact prospects, infer sensitive traits, determine consent, assign owners, change stage or probability, commit forecasts, send communications, issue quotes, accept orders, sign contracts, disclose protected data or delete records without delegated authority.

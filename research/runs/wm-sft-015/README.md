passes: definition/design, execution/result evidence, and governance/assurance.

deduplicated sources, routed coverage dimensions, selected governance service
layers and revalidated the combined structure. The repository owner explicitly
waived Grok for this queue, so no Grok output was fabricated or inferred.

The first no-tools audit found two critical merge-residue contradictions in the
design boundary prose. Only the design pass was re-run with a stricter complete-
model contract; the merged result and comparison were rebuilt and a fresh
no-tools audit found no critical conflict.

digest-bound test-case definition revision is the citation root, while
executions, observed outcomes, verdicts, evidence and governance are governed
members. The root is explicitly not a lifecycle owner: results may outlive a
retired definition and bindings may be many-to-many. The frozen registry-plane
`standalone-mm` value remains a separate catalogue classification.

Publication holds cover the owner-authorized single-provider waiver, live and
version verification of 33 sources, weak design-source authority, one
contradictory FDA citation set, a duplicated standard record, serial/capture
artifact identity defects and stale coverage statements. The package therefore
remains `reviewable-draft`, `canonical_publishable` is false, and every public

# WM-XCT-017 bounded external research focus

Produce one concise, complete `model-research.schema.json` result for
`WM-XCT-017 Attestation / Credential`.

## Frozen boundary

- Test the frozen `mixin` kind against the fact that an issued attestation or
  credential has its own identity and lifecycle. State any reclassification.
- Distinguish claim, attestation, credential, certificate, licence or permit,
  presentation, proof, status entry, issuer, holder, subject and verifier.
- Keep person and organization identity, issuer mandate, subject profile,
  source evidence, cryptographic keys, trust policy, authorization decision,
  permit grant and generic audit log in their external master models.
- Preserve issuer authority and role, subject binding, claim schema and
  semantics, issuance evidence, validity, suspension, revocation, expiry,
  renewal, correction, supersession, status freshness and provenance.
- Separate authenticity and integrity verification from claim truth, issuer
  trust, subject control, authorization, eligibility and business acceptance.
- Make holder, verifier, issuer, status-list and audit projections
  purpose-bound and privacy-minimizing, with selective disclosure and
  correlation risks explicit.

## Size and evidence limits

- Target 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 findings, 72 discriminating questions, 24
  artifacts and 10 functions.
- Use at least 8 current primary official sources from at least 4 independent
  organizations. Prefer W3C VC, Data Integrity, Controlled Identifiers and
  Bitstring Status List, IETF X.509, OCSP, SD-JWT and timestamp standards,
  OpenID4VP, NIST digital identity and official ISO credential profiles.
- Every structure and function element needs source references.
- State legal-effect, trust, privacy, status, interoperability,
  implementation-security and external-review limitations as holds.

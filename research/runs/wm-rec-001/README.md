The no-tools boundary decision classifies Document / Record as an `aggregate`.
The aggregate governs one intellectual record together with its versions,
instantiations, signatures, custody intervals, holds and disposition evidence,
because last-copy, legal-hold and tombstone invariants span those components.
Record sets remain a referenced WM-REC-015 sibling, while files, formats,
evidence objects, forms and publications retain their own identities.

The adjudication retained Claude's stronger sibling boundaries, bitemporal
governance, retention, disclosure and crosswalk-loss treatment. It accepted
Grok's capture threshold and non-record exclusions, record-to-record
supersession and dated authoritative-record assessment, plus four missing
operations. Eighteen decisions were recorded and no critical structural
conflict remains.

Grok returned a complete substantive answer in an alternate provider schema.
The immutable raw wrapper remains outside Git; a no-tools pass mapped it to the
frozen Vercy contract without access to Claude, web or shell. Both provider
documents pass schema and semantic validation.

Live source pins, consolidated eIDAS text, RiC and C2PA version drift, three
materially different validation profiles, the future WM-REC-015 boundary and
removal of residual conformance wording remain explicit publication holds. See
`synthesis-plan.json`, `adjudication.json`, `comparison.json` and
`synthesis.validation.json`.

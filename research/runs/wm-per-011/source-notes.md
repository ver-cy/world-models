# WM-PER-011 selected source evidence

Inspected 2026-09-09 UTC. Selected definitions only; not exhaustive social,
psychological or legal coverage. All structural questions are authored Vercy
design, not a claim that every source mandates every field. No private person
records, messages or contacts were accessed for this research.

## SRC-001 RFC 6350 vCard

https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6350

August 2011. Section 6.6.6 RELATED permits URI/text relationships and type
qualifiers; it is an exchange property, not a standalone bilateral truth record.
The current RFC page lists updates RFC6868/9554/9555. Those updates and errata
were not exhaustively audited; only the selected base clause is admitted.
No claim of full current vCard export conformance. Rights review still required.

## SRC-002 HL7 FHIR R5 RelatedPerson

https://hl7.org/fhir/R5/relatedperson.html

Version 5.0.0. Scope distinguishes related-person attribution from Patient.contact
contact information. Selected fields include relationship and valid period.
Useful healthcare-profile boundary example, not a universal person-relation
schema, clinical recommendation or authorization to disclose patient data.
Patient-specific roles and full person demographics must not be copied into
this root merely because the source resource supports them.

## SRC-003 schema.org Role

https://schema.org/Role

Page identifies itself as development version. Role supports qualifying a
relationship with properties such as roleName/startDate/endDate. This supports
qualified role context; current development content is not a pinned release.
No claim that generic Role establishes legal authority or social reciprocity.
Version and licensing verification remain holds.

## SRC-004 FOAF

https://xmlns.com/foaf/spec/

FOAF 0.99, 14 January 2014, selected knows definition. It describes broad
acquaintance with reciprocated interaction but does not imply friendship,
endorsement or face-to-face meeting; publication by the other party is not
required. FOAF also discusses speculative inferences from shared context;
this model does NOT adopt those as automatic sensitive-inference rules.
Use a selected semantic alignment, not a universal edge classifier. License
and dated-URL pin remain to verify.

## SRC-005 W3C PROV-O

https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/

Selected wasAttributedTo and qualifiedAttribution link a record to its author.
Attribution is not truth, consent or bilateral confirmation. The model uses
attributed assertions as the bridge between a relationship and conflicting
perspectives. Exact dated pin and reuse verification remain open.

## SRC-006 ICO data minimisation guidance

https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/data-minimisation/

Selected purpose/minimisation and opinion-record guidance: retain sufficient,
relevant context, clearly distinguish opinion and identify its basis. The page
explicitly warns of review following the Data (Use and Access) Act. UK guidance,
not globally binding advice or a complete privacy compliance determination.
OGL v3 noted, with exceptions; exact applicability and reuse review remain open.

# WM-PER-011 Interpersonal Relationship

Independently research a relationship between persons, with role direction,
validity, attributed claims and privacy. Do not conflate relationship with either
person, address-book entry, household, marriage record, encounter or access grant.
One person's statement is not bilateral agreement. Friendship, kinship, care,
mentorship and acquaintance can have distinct/asymmetric semantics and periods.
Do not infer sensitive relationships from messages, co-location or surname.

Cover participant references and scoped identity, typed direction/inverse,
reported/verified/contested state, roles and applicable period, provenance,
interaction and commitments by reference, contact boundaries, lifecycle changes,
confidentiality and authority to store/share. Distinguish social relation from
legal representation, contactability, consent and communication authorization.
No universal closeness score or automatic judgment of emotions and trust.

Use primary standards/ontologies and official privacy guidance; profile-specific
rules stay profile-specific. Consider vCard RELATED, FOAF knows, schema.org Role,
HL7 RelatedPerson and provenance. Return a bounded complete schema-valid JSON
with subject-specific bundles/layers/findings/questions, artifacts, functions
and all Vercy service layers. Do not access local files or contact other agents.

# WM-PER-011 planned acceptance fixtures

Design only, not executed tests.

1. Relationship ID cannot replace either person ID; ambiguous endpoints block merge.
2. Parent/child or mentor/mentee directions cannot be silently reversed.
3. One participant's report remains attributed, not bilateral agreement.
4. A knows edge does not imply endorsement, intimacy or legal representation.
5. Shared surname, location, photos or messages do not authorize sensitive inference.
6. Social relation, contact preference and permission to communicate stay distinct.
7. Ending one role/episode does not end all relationships between the same people.
8. Record time does not replace uncertain effective dates.
9. Contested perspectives remain separate; newest record alone does not decide truth.
10. Export refuses loss of required privacy/perspective/direction qualifiers.
11. A tombstone must not expose erased sensitive details; local retirement does
    not declare that a real-world relationship ended.
12. No record operation autonomously sends messages or grants access to another
    person's data; references require their own authority checks.

Nested field schemas, episode/reciprocity fixtures, legal privacy profiles and
round-trip adapters are still deferred. Validation must expose these gaps.

Both providers independently classify Decision / Approval Record as an
`entity`: one authoritative decision or approval instance. The model keeps the
decision act and its record distinct but co-modelled, while meeting, case,
generic record mechanics, reusable decision logic and party or policy masters
remain referenced siblings or parents.

The no-tools adjudication retained Claude's clearer identity, authority,
substance, reasoning, lifecycle, disclosure and automation boundary. It added
Grok's title and language designation, adjudicative grade, ruling completeness
and implementation confirmation, provenance-generation activity, and a
stay-or-invalidate-effect operation. Fourteen decisions were recorded and no
critical structural conflict remains.

Grok returned a complete substantive answer in an alternate provider schema.
The immutable raw wrapper stays outside Git; a no-tools pass mapped it to the
frozen Vercy contract without access to Claude, web or shell. Both provider
results pass schema and semantic validation against the same clean input
commit.

Live source pins, paywalled ISO clauses, EUR-Lex obligations, broader sector
and legal-tradition profiles, and the US-specific scope of the imported
5 U.S.C. section 557 findings remain explicit publication holds. See
`synthesis-plan.json`, `adjudication.json`, `comparison.json` and
`synthesis.validation.json`.

# WM-ACT-038 bounded external research focus

Produce one concise, complete `model-research.schema.json` result for
`WM-ACT-038 Learning Activity / Course Delivery`.

## Frozen boundary

- Treat frozen `standalone-mm` as record-plane metadata and choose the subject
  kind independently. Test event, entity and aggregate interpretations.
- Resolve the slash name with a mandatory delivery profile. Model a governed
  delivery aggregate for either a bounded learning activity or a course,
  class, cohort, self-paced offering, workshop or session delivery. Keep the
  reusable course and activity definitions separate from their delivery and
  participation occurrences.
- Cover identity and lineage, delivery profile, provider and authority,
  curriculum and definition binding, learning objectives and competencies,
  instructional design and sequence, prerequisites and workload, instructors
  and support roles, learner and cohort references, enrollment and access,
  schedule and sessions, modality and location, resources and tools,
  accessibility and accommodations, instruction and facilitation, attendance
  and participation, learning-experience events, assignments, assessments,
  submissions, feedback, grades, progress, mastery, completion and withdrawal,
  quality and improvement, lifecycle, provenance, interoperability, privacy,
  retention and safe agent operations.
- Preserve separate masters for Person, Learner, Organization, Course or
  Learning Activity Definition, Curriculum or Programme, Competency or Learning
  Outcome, Academic Session, Location, Resource, Tool, Enrollment, Attendance,
  Assessment Definition, Submission, Assessment Result, Qualification,
  Credential, Dataset, Communication, Artifact and Audit Record.
- Do not infer enrollment from access, attendance from login, engagement from
  time online, learning from activity completion, mastery from one score,
  qualification from course completion or causality from learning analytics.

## Size and evidence limits

- Target 6 bundles, 12 layers, 24 findings, 72 discriminating questions, 24
  artifacts and 10 functions.
- Use at least 10 primary official sources from at least 6 independent
  organizations. Prefer UNESCO ISCED, the European Learning Model, 1EdTech
  Edu-API, OneRoster, Caliper, CASE and QTI, ADL xAPI, W3C PROV and WCAG,
  NIST privacy guidance, records guidance and RFC 3339.
- Every structure and function element needs source references.
- State formal, non-formal, informal, workplace and self-directed education;
  K-12, higher-education and corporate terminology; accessibility; child and
  learner privacy; grading; analytics; regional law; standards-version and
  independent-review limitations as holds.

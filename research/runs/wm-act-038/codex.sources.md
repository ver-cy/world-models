# WM-ACT-038 Codex source notes

The Codex fallback used official primary material from UNESCO, the European
Commission, 1EdTech, ADL, W3C, Schema.org, NIST, the UK National Archives and
the IETF.

ISCED 2011 provides the distinction between an education programme and a
qualification, including the warning that course participation or credit alone
does not necessarily constitute a qualification. The European Learning Model
supports multilingual descriptions of formal, non-formal and informal learning
opportunities, qualifications, accreditation and credentials. Edu-API and
OneRoster inform provider, course, offering, academic-session, user, class,
enrollment and result projections, but their higher-education and K-12 scopes
are not treated as universal delivery semantics.

Caliper and xAPI inform source-qualified learning events while retaining actor,
action, object, result, context, time, authority and correction semantics. CASE
informs competency and learning-outcome bindings. QTI informs assessment items,
tests and results. Common Cartridge informs content and tool exchange. CLR
informs learner-specific achievement assertions. Schema.org CourseInstance
supports the separation of a reusable course from a time-, place-, mode- and
section-specific delivery.

PROV-O supports attributable activity, agent, derivation, revision and
invalidation. WCAG 2.2 informs accessibility requirements without claiming that
it is the only applicable accessibility regime. The NIST Privacy Framework and
UK National Archives guidance inform purpose-bound privacy, retention and
disposition controls. RFC 3339 supplies timestamps with seconds and explicit
offsets.

No independent Claude or Grok result was available because each provider timed
out after its single bounded 120-second attempt. The mandatory delivery profile
is therefore retained to disambiguate the slash name across course, cohort,
self-paced, workshop, workplace, community and single-activity deliveries.
Registry parentage and all proposed inter-model relations remain candidate
holds because no approved relation rows were supplied. Edu-API remains
Candidate Final Public with its full specification access-controlled, and
Common Cartridge 1.4 remains Candidate Final; no final conformance claim is
made. The source-grounded Codex result is schema-valid and proceeds only as a
reviewable draft subject to the separate no-tools audit.

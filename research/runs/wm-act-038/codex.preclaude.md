# WM-ACT-038 Codex pre-provider boundary

Status: preparatory boundary analysis. This is not a provider result.

- A Learning Activity / Course Delivery is a governed delivery aggregate, not
  the reusable Course or Activity Definition, the Curriculum or Programme, a
  Learner, an Enrollment, one Session, one xAPI or Caliper event, an Assessment,
  a Qualification or a Credential.
- A mandatory delivery profile distinguishes a bounded learning activity,
  course or cohort delivery, self-paced offering and other delivery forms.
- The root owns delivery identity and lineage, exact definition and curriculum
  bindings, delivery-scoped design, schedule, role, cohort, access, resource,
  instruction, participation, assessment-administration, progress, completion,
  quality and lifecycle assertions. Referenced masters retain their identity.
- Planned content, actual instruction, learner activity, observation,
  assessment evidence, grade, mastery, completion, qualification and credential
  are distinct assertions with separate authority and provenance.
- Planned, published, open for enrollment, scheduled, active, paused, closed,
  completed, cancelled, corrected, superseded and archived events are
  attributable and append-only. A repeat delivery receives a new identity.

Adjudication must reject a course-catalog clone, a learner-record clone, a
generic event log, access treated as enrollment, login treated as attendance,
time online treated as engagement or learning, completion treated as mastery or
qualification, overwritten grades or evidence, and autonomous enrollment,
grading, credential issuance, protected disclosure or disposition outside
delegated authority.

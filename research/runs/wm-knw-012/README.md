Claude completed one full web-enabled research pass and the local schema and
semantic validator accepted it. The repository owner explicitly waived Grok
for this queue; no Grok output was fabricated or inferred. A separate no-tools
Claude adversarial audit inspected the compact result without adding facts,
findings or functions. It reclassified the subject model from `entity` to
`aggregate` because the statement work, dated expressions, contained rule
slots and manifestations share one enforced consistency boundary. Registry
`standalone-mm` remains a separate record-plane classification. The final
synthesis passed validation with no critical conflicts.

The structure covers policy identity and expression lineage, authority and
mandate, normative modalities, parties and targets, applicability, conditions,
parameters, exceptions and derogations, priorities and conflict strategies,
interpretation, versioning, lifecycle, provenance, access, retention,
interoperability and point-in-time resolution. It references WM-KNW-013 for
constraint-expression internals and excludes runtime evaluation, enforcement,
decision outcomes and audit-log custody.

Open holds include live verification and version pinning of all 13 sources,
the single-provider waiver, verification of the RFC 3339/RFC 9557 timestamp
rule, the registry's candidate/boundary-review state after entry-kind
reclassification, and public support or licensed confirmation for the
paywalled ISO 37301 claims. Further work is required for multilingual
authenticity, delegated authority chains, open-ended retention, the missing
function surface and subject-plane access mappings. The result therefore
remains `reviewable-draft`, `publishable` is false, and every public artifact
exposes the waiver and holds. See `comparison.json`, `synthesis-plan.json`,
`adjudication.json`, `source-verification.csv` and
`synthesis.validation.json`.

# Independent WM-ORG-018 boundary

Governance Body / Committee: constituted collective body with mandate,
membership and decision authority. Distinguish enduring body from a meeting,
seat, office-holder, secretariat, board decision, organizational unit or legal
entity. Advisory, delegated and decision-making bodies need explicit authority
and scope; a committee name does not prove authority. Role/membership does not
automatically grant voting, access or power to bind the organization.

Use primary standards and official governance charters/guidance from different
organizations as contrasting examples, never universal law. Cover constitution,
mandate limits, membership terms and vacancies, chair/secretary roles, conflicts
and recusals, procedure/quorum/voting rules, decision/minute references, reporting,
delegation, renewal/dissolution and accountable access. Preserve rule version,
effective time, dissent and uncertainty. Do not invent a universal quorum or
voting denominator. Meetings and decisions are referenced child concepts.

Produce subject-specific bundles/layers/findings/questions with answer fields,
artifacts, six bounded record functions and all service layers/five facets.
Aim at six bundles, twelve findings, thirty-six questions and at least six
primary sources. Do not access private member data or local files; do not
convene meetings, cast votes, appoint people or execute governance decisions.
Output complete model-research.schema.json JSON with truthful license/source
pins and legal/profile/runtime-validation limits.

# Proposed negative acceptance cases

Not executed tests; future profile-specific validators must cover:

- A body persists through membership turnover; a meeting does not replace it.
- Advisory mandate does not authorize binding decisions.
- Seat exists while vacant; appointment assertion is not appointment execution.
- Observer attendance does not imply vote or quorum eligibility.
- Item-specific recusal uses the governing rule for both quorum and denominator;
  no universal arithmetic is supplied by this specification.
- Abstention, dissent and absence are distinct; simple majority is not assumed.
- Meeting loses quorum midway: preserve per-item evidence and unresolved outcome.
- Draft minutes are not approved minutes; objection survives a correction.
- Rule amendment is not retroactively applied to a previous decision.
- Expired mandate or dissolution does not silently transfer authority.
- Public body identity does not open confidential personnel papers.
- Export losing appointment term or authority scope must warn or refuse.

Five facets: body identity/class; mandate, composition and rule properties;
constitution/appointment recognition evidence; bounded record capabilities;
organizational context and provenance. Physical properties are not applicable
to this constituted entity, and meeting/site geometry stays delegated.

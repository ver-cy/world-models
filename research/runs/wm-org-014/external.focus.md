# WM-ORG-014 bounded independent scope

Research Customer / Account Relationship: a supplier-qualified commercial
relationship record mastered by CRM, not the underlying person/organization.
Keep customer role, CRM account, billing account, legal party, contact, user
login, prospect/opportunity and subscription separate. One party can have
several customer relationships across selling entities or service scopes.

Target six bundles, twelve findings, thirty-six questions and six scoped record
operations, all service layers and five facets. Use six or more primary sources
from three organizations. Examine TMF629, first-party CRM account/contact
schemas and billing customer objects to identify differences, not impose one
vendor as universal. Model identification, lifecycle, related-party authority,
commercial/service references, preferences, mastership, merge/split lineage,
freshness and loss-aware export. Explicitly separate expressed preference from
lawful basis/consent and account state from entitlement, payment or credit state.

No credentials, payment instruments or private real customer records. No
automatic outreach, charging, adverse eligibility decisions or inferred
sensitive profiling. Include unknowns, field access and retention. Source pins,
legal applicability and executable adapters remain visible gaps when unverified.
Return complete canonical-schema JSON, not generic padding.

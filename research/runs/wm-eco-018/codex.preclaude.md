# Codex pre-provider boundary: WM-ECO-018 Financial Statement

Frozen provisional boundary. The root is a versioned financial-report aggregate
covering one statement or a complete set, not its reporting entity, accounts,
positions, transactions, journal entries, trial balance, consolidation process,
accounting policy, audit opinion, filing or publication event. Instant and
duration facts, reporting and comparative periods, preparation, authorization,
issuance, filing, publication, observation, ingestion and knowledge times remain
distinct.

Provisional source families: IFRS Conceptual Framework, IAS 1, IAS 7, IFRS 18
and Accounting Taxonomy 2025, XBRL 2.1, Inline XBRL 1.1 and OIM 1.0, SEC
Regulation S-X and 2026 taxonomy support, FASB 2026 taxonomy, ESEF regulation and
ESMA 2025 manual, IPSASB 2025 handbook, PROV-O, DQV and RFC 3339. Provider output
remains untrusted until local schema and semantic validation.

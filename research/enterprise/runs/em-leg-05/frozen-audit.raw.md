# 1. Verdict on decision and artifacts

**Boundary: settled. Preserved unchanged.**

Claude (`claude-study.raw.md`) and Grok (`grok-study.raw.md`) converge on every load-bearing element, and `local-evidence.md` states the same disposition:

- WM-POL-009 is **reused/profiled** as one forum-qualified, docket-qualified proceeding; it is not the matter.
- **Dispute / Matter** is an independent, **identifier-unassigned** root, because one contested matter persists across transfer, severance, consolidation, first instance, appeal, recognition and enforcement.
- Procedural **claim / counterclaim / defense** and **ProceedingEvent** stay case-owned dependents; neither becomes a root.
- **Party allegation** is a WM-KNW-007 profile; **adjudicated finding** and **legal conclusion** stay authority-owned.
- **Case Outcome** is a projection over judgment identity, disposition, finality and remedy references, never a root or second master of finality.
- **Appeal / Review, Enforcement / Recognition Case, Remedy Obligation** remain unassigned gaps pending registry adjudication.
- Claimed amount is never confirmed debt; provision recognition requires its own authorized act.
- No identifier is allocated.

Only two reconcilable tensions exist in the supplied evidence, and neither disturbs the decision:

- **Appellate recording.** Claude: an appeal is "another WM-POL-009 instance in the appellate forum." Grok: "do not subtype appeal or enforcement into the completed WM-POL-009 profile." These are compatible once separated: the appellate **docket mechanics** may be recorded on a WM-POL-009 instance, while the **Appeal / Review record** (route, grounds, deadline, stay, effect on finality) is *not* covered by this profile and stays unassigned. The artifacts are silent on this split — a defect (D21), not a change of decision.
- **Cardinality.** Grok adds "a proceeding has exactly one primary Dispute / Matter"; Claude and `local-evidence.md` do not contradict it. Adopted as a missing cardinality contract (D6), not as a new boundary.

**Artifacts: conditionally rejected on semantics, structurally compliant on minimums.**

`validation-policy.json` minimums are all met by the current artifacts: `modelId` null, `registryId` null, `allocationState` `unassigned`, 14 invariants ≥ 8, 6 references ≥ 3, 7 fixtures ≥ 3, positive and negative fixtures both present, stable-identity statement present, independent lifecycle present. The artifacts nonetheless carry **24 material semantic defects** across authority, identity, lifecycle, references, time, provenance, privacy/access, finance separation, incomplete reserved specs and publication holds. All 24 are deterministically repairable by literal edits; none requires reopening the boundary, allocating an identifier, promoting a reserved spec, or converting a projection or dependent into a root.

# EM-LND-14 local synthesis

## Disposition

- Treat **AI Research Landscape** as a named, tenant-scoped, as-of declaration plus reproducible projection.
- Treat **AI Usage Network** as an impact/traversal viewpoint over the same declaration and WM-XCT-037 edges, not a root or second edge plane.
- Reuse AI system, agent, evaluation, inference, configuration, training, registry, safety, benchmark, incident, dataset, measurement and model-artifact masters.
- Allocate no catalogue or runtime identifier.

## Identity and mastership

Landscape outputs have artifact identity only: viewpoint, tenant, graph revision, source pins, as-of time, completeness perimeter, crosswalk and policy version. They never mint dataset, run, artifact, system or deployment identity.

Every fact remains owned by one master. Digests verify bytes but do not establish governed identity. Missing edges are unknown unless a closed completeness declaration covers them; withheld edges remain withheld.

## Landscape and usage network

The landscape declaration pins included masters and tenants, lifecycle phases, source revisions, freshness, completeness and redaction policy. The usage-network viewpoint traverses typed dependency edges under explicit propagation licences, depth, class, phase and tenant limits.

Reachability is exposure, not an impact verdict. A second AI-specific graph would fork edge mastership. Reuse of the EM-LND-12 graph host remains held until its managed-service boundary is proven general enough.

## Research, data and rights

Research Programme and Research Question remain unallocated. Dataset, version and distribution stay distinct under WM-DAT-001. Benchmark release remains distinct from its dataset snapshot.

Consent, licence, purpose and export restrictions attach per input and survive training, checkpoints, adapters, quantizations, merges, registry records and endpoints. Shared endpoints merge neither lineage nor rights.

## Training, artifacts and registry

WM-AI-006 owns an execution with immutable inputs, topology, effective configuration, attempts, checkpoints, metrics and outcome. One effective configuration digest pins code, hyperparameters, seeds, dependencies and environment.

WM-SFT-004 owns model artifact payload, serialization, manifest, digest and licence. WM-AI-007 owns registry/discoverability state. Checkpoint selection, artifact promotion, registry publication, release and deployment are separate acts.

## Evaluation, benchmark and safety

WM-AI-003 owns bounded evaluation instances. WM-AI-009 owns benchmark contracts/releases. WM-AI-008 owns one dated residual-risk judgement for a pinned system, purpose, population and context.

Measurement, assessment and authorization remain separate. Benchmark success never grants universal deployment permission. Deployment decisions remain profiles over rationale, authorized act and issued record.

## Systems, deployments and compute

WM-AI-001 is the accountable AI system; WM-AI-002 the durable agent; WM-AI-004 one inference execution. Deployment and endpoint masters are absent and remain external gaps.

Requested compute is run-owned planning; granted compute is an external reservation; observed consumption is a measured flow. GPU-hours, elapsed time, energy, emissions and currency remain distinct quantity kinds. Research compute creates no product, offering, registry entry, deployment or agent identity.

## Withdrawal and impact

Withdrawal of dataset revision D2 traverses pinned training and evaluation bindings, produced artifacts, registry records, system/deployment bindings and derived artifacts.

Direct digest bindings inside a complete perimeter are proven impact. Derived adapters, quantizations, merges or series-head references are potential impact pending rights adjudication. Missing, stale or uncovered lineage is unknown. Applicability becomes invalid and reassessment is triggered; evidence is retained and deployment decisions remain separately authoritative.

## Time, provenance and access

Effective, observation, ingestion, approval, release and knowledge times remain distinct. Evidence is revision-bound and stales under declared rules when pins change. Each edge records kind, nature, phase, determination method, evidence and confidence.

Access is deny-by-default and inherits source rights. Issued views are immutable and superseded additively.

## Acceptance result

Registry records remain but are flagged. Systems S1 and S2 keep separate authorization instruments and validity; withdrawal triggers reassessment rather than automatic revocation. An uncovered tenant segment is unknown, never declared unaffected.

## Holds

Research Programme, Research Question, Deployment/Endpoint, Metric Definition, Training Recipe, Evaluation Protocol and standing authorization remain unallocated. AI registry/artifact, benchmark/dataset and training/artifact edges are unapproved. WM-XCT-037 has scope, identity-strength and composition contradictions that block reliable traversal. Evaluation and benchmark protocol/metric ownership overlaps. Graph-host reuse is unverified. Several drafts are single-provider, all are non-canonical, and fixtures were not run. No installability or publication-readiness claim is made.

## Grok reconciliation

Grok independently accepted both candidates as non-root viewpoints. It sharpened withdrawal semantics: a recorded consume, derive or basis edge establishes proven participation; mixed-input artifacts remain proven impacted with sole-cause false; evaluation and safety records retain identity while applicability is invalidated; systems and endpoints are potential operational impact; missing edges, unofficial copies and undeclared fine-tunes are unknown. Projection membership grants neither rights nor authority, and deployment decisions remain separate records flagged for review rather than automatically revoked.


## Frozen-audit remediation

The single frozen audit returned ACCEPT WITH LIMITS. Eight limits were incorporated once: participation versus harm semantics, receipt-only impact sets, single declaration ownership, non-authoritative derived edges, per-hop and assembled rights filtering, no unaffected class, timed applicability overlays and bounded resolution of potential deployment impact. The audit was not repeated and no identifier was introduced.

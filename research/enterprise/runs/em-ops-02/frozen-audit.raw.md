# Single Frozen Semantic Audit — EM-OPS-02

## 1. Verdict

**REVISE.** The identity decision itself is sound and is not reopened: two identifier-unassigned candidates (Procurement Request, Sourcing Event), four profiles over WM-ECO-021 / WM-ACT-034 ×2 / WM-KNW-010, downstream WM-ECO-006 and WM-ECO-019 untouched. The defect is that the candidate artifacts do not carry the structure their own invariants and fixtures assert. Most invariants in both `allocation-candidate.json` files are unenforceable against the declared `objects`, and the profile candidate is prose with no objects, no fixtures and no validation policy.

## 2. Material defects

**D1 — Event lifecycle conflates event state with round state; the agreed multi-round scenario is unrepresentable.**
`SourcingEvent.lifecycle` = `["planned","published","open","sealed","opened","evaluating","awarded","cancelled","closed"]` while `SourcingRound.lifecycle` = `["draft","published","open","sealed","opened","finalized","superseded"]`. Invariant *"A material post-publication change creates an addendum, equal notification and a successor round"* plus fixture `criterion-addendum` (*"a successor round while the v1 round remains readable"*) require round 1 `finalized` and round 2 `open` at the same instant. A single event-level `sealed`/`opened`/`evaluating` state cannot hold both.

**D2 — No witnessed-opening record and no per-bid seal or time fields; two invariants and one negative fixture are undecidable.**
Invariants *"Receipt time, sealing time and opening time remain distinct"* and *"Sealed content is not exposed before the authorized witnessed opening"*; fixture `open-before-witness` expects *"The disclosure is rejected."* The only related fields are `contentDigest` (required, round-level, subject undefined) and `openingRule` (optional). There is no `openedAt`, no `openingWitnessRef`, no `sealedAt`, no `receivedAt`, and no per-bid digest — though grok-study's accepted scenario is *"Bids B1, B2 and B3 are submitted, hashed and sealed"*, i.e. per-bid.

**D3 — No receipt/admissibility object, though the event claims to own it.**
`owns` lists *"submission window and receipt rules"* and *"bid admissibility and evaluation-round bindings"*; local-evidence says *"the buyer owns only receipt and procedure bindings"*; claude-study adds *"disposition of late or non-compliant submissions"*. No object exists for any of it. Without it, buyer-side receipt has nowhere to live that does not re-mint supplier bid identity — a latent violation of the agreed invariant *"Supplier bid identity and immutable submitted versions remain supplier-owned."*

**D4 — Temporal layer absent.**
The only instant in either candidate is `closedAt` (optional). `submissionWindow` is required but untyped; criteria releases have no `effectiveFrom`, contradicting local-evidence *"Every criterion set has a version and effective time"* and the `identityTest` claim of *"immutable event releases and rounds"*. No format constraint is stated, although claude-study requires *"RFC 3339 with seconds and explicit offset throughout"* and separation of event, observation and ingestion time.

**D5 — No evaluator roster, conflict declaration, independent determination or recusal structure anywhere.**
Invariant *"Conflicts are declared before determination and recusals remain in history"* and fixture `conflicted-evaluator` have no backing fields. claude-study states the base gap explicitly — WM-ACT-034 *"records no function that assigns assessors or captures impartiality before determination begins — the BidEvaluation profile must add that as a required extension"* — and `profile-candidate.json` adds nothing beyond the sentence *"Conflicts, recusals, exclusions, waivers and superseded rounds remain append-only and explainable."* The declarant≠determiner rule and grok invariant 10 (*"A conflicted evaluator does not score the affected comparison"*) are therefore unenforceable.

**D6 — Round-creation and remediation rules are mutually contradictory (three parts).**
(a) Trigger: invariant 6 makes a successor round the consequence of a *criteria change*, but fixture `conflicted-evaluator` expects *"a replacement round is attributable"* from a *recusal* — a round with no criteria change and no defined criteria binding.
(b) Bias adjustment vs immutability: local-evidence permits outcomes to be *"excluded, replaced or bias-adjusted according to a declared method"* while the invariant states *"Finalized rounds are append-only and never recomputed in place"*; bias adjustment of a finalized outcome is a recomputation unless it is defined as a new marked result.
(c) Bid carry-over: grok-study holds *"Round-1 bids are not re-scored on V2. Suppliers may submit round-2 responses"*; claude-study has *"round 2 is opened and scored under v2"* and *"excluded with re-scoring in round 3"*. The artifacts resolve neither, so `rewrite-finalized-round` only forbids in-place mutation and still permits scoring a round-1 bid version under v2 without resubmission.

**D7 — Notification scope is indeterminate and unrecorded.**
local-evidence: *"equal notification to affected participants"*; claude-study: *"must be notified to all participants"*; `profile-candidate.json`: *"equal participant notification"*. No notification record exists and `participantScope` is `optional`, so "equal" has no referent set and no evidence.

**D8 — `procedureKind` is unenumerated and single-source obligations are inconsistent.**
Invariant 1 requires a `procedureKind` but no value set is declared, so the conditional single-source rules cannot bind. No field holds justification ground, deciding authority, mandate scope or baseline. Fixture `single-source` expects *"A real one-alternative set, justification and waiver are recorded"* — making a waiver mandatory — while the invariant requires only *"a real one-alternative set and justification"* and local-evidence says *"and any waiver"* (optional).

**D9 — `evaluating` and `awarded` are states with no anchoring field, and `awarded` leaks award into the event.**
`excludes` lists *"assessment or award-decision identity"*, yet `owns` lists *"award linkage and closure history"* and the lifecycle contains `awarded`. `objects.SourcingEvent` has no `evaluationRefs` and no `awardDecisionRef`, so both states are unverifiable, and grok invariant 12 (*"AwardDecision does not create, sign or imply WM-ECO-006 or WM-ECO-019"*) has no counterpart constraint keeping `awarded` purely referential.

**D10 — Procurement Request declares version identity it cannot express.**
`versionIdentity`: *"Changes to requested scope, quantity, delivery constraints, authority or funding references create immutable request versions"*; invariants *"Scope, quantity and delivery changes create traceable request revisions"* and *"Historical request versions remain resolvable after fulfilment or closure."* `identity` is `["procurementRequestId"]` only — no revision identifier, no supersession chain, no effective time. `successorRef` is overloaded: `versionIdentity` also says *"a materially distinct need creates a new request"*, so the same field would denote both revision succession and new-request succession.

**D11 — No approval record, and `authorityRef` is optional.**
`owns` includes *"approval, rejection and withdrawal history"*; lifecycle includes `approved`; invariant *"Approval authorizes sourcing activity only within the recorded scope"*. No approver, no authority binding at approval, no decision instant, no pinned scope revision — and `authorityRef` sits under `optional`, so an `approved` request with no authority at all validates.

**D12 — Trace links are claimed but absent, and the request↔event direction is contradictory.**
`owns` includes *"sourcing-event and downstream trace links"* and fixture `split-fulfilment` expects *"The request retains one identity and references both orders without merging them"* — yet `ProcurementRequest` has no `sourcingEventRefs`, `agreementRefs` or `orderRefs`. On the event side `requestRefs` is `optional` and plural, contradicting grok-study's accepted boundary *"An event cites one authorizing request"* and leaving the invariant *"A rejected request cannot silently enter a sourcing event"* with no enforcement point on either side. Compounding this, the reference entry `{"target":"WM-KNW-010","purpose":"Approval or award decision"}` fuses the request's *owned* internal approval with the award decision its own `excludes` disclaims.

**D13 — The profile candidate carries no testable obligation, and is misfiled.**
`profile-candidate.json` has `bases` and six prose `constraints`; it declares no objects, no required fields, no fixtures file and no validation policy — so SupplierBid, BidEvaluation, SupplierQualification and AwardDecision, which carry most of the fairness load, are entirely unverified. The seam rule *"references evaluation results without copying them"* has no field-level prohibition and no stated behaviour when no BidEvaluation exists; AwardDecision is nowhere required to cite the criteria version, the relied-upon round, the considered bid set or the selected bid, although both studies make those mandatory. It is also stored inside `candidate-allocation-offline-procurement-request/`, whose `excludes` list disclaims *"supplier qualification or bid evaluation"* and *"award decision, agreement or purchase order"*.

**D14 — Validity windows are never checked at the moments that matter.**
claude-study: *"bid validity windows can lapse before award"* and qualification is *"a precondition or screening input to bid admissibility"* with *"its own validity window"*. No invariant, field or fixture requires bid validity to hold at the award instant or qualification standing to be pinned as of receipt, so a lapsed bid can be awarded and an expired qualification can gate admissibility silently.

**D15 — Fixtures are not mechanically checkable, and one bundles two assertions.**
All twelve cases state `expect` in prose with no code; `validation-policy.json` requires `minimumFixtures`, `requiresPositiveAndNegativeFixtures` and similar counts but nothing tying fixtures to invariants, so 24 invariants are covered by 12 prose cases. `winner-is-contract` expects *"Both transitions are rejected"* — contract formation and order issuance in one undiscriminated assertion. Separately, both candidates write `"canonicalPublishable":false` while the hold text in both studies names the field `publishableCanonical`; one of the two spellings will not validate.

## 3. Required bounded fixes

1. **D1** — Reduce `SourcingEvent.lifecycle` to round-agnostic states `["planned","published","active","evaluating","awarded","cancelled","closed"]`. Keep `sealed`/`opened`/`finalized`/`superseded` only on `SourcingRound`. Add `SourcingRound.roundNumber` (serial, append-only).
2. **D2** — Add required `SourcingRound.openedAt` and `SourcingRound.openingWitnessRef` as a conditional pair on state `opened`; rename round `contentDigest` to `criteriaSnapshotDigest` with a stated subject; move seal evidence to the receipt object (below).
3. **D3** — Add object `BidReceipt`, identity `["sourcingEventId","roundId","bidRef","bidVersion"]`, required `receivedAt`, `contentDigest`, `sealState` (`sealed|opened`), `admissibility` (`admitted|late-admitted|rejected|withdrawn`), optional `latenessDisposition`, `waiverRef`, `qualificationStandingRef`. State explicitly that `bidRef`/`bidVersion` are supplier-issued and never re-minted.
4. **D4** — Require RFC 3339 with seconds and explicit offset for every instant; add `publishedAt`, `submissionWindow.{opensAt,closesAt}`, `criteriaEffectiveFrom`, `finalizedAt`, `awardedAt`; keep event time separate from record/ingestion time.
5. **D5** — In the BidEvaluation profile add required `evaluatorRoster[]` with `{evaluatorRef, mandateRef, assignedAt, declaredInterests[]}`, and `conflictDetermination[]` with `{interestRef, determinedBy, determinedAt, outcome, recusalEffectiveAt}`. Constrain `determinedBy != declarant` and `determinedAt < first outcome instant for the affected comparison`.
6. **D6** — (a) Add `SourcingRound.roundKind` ∈ `{solicitation, remediation}`; `remediation` must pin the same `criteriaSetRef` and the same bid versions, and may not reopen submission. (b) Define bias adjustment and exclusion as **new marked results in a successor round**, never edits to a finalized outcome; add `resultMarker` ∈ `{active, excluded, bias-adjusted, contested, superseded}` with `supersedesResultRef`. (c) State the rule once: a bid version scored under criteria vN may never be scored under vN+1; a new criteria version requires a solicitation round with new or re-affirmed submissions.
7. **D7** — Make `participantScope` required on any `published` round. Add `Addendum` with required `notifiedParticipantRefs[]`, `notifiedAt`, `criteriaSetRef`, and a rule that the notified set equals the round's participant scope. Fix the wording to **all participants in scope** and align `profile-candidate.json` to it.
8. **D8** — Enumerate `procedureKind` ∈ `{open, restricted, negotiated, single-source}`. When `single-source`, require `justificationGround`, `decidingAuthorityRef`, `mandateScope`, optional `baselineComparisonRef`, optional `waiverRef`. Correct the `single-source` fixture: waiver optional, justification and authority mandatory.
9. **D9** — Add optional `evaluationRefs[]` and `awardDecisionRef`, required when status is `evaluating`/`awarded`. State that these are referential only and create no agreement or order.
10. **D10** — Add `ProcurementRequest.revisionId` to identity, plus required `revisionEffectiveFrom` and `supersedesRevisionRef`; rename `successorRef` to `replacementRequestRef` for the distinct-need case.
11. **D11** — Add required `approvals[]` `{approverRef, authorityRef, decidedAt, decision, approvedScopeRevisionRef}`; move `authorityRef` to required once status reaches `submitted`.
12. **D12** — Add `sourcingEventRefs[]`, `agreementRefs[]`, `orderRefs[]` to `ProcurementRequest`. Make `SourcingEvent.authorizingRequestRef` required with a single value, or an explicit `noRequestGround` from a closed list. Add the invariant that a request in `rejected`/`withdrawn` cannot be an `authorizingRequestRef`. Split the WM-KNW-010 reference into two purposes: *internal procurement approval* (owned) and *award decision* (referenced only).
13. **D13** — Give the profile candidate objects and required fields for SupplierBid, BidEvaluation, SupplierQualification and AwardDecision; add `fixtures.json` and `validation-policy.json` alongside it; relocate it out of the Procurement Request directory into a shared `profile-em-ops-02/`. Require on AwardDecision: `criteriaSetRef` (versioned), `reliedUponRoundRef`, `consideredBidRefs[]`, `selectedBidRef`, `authorityRef`, `divergenceFromRanking`, `conflictRecordRefs[]`; prohibit `criterionOutcomes` whenever a BidEvaluation exists, and require an explicit `noEvaluationGround` when none does.
14. **D14** — Add invariants and fields: award requires `selectedBid.validUntil >= awardedAt` or a recorded extension; `BidReceipt.qualificationStandingRef` must be valid at `receivedAt`; a bid score never writes back to qualification standing.
15. **D15** — Add `expectedCode` to every fixture; split `winner-is-contract` into separate contract and order cases; add `requiresInvariantCoverage: true` and `requiresExpectedCode: true` to both `validation-policy.json` files; settle on one spelling of the publishability field across all artifacts.

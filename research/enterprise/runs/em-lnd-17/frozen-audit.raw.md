# Frozen no-tools semantic audit — EM-LND-17

## Verdict

**REVISE.** The identity decision and the boundary direction are sound and are accepted as audited; the revision is confined to object definitions and invariant enforceability within the two candidates and the projection boundary. Three of the supplied fixtures (`plan-observation-divergence`, `unknown-whereabouts`, `delegated-maintenance`) are **unsatisfiable against the objects as written**, and several stated invariants have no field to bind to. These are internal contradictions, not base or registry gaps, so they cannot be carried as holds.

## Material defects

1. **Observation provenance unenforceable.** The invariant requires observed placement to record observation time, ingestion time, **source**, precision and confidence, yet `observationTime`, `ingestionTime`, `precision`, `confidence` are all `optional` and no source/sensor field exists at all (`evidenceRefs` is optional and is not a source). An observed episode with none of these is admissible.
2. **Planned placement unanchored.** Synthesis and Grok both require a planned placement to belong to a named plan/scenario with authorizing context; `scenarioRef` and `basisRef` are both optional. A plan with no scenario and no basis is admissible, contradicting the mastership claim.
3. **Divergence owned but not representable.** `boundary.owns` includes "planned-versus-observed divergence" and an invariant asserts an "explicit divergence state", but no lifecycle value and no field carries it. Fixture `plan-observation-divergence` cannot pass.
4. **Lifecycle conflates mode, validity and epistemic state.** `status` repeats `placementMode` (`planned`/`observed`), permitting `placementMode: observed` with `status: planned`; `effective|corrected|superseded|closed` are record/validity states; `unknown` is an epistemic state about the asset, not a state of an episode.
5. **Unknown whereabouts unrepresentable.** `locusRef` and `locusKind` are required, so `status: unknown` is self-contradictory and the only escapes are a sentinel locus or omitting the fact. Fixture `unknown-whereabouts` cannot pass, and the required five-way distinction (**withheld / unknown / absent / disposed / never observed**) has no carrier — `disclosureClass` is optional and does not express it.
6. **No record-time axis and no version identity.** `versionIdentity` promises versions and the invariant requires corrections to "preserve the assertion that was previously effective", but there is no version id, no `assertedAt`/record interval, and no immutability rule. `ingestionTime` is optional and scoped to observations. Consequently `overwrite-move-history` is unenforceable and **reproducible history and as-of projection are unverifiable**.
7. **Lineage direction collides and is inconsistent across candidates.** `supersedesRef` serves both correction-versioning and successor-episode succession; the responsibility candidate exposes only `successorRef`. One direction, distinct from version lineage, must be used in both.
8. **No mutual exclusion over effective observed placements.** Nothing forbids two concurrently effective observed episodes at mutually exclusive loci at the same granularity for one asset; "a new effective locus closes or supersedes the prior episode" is prose, not a constraint.
9. **Gap semantics undeclared.** Episodes need not be contiguous (the in-transit leg of `leased-asset-move` has no locus), but no invariant states that *absence of an episode is not absence, disposal or non-placement* of the asset.
10. **`locusKind` is open, and granularity is conflated with precision.** The claimed "declared spatial granularity" needs a closed enumeration bound to the cited masters; `precision` carries no method and does not express granularity. Interior space (WM-BLT-002) is cited in synthesis as the finest level but is absent from `references` — the omission must be explicit, not silent. *(The WM-BLT-002 completion and multi-site facility apportionment gaps themselves remain holds.)*
11. **Responsibility: basis optional, transfer/suspension/revocation unenforced.** "Responsibility requires an authorizing authority **and governing basis**" while `governingBasisRef` is optional; `status: transferred` does not require `successorRef` (so `silent-transfer` cannot be rejected); suspension has no interval or resumption record; `revoked` vs `ended` are declared distinct but carry no revocation authority or reason, contradicting "retain authority provenance".
12. **Delegation cannot name a delegate.** Only `delegationConstraints` exists — no delegate party, no `delegatedFromRef`, no delegated validity. Fixture `delegated-maintenance` (bounded inspection to a contractor) cannot be expressed.
13. **Scope composition ambiguous.** `assetScopeRef` plus optional `locusScopeRef` admits two materially different readings (responsible for the asset everywhere vs only while at that locus), and no `scopeKind` distinguishes instance / class / locus-qualified set. No rule governs overlapping same-role assignments to different parties.
14. **Responsibility has no disclosure class.** A responsible party is frequently a natural person. Placement carries `disclosureClass`; responsibility carries none, so the projection boundary cannot suppress party identity.
15. **Projection constraints are owned by the wrong object.** "A projection never reconstructs a finer location than its authorized source rendition" is an invariant of the placement episode, which cannot enforce it; no projection object, pinned-input/as-of/digest requirement, suppression threshold, or cross-rendition composition rule exists. Equal-disclosure renditions at the same as-of can be differenced (generalized place ∩ count-of-one) to recover a withheld locus, and "withheld" has no output marker distinguishing it from absent.

## Required fixes

- Split `status` into `placementMode` (retained), a record/validity `status` (`effective|corrected|superseded|closed`) and an explicit `whereaboutsState` (`asserted|unknown|withheld|never-observed`); make `locusRef`/`locusKind` required only when `whereaboutsState = asserted`; forbid sentinel loci; add `lastKnownLocusRef` + `lastKnownAsOf` qualified separately.
- Make observation provenance (`observationTime`, `ingestionTime`, `sourceRef`, `precision` with method, `confidence`) conditionally required when `placementMode = observed`; make `scenarioRef` and `basisRef` conditionally required when `placementMode = planned`.
- Add a `divergence` assertion (planned episode ↔ observed episode, state, detected-at) satisfying the owned divergence fact.
- Add bitemporality to **both** candidates: `versionId`, `assertedAt`, record interval, immutability of previously effective versions, and one declared lineage direction (`supersedesRef` for succession, version chain separate).
- Add mutual-exclusion and gap invariants for placement; add overlap rules (or explicit joint accountability with apportionment) for same-role responsibility.
- Close `locusKind` over the cited masters; state interior-space deferral explicitly.
- Make `governingBasisRef` required; require `successorRef` when `transferred`; model suspension as an interval with authority and reason; separate `revoked` with revocation authority/reason.
- Model delegation as a related assignment with `delegateRef`, `delegatedFromRef`, delegated scope and validity, preserving accountability.
- Add `scopeKind` and declared scope-composition semantics for `assetScopeRef` × `locusScopeRef`; add `partyKind` + `disclosureClass`.
- Move all projection rules to a `LandscapeProjection` definition inside the landscape declaration: pinned input record-as-of, projection as-of, disclosure policy ref, digest, suppression threshold, no-refinement rule, cross-rendition composition ban, withheld-marker output, purpose-bound availability. It receives **no independent identity**.

## Identifier decision

**Both candidates genuinely require independent identity and both remain `allocationState: unassigned`.** `AssetPlacementEpisode` and `OperationalResponsibilityAssignment` are time-bounded, authority-bearing, externally referenceable facts whose history must survive changes to the asset, locus, party, declaration and view; they cannot be folded into WM-OBJ-001 custody, WM-BLT-* loci, or the landscape artifact. This audit **allocates no registry identifier, reserves no namespace, and confers no publication authority**; no WM-* identifier may be borrowed or guessed. `assetPlacementEpisodeId` and `operationalResponsibilityAssignmentId` are candidate-local semantic keys only. **Asset Place Landscape** keeps local governed-artifact identity only (scope, viewpoint, owner, pinned inputs, as-of, disclosure, digest); **Occupancy View / LandscapeProjection** receives **no independent identity**.

## Freeze decision

This is the single frozen semantic audit and it is **closed**; it will not be repeated. It discharges the "one frozen semantic audit" hold and records **REVISE**. The required fixes are a bounded editorial revision of the two candidates' object definitions, invariants and the relocated projection boundary, to be verified locally against the supplied plus additional fixtures — not a new review cycle. All other holds remain open and unaffected: registry allocation and namespace, WM-BLT-002 completion and interior-space authority, multi-site facility apportionment, WM-BLT-006/WM-BLT-008 publication holds, source crosswalks, privacy and employment-law review, canonical validation, package conversion and live verification. Both candidates stay `canonicalPublishable: false`; the artifact remains a declaration plus projection, not an operational placement or responsibility store.

Claude and Grok agree on the no-new-ID disposition: WM-POL-001 remains sole master of legal work, expression, manifestation, source, provision and norm identity; ExternalRequirement is a norm-layer profile and NormVersion is an expression-layer profile. Citation, applicability, obligation and compliance are four separate claims; applicability must not instantiate a duty and compliance must not be stored on the assessment.

The reconciled result allocates no model or runtime ID and removes Jurisdiction from the allocation queue. Publication remains held until the base profiles can represent the temporal axes, provision-level commencement, structured assessment pins and external jurisdiction key-as-of contract.

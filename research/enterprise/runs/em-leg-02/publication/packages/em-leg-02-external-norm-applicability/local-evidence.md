# EM-LEG-02 provider-reconciled synthesis

## Disposition

Reuse WM-POL-001. ExternalRequirement is a constrained profile of one externally originated norm identity; NormVersion is a constrained profile of one expression. ApplicabilityAssessment is a WM-ACT-034 profile with structured immutable legal pins. Jurisdiction remains an external time-qualified reference. WM-KNW-012 supplies attributable interpretation evidence, and WM-XCT-029 alone owns instantiated duties. No model or runtime identifier is allocated.

## Identity and temporal semantics

WM-POL-001 alone masters legal work, expression, manifestation, source, provision and normalized norm identities. A URL, title, date or hash is locator or evidence. Work identity survives amendment; expression changes with authentic formulation; provisions are expression-relative. Neither market nor assessment mints a legal version.

Publication/promulgation, provision-scoped commencement, efficacy/applicability, transitional or compliance deadline, repeal/supersession, knowledge time, assessment facts-as-of and assessment performed-at are pairwise distinct. Citability after publication does not imply efficacy. NormVersion has no single commencement field. Historical versions and assessments remain resolvable after repeal.

## Exceptions and assessment

Source exceptions, authority-backed derogations, beneficiary-scoped exemptions and transitional periods are separate typed records. A transition binds prior and successor expressions, affected provisions, scoped subject/activity/product, interval and ending event. Expiry never mutates the norm. Derogations cite enabling authority; exemptions identify beneficiary, scope, authority and interval; non-derogable floors survive.

WM-ACT-034 owns assessment identity, status, assessor, performed-at, review or expiry and named supersession. Issued pins cover expression, provisions, context state, jurisdiction key-as-of, facts-as-of, reasoning, evidence, conclusion, confidence and limitations and are immutable. Conflicting or market-distinct assessments coexist. Silent merge, latest-wins and cross-series supersession are prohibited. Indeterminate and disputed remain first-class conclusions.

## Claim separation and fixtures

Citation, applicability, obligation and compliance are four separate claims. Applicability never mints a duty; WM-XCT-029 owns duty identity. Compliance is a separate claim against a duty and is never stored on the assessment. The reconciled profile has 15 constraints and 24 fixtures, including provision commencement, immutable pins, exceptions, jurisdiction key-as-of, duty separation, historical citability and distinct assessment times.

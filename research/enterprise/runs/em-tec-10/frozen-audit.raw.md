# Frozen Semantic Audit — EM-TEC-10 (single pass, closed)

## 1. Verdict

**REVISE.**

The identity split is sound and I do not disturb the fixed decision. The artifacts, however, assert a large body of invariants that their own object definitions cannot carry: the emergency path is unsatisfiable as specified, rollback is re-collapsed inside `ChangeExecution.lifecycle`, Freeze Period survives as a label with no enforcement (and silently becomes an executable window in the reconciled scenario), `DutyRoster` owns shifts, substitutions and handovers while defining no object for any of them, and `profile-candidate.json` specifies no fields at all. These are artifact defects, not registry holds.

## 2. Material defects

**D1 — The emergency fixture is unsatisfiable against the required field set.**
`fixtures.json` (ChangeExecution): `{"id":"emergency-change","kind":"positive","input":"An emergency execution relaxes one control under named authority.","expect":"The relaxation, reason and retrospective obligation are explicit."}`. But `allocation-candidate.json` has `"required":["approvedRequestRevisionRef","authorityRef","targetScope","startedAt","status"]` and the invariant *"Every execution pins exactly one approved request revision and its conditions."* claude-study states the opposite ordering: *"retrospective authorisation is a new decision bound to the same request, not an edit."* An emergency execution runs before that decision exists, so no approved revision can be pinned at start. No `emergency`, `relaxedControls`, `relaxationReason`, `relaxationAuthorityRef`, `retrospectiveDueAt` or `retrospectiveDecisionRef` field exists, and no lifecycle state marks an undischarged retrospective obligation — `closed` is reachable with it open. The invariant *"Emergency execution records relaxed controls, authority, reason and retrospective obligations."* has no carrier.

**D2 — "and its conditions" is unenforceable.**
local-evidence: *"Authorization freezes an approved proposal revision plus conditions and effectivity."* The object carries `approvedRequestRevisionRef` and `authorityRef` only. There is no `conditionsRef`, no `effectivityFrom/To`, and therefore no way to test an execution against the conditions it was authorized under.

**D3 — No version component or digest on any pin.**
grok invariant 14: *"A later plan version does not rewrite the version cited by a past execution."* local-evidence invariant 12: *"Approved revisions, plans, windows, rosters and rules are immutable and superseded explicitly."* Yet `approvedRequestRevisionRef`, `windowRef`, `rollbackPlanRef`, `authorityRef` are scalar references with no revision or digest component, and `ChangeExecution` has no `contentDigest` (unlike `RosterRevision`). `RosterRevision.contentDigest` itself declares no algorithm or canonicalization, so it is unverifiable.

**D4 — The lifecycle re-collapses rollback into the reverted execution.**
`"lifecycle":["scheduled","started","partially-completed","completed","failed","rollback-started","rolled-back","aborted","closed"]` puts rollback states on the occurrence being reverted, contradicting *"Rollback intent, plan, execution and verified restored state remain distinct."*, *"Retries and continuations receive distinct linked execution identities."* and claude-study's *"a ChangeExecution occurrence with kind=rollback and a typed link to the reverted occurrence"*. There is no `kind` discriminator in `objects.ChangeExecution` at all, and `rollbackExecutionRef` is undirected — E1→E2 and E2→E1 are indistinguishable.

**D5 — `rolled-back` and `closed` are reachable without verification, and the verification master is undeclared.**
`verificationRefs` is in `optional`, against the invariant *"Rolled back never means restored without verification evidence."* claude-study anchors verified state in WM-SFT-015 (*"referenced from WM-SFT-015, never restated"*), but WM-SFT-015 appears nowhere in local-evidence, `references`, or `bases`. `verificationRefs` has no declared target type. (The base's publication state is a hold; its absence from the reconciled reference set is the defect.)

**D6 — `startedAt` is required while `scheduled` is a lifecycle state.**
A scheduled occurrence has no start instant, so every scheduled record violates the required set. Symmetrically, `endedAt` is optional with no obligation in `completed`, `failed`, `aborted`, `rolled-back` or `closed`.

**D7 — Per-target recording is optional while `partially-completed` exists.**
`perTargetOutcomes` is in `optional`; the invariant says *"Partial rollout records per-target actions and outcomes."* and claude-study adds *"no aggregate success is inferred"*. Nothing requires outcomes to cover the required `targetScope`, so a partial execution may claim an aggregate result over an uncovered scope.

**D8 — Freeze Period is unenforceable, and the reconciled scenario converts a freeze into an available window.**
grok: *"citing EU window W-EU (open) and US freeze W-US"*, *"Freeze is a prohibition interval, not a denied decision."* local-evidence instead reads *"binds EU and US windows with distinct zones"* and *"the unused US window remains bound"*; the fixture `multiple-windows` repeats *"binds EU and US windows"*. claude-study's derogation machinery (*"derogable only by recorded, bounded derogation"*, *"registered derogation with granting authority, bounded validity, compensating obligation"*) was dropped entirely: local-evidence's twelve invariants and the twelve `ChangeExecution` invariants contain no freeze or derogation clause, and `profile-candidate.json` names Freeze Period only as one of *"immutable schedule or plan releases."* A later executor reading the reconciled artifact sees a bound, unexecuted US **window** where grok recorded a **prohibition**.

**D9 — Two bound windows cannot be represented; the window check is optional.**
Fixture expectation: *"The execution records the EU window while the unused US window remains unchanged."* claude-study: *"a change binding two windows holds two pinned window references, each with its own tz identifier."* `windowRef` is singular and optional on `ChangeExecution`, and `profile-candidate.json` defines no field on the WM-ACT-032 profile to hold the bound set. An execution may also run with neither `windowRef` nor a recorded exemption.

**D10 — The profile candidate specifies nothing.**
`profile-candidate.json` contains `bases` plus six prose `constraints` — no objects, no fields, no identity statements, no lifecycle, no fixtures, and no `validation-policy.json`. Against this, local-evidence asserts *"Change Window has identity, recurrence, exclusions, overrides, disclosure class, timezone identifier, tzdb release and version."* Every profiled thing (Operational Change, Change Window, Freeze Period, Rollback Plan, Escalation Rule and its condition) is unspecified and untested, while both new roots have full specs and policies.

**D11 — The decision triad is missing from the profile bases and from the execution's references; `authorityRef` is untyped.**
local-evidence: *"Decision rationale, approval act and issued instrument stay in WM-KNW-010, WM-ACT-024 and WM-REC-010."* `bases` lists WM-ACT-032, WM-ACT-008, WM-KNW-012, WM-KNW-013, WM-SFT-008, WM-SFT-009, WM-ORG-016, WM-ACT-006 — none of the triad. `ChangeExecution.references` includes only WM-REC-010 of the three. With no target typing on any reference field, invariant *"A window permits disturbance within an envelope but never authorizes execution."* cannot be enforced: `authorityRef` may legally point at a WM-ACT-008 window instance.

**D12 — WM-SFT-009 is asserted as a specialization of an unallocated root.**
`references`: `{"target":"WM-SFT-009","purpose":"Software deployment specialization"}`; local-evidence: *"WM-SFT-009 is the software-deployment specialization"*; claude-study: *"WM-SFT-009 becomes a software specialisation of it"*. This contradicts the same file's `excludes` (*"release, build or deployment identity"*), grok (*"Deployment (WM-SFT-009) may be caused by execution; it is not the change"*) and grok's blocker (*"Profiling is valid only as association, not as subtype collapse."*). A published registry model is being re-parented under a root with `modelId: null`.

**D13 — Duty Roster owns shifts, substitutions, overrides and handovers but defines no object for any of them.**
`owns` includes *"shift occurrences"*, *"substitution and override records"*, *"handover, acknowledgment and retirement history"*. `objects` contains only `DutyRoster{serviceScope, ownerRef, status}` and `RosterRevision{shifts, timezoneId, tzdbRelease, validFrom, contentDigest, …}`, with `shifts` structurally undefined. Eight invariants have no carrier, including *"Every shift references an eligible standing assignment."*, *"Handover records outgoing and incoming parties, instant, open work and incoming acknowledgment."*, *"Substitution and override append attributable records with authorizer and effective instant."* and *"Missing acknowledgment remains an explicit gap and never becomes acceptance."* Fixture `authorized-substitution` expects *"changes the effective shift without rewriting the roster"* while `shifts` sits inside the digested immutable revision — the overlay it requires does not exist.

**D14 — As-of resolution is non-deterministic.**
`validFrom` is required, `validTo` optional, and no invariant forbids overlapping effective revisions, so two revisions may cover one instant. claude-study's *"DST gaps and overlaps require a declared resolution policy per window"* was dropped and no `dstPolicy` field exists. Fixture `cross-zone-shifts` therefore cannot meet its own expectation, *"Each occurrence resolves deterministically with offset and revision."*, and the invariant *"Escalation resolves its target through the roster as of event time."* has no unique answer.

**D15 — No assertion time, no bitemporal separation, no declared instant format.**
claude-study: *"event, observation and ingestion times are separate fields"*; local-evidence reduces this to *"Every instant carries offset"*. Neither object has `assertedAt`/`recordedAt`, so `supersedesRef` chains (*"Corrections append successor assertions for the same occurrence"*) have no ordering key and two competing successors cannot be ranked. The RFC 3339 / explicit-offset requirement exists only in claude-study prose; no field in either JSON declares it.

**D16 — Roster root lifecycle contradicts revision-level immutability.**
`DutyRoster.lifecycle` includes `amended` and `superseded` with `optional:["successorRef","retiredAt"]`, while the invariant is *"Roster revisions are immutable and superseded explicitly."* and the identity test says the roster *"remains identifiable across shift occurrences, substitutions and handovers while its schedule revisions evolve"*. Root-level supersession with a `successorRef` migrates identity and defeats the stability claim. `retiredAt` is not required in `retired`, and nothing carries open-work carryover despite *"Roster retirement never closes employment, assignment, incidents or in-flight changes."*

**D17 — Handover is non-blocking in the reconciled text, and the execution side records no shift.**
grok: *"Handover requires acknowledgment before the receiving shift may act under that change. Unacknowledged shift end is not a handover."* local-evidence weakens this to *"Missing acknowledgment is a gap."* `ChangeExecution` has `operatorRefs` in `optional`, no `actingShiftRef`, and no `handoverRefs`, so in the acceptance scenario (*"During E2, duty passes from A to B with explicit acknowledgment."*) the acting party across the handover instant is unrecoverable from the execution record.

**D18 — The privileged-access invariant has no carrier.**
*"Privileged on-call access requires a separate time-bounded authorized audited grant."* / *"Any privileged access for on-call work is a separate time-bounded authorized and audited grant with expiry."* Both roots `exclude` IAM grant identity, `operatorRefs` is optional and carries no grant reference, and no field records expiry or audit. The invariant is stated where it cannot be evaluated. (The absent access-grant base is a hold; the unanchored invariant and the missing `operatorRefs` grant slot are the defects.)

**D19 — Roster→escalation reference direction is inverted, the WM-ACT-008 roster profile is undeclared, and the escalation target may be a frozen shift.**
`DutyRoster.references` includes WM-KNW-012 *"Escalation rule"* and WM-KNW-013 *"Escalation condition"* while `excludes` lists *"incident, escalation or change-execution identity"* — the resolver is made to depend on the rule that resolves through it. The same list has WM-ACT-008 *"Schedule profile"*, a fourth WM-ACT-008 profile that `profile-candidate.json` never defines (it names only *"Change Window, Freeze Period and Rollback Plan"*). grok also permits *"a roster role or shift as the notify target"* and *"Escalation names S-US"*; naming a shift occurrence pins the target at authoring time and bypasses as-of-event-time resolution.

**D20 — Latent authorization: no effectivity end and no closure precondition.**
*"One authorization may govern multiple executions without rewriting the approved revision."* plus *"the unused US window remains bound"* and no effectivity end, no bar on executing after request disposition or closure, and no closure precondition asserting that no latent or in-flight execution remains. local-evidence assigns WM-ACT-032 *"closure references"* while grok holds *"Partial failure does not auto-close the request or the decision."* — the reconciled artifact specifies neither direction, leaving an indefinitely executable approval.

**D21 — Every negative fixture asserts a rejection no artifact mechanism can produce.**
`window-is-authorization`, `rollback-is-restored`, `release-is-deployment`, `roster-grants-admin`, `silence-is-ack`, `assignment-is-shift` all expect a bare *"The inference is rejected."* with no rejection code, no rule reference and no typed field to reject against; `validation-policy.json` requires only `"requiresPositiveAndNegativeFixtures":true`. The negative suite is currently decorative.

## 3. Required bounded fixes

1. **Emergency path (D1, D20).** Make `approvedRequestRevisionRef` conditionally required: required unless `authorizationKind = emergency`. Add required-on-emergency `relaxedControls`, `relaxationReason`, `relaxationAuthorityRef`, `retrospectiveDueAt`, and `retrospectiveDecisionRef`; add lifecycle state `pending-retrospective-authorization` and bar `closed` while it is open.
2. **Authorization pin (D2, D3, D11, D20).** Add required `conditionsRef`, `effectivityFrom`, `effectivityTo`. Give every reference an explicit `{model, id, revision, digest}` shape and a declared target-model whitelist; add WM-ACT-024, WM-KNW-010, WM-REC-010 (and the verification base once published) to `bases` and to `ChangeExecution.references`; constrain `authorityRef` to the decision triad only. Add invariants: execution start within effectivity; no execution after recorded closure; closure asserts no latent or in-flight execution.
3. **Rollback (D4, D5).** Remove `rollback-started` and `rolled-back` from the lifecycle. Add required `kind ∈ {normal, standard, emergency, rollback, retry, continuation}` and directed `revertsExecutionRef` (required when `kind = rollback`). Add `restoredStateAsserted` gated on non-empty `verificationRefs`, and declare the verification target model (hold until published).
4. **Lifecycle/field coherence (D6, D7).** `startedAt` required for all states except `scheduled`; `endedAt` required in every terminal state; `perTargetOutcomes` required in `partially-completed`, `completed` and `failed`, with a coverage rule against `targetScope`.
5. **Freeze and windows (D8, D9).** Add `boundWindowRefs[]` (set, each with zone + tzdb pin) on the WM-ACT-032 profile and `executedWindowRef` on `ChangeExecution`. Add required `intervalRole ∈ {window, freeze, rollback-plan}` to the WM-ACT-008 profile. Add invariants: execution start inside a `freeze` interval requires a recorded derogation (`grantingAuthorityRef`, bounded validity, compensating obligation); a freeze interval is never a bindable executable window. Correct the reconciled scenario to carry grok's US freeze, or state explicitly that W-US is a window and that grok's freeze case is separately covered.
6. **Profile specification (D10, D21).** Replace the prose-only `profile-candidate.json` with object and field definitions for Operational Change, Change Window, Freeze Period, Rollback Plan, Escalation Rule and condition — including the attributes local-evidence already claims (recurrence, exclusions, overrides, `disclosureClass`, `timezoneId`, `tzdbRelease`, `version`, `dstPolicy`) — plus a `fixtures.json` and a `validation-policy.json` for the profile, and an `expectedCode` on every negative case across all three suites.
7. **Deployment relation (D12).** Replace *"Software deployment specialization"* with association: `causedDeploymentRefs` on `ChangeExecution`, purpose *"Software deployment caused by this execution"*. State explicitly that no existing registry model is re-parented under an unallocated root.
8. **Duty Roster objects (D13, D16, D17).** Define `RosterShift{rosterShiftId; rosterRevisionRef, standingAssignmentRef, serviceScope, startAt, endAt, timezoneId}`, `ShiftOverride{overrideId; supersededShiftRef, substituteRef, substitutedForRef, authorizerRef, effectiveAt, kind}` as an overlay outside the revision digest, and `DutyHandover{handoverId; outgoingShiftRef, incomingShiftRef, atInstant, openIncidentRefs, inFlightExecutionRefs, acknowledgment{by, atInstant} | gapRecorded}`. Remove `amended`/`superseded`/`successorRef` from the root lifecycle (supersession is revision-level); require `retiredAt` in `retired` and require that retirement enumerate uncovered open work. Add `actingShiftRef[]` with intervals and `handoverRefs` on `ChangeExecution`, and the rule that an incoming party may not act under an in-flight execution before acknowledgment.
9. **Time and provenance (D14, D15).** Declare the instant type once (RFC 3339, seconds, explicit offset). Add `assertedAt` (and `observedAt` where distinct) to both roots as the ordering key for `supersedesRef`. Require non-overlapping `[validFrom, validTo)` per roster, add required `dstPolicy` per window and per roster revision, and specify the `contentDigest` algorithm and canonicalization.
10. **Escalation and access (D18, D19).** Remove WM-KNW-012/013 from `DutyRoster.references` (keep the dependency one-way: rule → roster); add the WM-ACT-008 roster-schedule profile to the profile candidate or drop the reference. Constrain the escalation target to a role/service plus a roster reference, never a shift occurrence. Add optional `accessGrantRefs` with expiry on `operatorRefs` entries, restate the privileged-access invariant as a prohibition the artifacts can enforce (no permission attribute on roster, shift, override or handover), and record a gap when a privileged action has no grant reference.

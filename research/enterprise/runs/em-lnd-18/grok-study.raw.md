**Verdict.** ACCEPT-WITH-GAPS both candidates. EnterpriseLandscape is an identifier-unassigned selective read-only declaration with its own revision lifecycle, not a master. EnterpriseViewScope is accepted only as an immutable WM-KNW-012 expression/release profile, not a new type. Outputs are non-reimportable artifacts. Every fact stays in its domain master. WM-XCT-039 is rejected as the universal host and allowed only as an optional managed-IT projection when that is the named question. No identifier is allocated. This review does not claim publication readiness and does not close host/policy gaps.

**Strongest evidence.** Seventeen completed domain-landscape syntheses converge on selective, read-only declarations and projections. Adjacent complete drafts (graph hosting, dependency edges, organizations, inter-organizational relationships, policy/rules) supply topology and constraints without a managed-IT container. Named-question entry, facts-in-master, and non-reimport prevent a second master.

**Strongest counterexample.** A holding-group board pack and a subsidiary legal-hold file share the label “matter”; merging nodes or dropping axis or withheld marks breaks no-collapse and withholding together. A three-person firm may treat the latest declaration revision as the working ledger. If WM-KNW-012 cannot carry perimeter, withheld mark, axis reading, and non-reimport flag, the profile silently omits required marks or forces a forbidden sibling type.

**Identity/mastership.** The landscape is a declaration, not a master, and receives no allocated identifier. Revisions version question, selection, and perimeter, not domain facts. Pointers do not clone. Projection does not transfer mastership or mint competing identity. Shared labels are not shared identity. Withholding is not an identity event.

**Declaration/scope.** Landscape is bound to named management questions and decisions. View Scope is an immutable WM-KNW-012 expression/release of that declaration for an audience and moment. The declaration may revise; a released scope may not. Scope states perimeter, selection, withheld marks, and axis-local readings. Outputs may be cited, not loaded back as masters.

**Selection.** A domain enters only when a named question or decision requires it. Selection is question-scoped, not registry-scoped. Neither enterprise installs all registry domains or copies data. A withheld domain is an explicit mark on the perimeter. Re-selection is a new revision or release, not in-place mutation of a released scope.

**Transitions.** A context transition is a change of question, perimeter, or audience, not a data merge. Mastership, rights, provenance, time, and uncertainty must survive every transition or the fact does not enter. Projection may filter or frame; it may not rewrite a domain fact.

**Semantic conflicts.** Resolve per axis; never collapse shared labels. Local reading lives on the view; masters keep their own senses. Firm homonyms and group-versus-subsidiary senses remain two readings.

**Completeness.** Relative to an explicit perimeter bound to the named question. Not-selected domains are out of scope, not missing. A withheld domain is complete-as-declared if the mark is present.

**Access.** Membership grants no access. Rights stay with the domain master and are re-evaluated at each view release. Membership must not leak withheld content or existence-beyond-mark. Landscape operations are read-only.

**Time/provenance.** Three clocks stay unfused: domain valid-time and uncertainty on the master; declaration-time on the revision; release-time on the View Scope cut. Projected-fact provenance is domain provenance plus declaration/release citation. Revising the landscape does not rewrite past releases.

**Host boundary.** WM-XCT-039 is managed-IT-specific and is not the landscape container. Residual use is an optional projection target only when the named question is managed-IT. A three-person firm must declare a landscape with no managed-IT host. Adjacent drafts do not absorb landscape mastership or become the fact store. The declaration host is unnamed here — a gap, not a default to WM-XCT-039 or to the graph-hosting draft.

**Governance.** Two ledgers: domain mastership and rights; who may revise the unassigned declaration and mint a scope release. Policy/rules may constrain selection and release; they do not move facts. Cite unassigned revisions by content, not by allocating a code. Withheld existence-mark without content disclosure is required.

**Scenario.** Three-person service firm: named questions only (who may bind the firm; open engagements; cash); pointers to existing masters; no registry install or copy. Withheld domain: partner personal tax, marked on the perimeter; membership grants no tax access. Homonyms stay axis-local. Ledger-creep is blocked by non-reimport and master retention, not by an allocated identifier.

Holding group: named questions of capital allocation, control, and group risk. Organization and inter-org drafts supply edges; subsidiary ledgers are not copied. Unselected operational domains stay out. Withheld domain: JV pricing or subsidiary litigation — mark only. Board, regulatory, and opco packs are separate immutable releases. Statutory versus management revenue and legal versus management unit keep both masters. Neither enterprise uses WM-XCT-039 as container.

**Invariants.** (1) No identifier allocated in this review. (2) Facts mastered only in domain masters. (3) Landscape and view are read-only declarations and projections. (4) Outputs are not reimportable as masters. (5) Domains enter only for a named question or decision. (6) Mastership, rights, provenance, time, and uncertainty survive each transition or the fact does not enter. (7) Conflicts resolve per axis; shared labels never collapse. (8) Completeness is perimeter-relative; not-selected is not missing. (9) Membership grants no access. (10) View Scope releases are immutable; change requires a new revision or release. (11) WM-XCT-039 is not the universal host. (12) Withholding is an explicit mark, not silence or deletion.

**Minimum model set** (role names, not identifiers). Landscape declaration; declaration revision; View Scope as WM-KNW-012 expression/release; named question or decision; selection; explicit perimeter; domain pointer; semantic axis; withheld mark; mastership, rights, provenance, time, and uncertainty stamps; access decision distinct from membership; non-reimportable artifact; host binding.

**Blockers.** Fact copy-out; label collapse; membership treated as access; WM-XCT-039 as universal host or container; mutation of a released View Scope; reimport of artifacts as masters; allocating an identifier in this review; a WM-KNW-012 profile that cannot carry perimeter, withheld mark, axis reading, and non-reimport flag.

**Host/policy gaps** (named, not closed). Non-IT host unnamed; do not default it to graph hosting. Citation of unassigned revisions unspecified and must not be solved by allocating an ID. WM-KNW-012 field-level fitness unverified. Adjacent policy/rules draft does not specify withheld existence-versus-content rights. The declaration revision log must not become a membership master.

Host: WM-XCT-039 rejected as universal; residual IT-question projection only; non-IT host unnamed. Policy: adjacent draft does not close withheld-rights language.

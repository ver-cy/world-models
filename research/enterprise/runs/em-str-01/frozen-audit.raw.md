# EM-STR-01 — Frozen Semantic Audit (final, no-tools, single pass)

## 1. Verdict on decision and artifacts

**Decision: ACCEPTED as settled.** The four-root split with a non-authoritative Strategic Scenario is correct and survives both acceptance cases in the admitted evidence. Strategy (choice), Business Model (mechanism) and Value Stream (recipient value-state) have distinct masters, distinct change drivers and distinct review triggers; the channel-shift case changes mechanism and possibly stages while leaving the choice set standing, and the segment-exit case retires a mechanism and a stream while restating the choice set only if the market was a named choice. No merge of any pair reproduces that behaviour. Strategic Scenario's independent identity is justified by multi-strategy citation and branch-without-bump reuse; its non-authoritative profile is justified because a versionable hypothesis space that could master facts is a laundering channel. Strategic Theme and Value Proposition correctly have revisions but no authority, mandate or independent lifecycle, and are therefore contained, not roots. Reuse of WM-KNW-011, WM-ACT-030, WM-KNW-010, WM-ACT-024 and WM-REC-010 by reference is correct; Capability (WM-ACT-001) and Process/Workflow (WM-ACT-003) correctly remain legacy-only completion dependencies with declared, unenforceable edges; Outcome and Benefit Realization correctly remains a separate identifier-unassigned candidate.

**Artifacts: ACCEPTED WITH MATERIAL DEFECTS — not conformant as submitted.** The four allocation candidates correctly hold `modelId: null`, `registryId: null`, `allocationState: "unassigned"`, `canonicalPublishable: false` and non-empty holds, and each meets its stated minima. However the artifact set **violates the audited boundary in a way the artifacts' own validation policies cannot detect**: five referent identifiers appear that are outside the admitted reuse set, one of them pre-empting the EM-STR-02 allocation; the three decision referents are collapsed into one field; the contained records have no identity or schema, so the containment invariants are unenforceable; the Scenario non-authoritative profile exists only as prose invariants with one textual loophole; and the prior fixture set is non-conformant to the required fixture shape. These are remediable deterministically without reopening the boundary.

**No identifier is allocated. `boundary_decision` remains pending registry allocation. No crosswalk, mastership confirmation, installability or publication-readiness claim is made or implied by this audit.**

**Adjudicated disagreement:** Grok's invariant 2 admits an "explicit none-considered rationale" as an alternative to an attached rejected alternative. The initial study (invariant 1), the local synthesis (invariant 1) and the settled boundary ("Strategy owns choices, rejected alternatives…") all require an actual rejected alternative. The study's stated reason is decisive on the admitted evidence: a choice with no excluded option is indistinguishable from a project list, which is exactly the negative case both Grok and the study reject. **Grok's escape hatch is rejected**; `excludedChoices` stays required and non-empty. All other Grok positions are consistent with the settled boundary and are admitted.

## 2. Material defects and exact deterministic remediation

**D1 — Unadmitted referent identifiers in four artifacts.**
`WM-ORG-001`, `WM-ECO-021`, `WM-ECO-002` (business-model), `WM-MAT-008` (value-stream, strategic-scenario) and `WM-KNW-007` (strategic-scenario) are outside the admitted reuse set `{WM-KNW-011, WM-ACT-030, WM-KNW-010, WM-ACT-024, WM-REC-010}` and the legacy-only set `{WM-ACT-001, WM-ACT-003}`. The study itself classes party, offer/product and metric as declared-unallocated. These are unverified identifiers asserted as reference targets.
**Remediation:** in every `boundary.references` entry whose `target` is not in the admitted or legacy sets, replace the entry with exactly `{"target":null,"targetState":"declared-unallocated","purpose":"<existing purpose text>","enforceable":false}`. Do not substitute any other identifier. Applies to 5 entries: business-model (3), value-stream (1), strategic-scenario (2 — see also D8).

**D2 — Strategy omits WM-ACT-024 and collapses three decision referents into one field.**
`boundary.references` lists WM-KNW-011, WM-ACT-030, WM-KNW-010, WM-REC-010 but not WM-ACT-024, while `StrategyRelease.required` carries a single `decisionRef`. The settled boundary requires the approval occurrence, the rationale and the issued expression to be three distinct referents; one field cannot carry authority basis, rationale and finality without restating one inside another.
**Remediation:** add `{"target":"WM-ACT-024","purpose":"Approval occurrence"}` to Strategy `boundary.references`. Replace `"decisionRef"` in `StrategyRelease.required` with exactly `"rationaleRef","approvalActivityRef","issuedRecordRef"`, bound respectively to WM-KNW-010, WM-ACT-024, WM-REC-010.

**D3 — Business Model omits the rationale and approval-occurrence referents; Scenario reference purposes do not bar authority transfer.**
Business Model references WM-REC-010 only ("Authorizing decision"), so a mechanism successor can be released with a fixed expression and no recorded rationale or approval occurrence. Strategic Scenario references WM-REC-010 as "Approval decision" with no statement that approval publishes a hypothesis space rather than authorizing its content.
**Remediation:** add `{"target":"WM-KNW-010","purpose":"Decision rationale"}` and `{"target":"WM-ACT-024","purpose":"Approval occurrence"}` to Business Model `boundary.references`. In strategic-scenario, change the WM-REC-010 purpose string to exactly `"Publication of hypothesis space; confers no source-of-record status"` and add `"authorityTransfer":false` to that entry.

**D4 — Profile candidate is mislabelled and unvalidated.**
`profile-candidate.json` declares `"decision":"PROFILE"` over five bases while every listed constraint constrains the *new* candidates, not the bases. A profile with no base-level constraint is a reuse-by-reference, and mislabelling it asserts a restriction on bases that are themselves non-canonical drafts (WM-KNW-011/010/024 Claude-only under owner waiver, WM-ACT-030 Codex-only). The artifact also has no validation policy and no fixtures.
**Remediation:** set `"decision":"REUSE_BY_REFERENCE"`, rename `"bases"` to `"referencedBases"`, add `"baseLevelConstraints":[]`, add `"baseSpecificationState":"non-canonical-draft"`. Add `candidate-allocation-offline-strategy/profile-validation-policy.json` with `{"format":"vercy-allocation-validation/v1","requirements":{"newRuntimeIdMustBeFalse":true,"referencedBasesMustBeAdmitted":true,"baseLevelConstraintsMustBeEmptyUnlessDecisionIsProfile":true,"requiresConstraintAttribution":true}}`.

**D5 — Strategic Scenario non-authoritativeness is prose-only; lifecycle states can imply authority.**
The non-authoritative profile is the single highest integrity risk in the contour (Grok's own blocker list says so) and is held only by invariants. `"approved"`, `"published"` and `"active"` are indistinguishable from authoritative-release states to any consumer.
**Remediation:** add to the strategic-scenario `identityTest` object exactly `"authoritativeness":{"sourceOfRecord":false,"mayMasterActuals":false,"mayMasterBaselines":false,"mayMasterOutcomes":false,"mayMasterBenefits":false,"authorityTransferOnCitation":false}`. Append the invariant `"Lifecycle state never confers source-of-record status."` Add to `validation-policy.json` for this candidate `"requiresNonAuthoritativeBlock":true`.

**D6 — "silently" loophole in a Scenario invariant.**
`"Scenario output never becomes target baseline silently."` admits an explicit, documented promotion path. The settled boundary bans it absolutely: the scenario "never masters actuals, baselines, outcomes or benefits".
**Remediation:** replace that invariant string with exactly `"Scenario output never becomes a target baseline under any promotion path, documented or otherwise."`

**D7 — Value Stream claims ownership of an unenforceable binding and flags legacy-only references only in prose.**
`owns` contains `"realization bindings"` and `references` lists WM-ACT-003 and WM-ACT-001 with no machine-readable state, while the boundary holds both as legacy-only completion dependencies. Ownership of an edge that cannot be enforced invites silent promotion.
**Remediation:** change the `owns` entry `"realization bindings"` to exactly `"declared realization binding assertions (non-enforceable)"`. Add to both the WM-ACT-003 and WM-ACT-001 reference entries exactly `"specificationState":"legacy-only","enforceable":false,"boundaryReview":"migration-boundary-review"`.

**D8 — Value Stream pre-empts the EM-STR-02 allocation.**
`{"target":"WM-MAT-008","purpose":"Outcome observation"}` asserts an outcome-observation master while Outcome and Benefit Realization is a separate identifier-unassigned candidate. The same target appears in strategic-scenario as "Observation comparison".
**Remediation:** replace both entries with exactly `{"target":null,"targetState":"identifier-unassigned","purpose":"Outcome and Benefit Realization (EM-STR-02)","enforceable":false}`. Add to both candidates the invariant `"The Outcome and Benefit Realization referent is unallocated and is never silently promoted."`

**D9 — Contained records have no identity and no schema; containment invariants are unenforceable.**
`StrategyRelease.themes`, `BusinessModelRelease.valuePropositions` and `ValueStreamVersion.stages` are bare fields. Nothing enforces "a theme without a containing Strategy is invalid", "a Value Proposition is contained by exactly one Business Model at a time", or the Value Stream invariant "entry and exit criteria are explicit".
**Remediation:** add to strategy `objects` exactly `"StrategicTheme":{"identity":["strategyId","version","themeKey"],"containedBy":"StrategyRelease","required":["intent","choiceRefs"],"optional":["alignmentAnchor"],"independentLifecycle":false}`. Add to business-model `objects` exactly `"ValueProposition":{"identity":["businessModelId","version","propositionKey"],"containedBy":"BusinessModelRelease","required":["segmentRef","channelRef","benefitClaim"],"optional":["jobRef","painRef","gainRef","alternativeDisplaced"],"independentLifecycle":false,"maxContainers":1}`. Add to value-stream `objects` exactly `"Stage":{"identity":["valueStreamId","version","stageKey"],"containedBy":"ValueStreamVersion","required":["recipientValueState","entryCriteria","exitCriteria","sequenceIndex"],"optional":["declaredRealizationRefs"],"independentLifecycle":false}`. Move `themes` from `optional` to `required` in `StrategyRelease` only if at least one theme exists; otherwise leave optional and add the invariant `"A theme exists only within a Strategy release."`

**D10 — Cross-boundary invariant misplacement and incomplete exclusion lists.**
Strategy carries `"Forecast never becomes observed outcome by relabelling."` although Strategy owns neither forecasts nor outcomes (forecasts stay inside Scenario; Strategy excludes outcomes and benefits). Each `excludes` list is also under-specified against the settled boundary.
**Remediation:** delete that string from the Strategy invariants (it is already present in strategic-scenario) and add to Strategy exactly `"Strategy never records a forecast, an observed outcome or a realized benefit."` Then append to `boundary.excludes`: strategy — `"benefit realization","forecast","scenario projection","value mechanism","recipient value-state stage"`; business-model — `"strategy choice set","recipient value-state stage sequence","strategic scenario","observed outcome","benefit realization"`; value-stream — `"value mechanism","strategy choice set","strategic scenario"`; strategic-scenario — `"value mechanism","recipient value-state stage","initiative","strategic theme","value proposition"`.

**D11 — Alignment basis is asserted as an invariant with no field to carry it and no forbidden-basis set.**
Strategy invariant `"Alignment requires attributable basis."` has no corresponding structure, so membership, tag or budget line satisfies it by default.
**Remediation:** add to `StrategyRelease.optional` exactly `"alignmentAssertions"`, and add to strategy `objects` exactly `"AlignmentAssertion":{"identity":["strategyId","version","assertionKey"],"required":["targetRef","basis","basisKind"],"basisKindEnum":["rationale","evidence","decision"],"forbiddenBasis":["tag","budgetLine","portfolioMembership","reportingLine"]}`.

**D12 — No version non-coupling rule between the roots.**
Grok lists this as an open blocker. Without an explicit rule, a Business Model successor can be read as requiring a Strategy successor, which collapses the change drivers the split exists to separate.
**Remediation:** add to all four candidates exactly `"versionCoupling":{"coupled":false,"linkedBy":["WM-ACT-024","WM-REC-010"],"sharedBaseline":false}` at the top level of `boundary`, and add the invariant `"A successor release of one root never compels a successor release of another; cross-root change is linked only by reference."`

**D13 — Business Model makes revenue and cost logic optional while claiming to own the value mechanism.**
`boundary.owns` includes "revenue and cost logic" and the mechanism is defined to include capture logic ("who pays, exchange logic, channel economics"), yet `revenueLogic` and `costLogic` are `optional`. A release can therefore claim mechanism mastership with no capture logic, which is exactly the organization/offer conflation the invariants forbid.
**Remediation:** move `"revenueLogic"` and `"costLogic"` from `BusinessModelRelease.optional` to `BusinessModelRelease.required`.

**D14 — Immutability is unenforceable: `contentDigest` has no algorithm, canonicalization or scope.**
All four candidates assert immutable releases and require `contentDigest`, but no digest specification exists, so "released versions are immutable" cannot be checked.
**Remediation:** add to each of the four candidates at the top level exactly `"digest":{"algorithm":"sha-256","canonicalization":"RFC 8785 JSON Canonicalization Scheme","scope":"the full release or version object with the contentDigest field removed"}`, and to each `validation-policy.json` `"requiresDigestSpecification":true,"requiresImmutabilityCheckOnReleasedStates":true`.

**D15 — The 16 prior fixtures are non-conformant and not linked to invariants.**
Every prior case carries only `id`, `kind`, `input`, `expect`. None carries `target`, `violates` or `closesDefect`, and no negative carries `expectedCode`, so no prior fixture can be mechanically attributed to an invariant or a failure code.
**Remediation:** migrate all 16 in place to the shape `{id, target, kind, input, expect, violates, closesDefect, expectedCode?}` by the following deterministic rule — `target` = the file's `candidateName`; `violates` = for negatives, the verbatim invariant string from the same candidate that the case breaches, and for positives the literal `"none (exercises: <verbatim invariant string>)"`; `closesDefect` = `"D0-baseline"`; `expectedCode` for the eight negatives = `project-list` → `EMSTR01-CHO-002`, `forecast-as-fact` → `EMSTR01-AUTH-002`, `organization-collapse` → `EMSTR01-BND-003`, `proposition-offer` → `EMSTR01-BND-004`, `process-collapse` → `EMSTR01-BND-002`, `completion-benefit` → `EMSTR01-AUTH-002`, `actual-feed` → `EMSTR01-AUTH-002`, `baseline-mutation` → `EMSTR01-AUTH-002`. Add `"fixtureShapeVersion":"vercy-enterprise-allocation-fixtures/v2"` to each fixtures file.

**D16 — The four validation policies are byte-identical and cannot detect D1, D5, D14 or D15.**
They check identifier nullity and minima only. Nothing constrains reference targets to the admitted set, requires `canonicalPublishable: false`, requires non-empty holds, or requires error codes on negatives — which is why the five unadmitted identifiers passed.
**Remediation:** add to all four `requirements` blocks exactly `"admittedReferenceTargets":["WM-KNW-011","WM-ACT-030","WM-KNW-010","WM-ACT-024","WM-REC-010","WM-ACT-001","WM-ACT-003"]`, `"unadmittedReferenceTargetsMustBeNull":true`, `"canonicalPublishableMustBeFalse":true`, `"requiresNonEmptyHolds":true`, `"negativeFixturesRequireExpectedCode":true`, `"minimumPositiveFixtures":4`, `"minimumNegativeFixtures":4`, `"forbidsPublicationReadinessClaim":true`, `"requiresContainedObjectIdentity":true`, `"requiresVersionCouplingStatement":true`.

**D17 — Divergence on the rejected-alternative requirement left unresolved in the artifacts.**
Grok's invariant 2 permits a "none-considered rationale" in place of a rejected alternative; the artifact requires `excludedChoices`. The artifacts record no adjudication, so a later reader may apply the weaker rule.
**Remediation:** keep `"excludedChoices"` in `StrategyRelease.required` and add the invariant `"excludedChoices is non-empty; a none-considered rationale does not satisfy this invariant."` Add to the strategy artifact a top-level `"adjudications":[{"source":"grok","item":"none-considered rationale as alternative to rejected alternatives","disposition":"rejected","basis":"indistinguishable from the rejected project-list case"}]`.

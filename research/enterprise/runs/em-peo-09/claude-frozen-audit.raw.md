Closed defect checklist.

1. **BLOCKING — Assignment lifecycle contradicts its own rules.** `independentLifecycle` opens with `proposed, reviewed`, but CA-07 and CA-08 place proposed terms and unapplied recommendations on the WM-REC-010 decision, not on the assignment. 2. **BLOCKING — Enrollment lifecycle flattens two state machines and admits an electionless enrollment.** BE-01 makes election constitutive of identity and BE-04 makes election and coverage separate owned components, yet one linear lifecycle mixes election states (`elected`, `waived`), a pre-election state (`offered`) and coverage states (`active`, `suspended`, `continued`). `offered` is a plan/eligibility fact and pre-empts BP-03. 3. **BLOCKING — Enrollment→payroll reference is inverted.** `benefit-enrollment.json` references WM-ECO-031 for "optional payroll deduction input only". Payroll reads enrollment; enrollment does not consume payroll. As written it plants a payroll dependency inside an aggregate whose BE-01/BE-05/BE-06 declare independence from payroll. Delete the reference and state the consumption direction in the profile.

4. **BLOCKING — No person/party master is cited.** BE-01 identifies an enrollment "by person" and CA-01 "per worker", but neither root references a party/worker master; Employment is optional (BE-03) or "and/or" (CA-05), so under continuation the person identity has no cited authority.

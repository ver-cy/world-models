Remaining research and host requirements

Authenticated source/agent attestations and connectors; independent-source proof; calibrated numeric confidence; sector/legal/forensic profiles and full parent holds; fine-grained disclosure and retention/erasure; durable concurrency, capacity/rollover and existing-Dimension transactional migration. This companion is a bounded trusted-host reference, not complete EM-XCT-03.

Authentication, the latest complete root/configuration, clock, serialized writes, generic writer-only receipts, whole-register access, native envelope checks and execution budgets belong to the trusted host. Recorded confidence is not a truth verdict.

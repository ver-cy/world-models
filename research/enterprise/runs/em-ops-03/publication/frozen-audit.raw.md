# EM-OPS-03 — Final Frozen Semantic Audit

Single audit. No tools used, nothing browsed, no identifier invented, no rerun requested. Admitted evidence: the initial Claude study, the local synthesis, the exact visible Grok response (text only; its hidden reasoning, team coordination and sources control are excluded and carry no evidentiary weight), and the ten appended candidate artifacts.

---

## 1. Verdict

**Decision (the settled boundary): ACCEPT, as stated, in full.** Every clause listed in the audit brief survives the three admitted inputs:

- Asset Instance profiling WM-OBJ-001 with no parallel physical identity is the only reading consistent with WM-OBJ-001's stated mastership of instance identifiers, marks, condition, whereabouts, custody and lifecycle. Both Claude and Grok converge here.
- Custody as an **effective-dated, independently identified relationship record inside the WM-OBJ-001 boundary, allocating no model or runtime identifier**, is the correct resolution of the one genuine disagreement in the record. Grok's objection ("Custody profiles nothing in WM-OBJ-001… requires an independent temporal relationship identity") is sound on *record identity* — a holder/interval/basis triple cannot be a property of the item — and wrong on *allocation*: nothing in the admitted evidence shows WM-OBJ-001 refusing to govern custody periods, and it expressly lists custody period, transfer, basis of holding and acknowledgement among its owned concerns. Independent record identity inside an existing boundary is not a new master. The settled clause states exactly this and is upheld.
- WM-ECO-011 as aligned holding view only is forced: the dossier holds no current spec and no crosswalk, and its legacy content is person-side. WM-OBJ-022 as projection only is stated by WM-OBJ-022 itself ("no second master identity").
- WM-ACT-007 authorizing without proving execution is self-declared in that spec and independently affirmed by Grok's counterexample.
- Maintenance Plan / Maintenance Event / Calibration Event as independent identifier-unassigned roots passes the identity test on all three: each has stable identity, version identity and a lifecycle that no frozen base supplies.
- Reuse of the EM-LND-17 responsibility candidate rather than a second candidate is correct and prevents a duplicate relation root.
- WM-OBJ-023 as reserved boundary and completion dependency — not an EM-OPS-03 allocation — is correct; Grok's contrary demand that it "requires an independent configuration identity, as a specialization" would allocate inside a reserved boundary on an unverified parent (WM-OBJ-008 is named but never supplied) and is rejected.
- The pinned identity-continuity rule, the prohibition on calibration transfer, the calibration content set, purpose-scoped derived fitness, and the refusal of class/model propagation are all upheld. The negative case stays rejected.
- No runtime or model identifier is allocated by this audit.

**Verdict (the artifacts): CONDITIONAL ACCEPT — NOT PUBLICATION-READY.** The ten artifacts are structurally sound candidate material and correctly carry `modelId: null`, `registryId: null`, `allocationState: "unassigned"`, `canonicalPublishable: false` on the three roots. They do **not** yet encode the settled boundary: Custody has no artifact at all, three reference identifiers appear in the Calibration Event candidate that exist nowhere in admitted evidence, the WM-OBJ-023 completion dependency is undeclared everywhere, several owned concepts have no fields, the calibration lifecycle contradicts its own separability invariant, and the fixture set is both schema-nonconformant and silent on seven settled clauses. Twenty material defects follow with exact remediation. No publication readiness, no canonical completeness and no installability is claimed or implied.

**D04 — WM-OBJ-023 completion dependency undeclared.** `CalibrationEvent.required` includes `configurationRef` while no artifact names the boundary that resolves it. An implementer closing the gap would allocate a configuration master inside a reserved boundary.

**D05 — Calibration Event owns concepts it has no fields for.** `boundary.owns` lists range and calibration points, traceability, decision rule and validity/invalidation conditions; `objects.CalibrationEvent` carries none of them, and `decisionRef`/`validUntil` are optional. *Remediation:* replace `objects.CalibrationEvent.required` with exactly `["instrumentRef","configurationRef","methodRef","methodVersion","referenceStandardRefs","traceabilityChain","calibratedRange","calibrationPoints","performedAt","results","resultUnitSystem","uncertainty","validityConditions"]` and `optional` with exactly `["decisionRule","conformityStatement","certificateRef","validFrom","validUntil","supersedesRef","impartialityDeclarationRef","competenceRef"]`; add the conditional rule `"conditionalRequirements":[{"when":"conformityStatement present","require":["decisionRule","uncertainty.coverageFactor","uncertainty.coverageProbability"]}]`.

**D06 — The calibration lifecycle contradicts its own separability invariant.** `independentLifecycle` is one linear axis mixing determination (`recorded`,`evaluated`), decision (`conforming`,`nonconforming`), attestation (`attested`) and validity (`expired`,`invalidated`), while invariant 5 requires determination, decision and attestation to remain separable.
*Remediation:* delete `independentLifecycle` from the Calibration Event candidate and replace with exactly:

**D07 — The pinned continuity rule has no field.** `Maintenance Event` invariants require asset continuity to follow a pinned local rule and forbid silent calibration transfer, but `objects.MaintenanceEvent` has no rule reference, so the existing negative fixtures `silent-continuity` and `copied-calibration` are not deterministically decidable.
*Remediation:* add `continuityRuleRef` and `continuityRuleVersion` to `optional`, plus `"conditionalRequirements":[{"when":"componentChanges present","require":["continuityRuleRef","continuityRuleVersion"]},{"when":"componentChanges affects a measuring subsystem","require":["calibrationInvalidationRef"]}]`; add the invariant `"A component change that affects a measuring subsystem invalidates dependent fitness and never carries a calibration event, certificate or validity to the successor configuration."`

**D08 — Order/event cardinality unstated.** Grok's invariants 8 and the separation of authorization from execution require at most one authorizing order per event and permit an order with zero events; `workOrderRef` is an unconstrained optional field.
*Remediation:* add `"cardinality":{"workOrderRef":{"min":0,"max":1}}` to `objects.MaintenanceEvent` and the invariants `"A maintenance event cites at most one authorizing work order."` and `"An authorizing work order may cite zero maintenance events, including after closure."`

**D09 — No non-substitution rule for evidence or certificate.** The artifacts state that measurements remain WM-MAT-008 facts and judgements remain WM-ACT-034 assessments, but never forbid them from *being* the event; the Calibration Event candidate omits the rule entirely and does not forbid the certificate standing in for the event.

# 1. Verdict on decision and artifacts

**Decision: ACCEPT, unchanged.** All three admitted lines of evidence converge, and the convergence survives independent reconstruction:

- **WM-ECO-034 reused for Sustainability Disclosure only** — accepted. A disclosure revision is a publication surface; it does not choose a consolidation approach, hold activity, version factors, or own an execution. WM-ECO-034's scope statement does contradict its own out-of-scope list (study hold, synthesis hold); that is a defect **in WM-ECO-034**, not a reason to re-master here, and the narrowing constraint in the profile candidate is the correct deterministic fix.
- **Activity Data profiled over WM-FLW-015 and WM-MAT-008; no Activity Data root** — accepted. WM-FLW-015 already self-declares consumption as "an activity datum for external factor, footprint or LCA calculations," which defeats a separate root on identity grounds, not merely on parsimony grounds.
- **Three independent identifier-unassigned roots** — accepted. Each passes the independent-lifecycle test on a distinct clock: boundary on consolidation change, factor set on publisher release, run on execution. Grok's reconstruction of this split is corroborating but adds nothing the study did not already establish from the bases.
- **Results run-contained; factor versions set-contained** — accepted in principle, **not representable in the artifacts as written** (defects D1, D5, D9).
- **Distinctions held** — organizational/operational/value-chain, location-based/market-based non-additivity, the five quality classes, cost-is-never-impact, no cross-counterparty netting: all accepted as settled semantics. **D2, D3, D5, D6, D7, D8, D14 show that several of these are asserted in prose and invariant lists but cannot be expressed, enforced, or falsified by the object schemas and fixtures supplied.**
- **No runtime or model identifier allocated** — accepted and verified: `modelId`, `registryId` are null and `allocationState` is `unassigned` in all three candidates; `newRuntimeId` is false in the profile. No identifier is allocated by this audit.

**Artifacts: REJECT as frozen-ready; ACCEPT as the correct basis.** The three allocation candidates, the profile candidate, the three fixture sets and the three validation policies are structurally sound and semantically aligned with the settled boundary, but they are not a sufficient artifact set. Fourteen material defects are listed below. Nine (D1, D2, D3, D4, D5, D6, D7, D9, D14) are schema defects that make a settled invariant unrepresentable or silently satisfiable; the remainder are declaration, contradiction or test-harness defects. The three validation policies are byte-identical and all three are passed by the current artifacts, which means the policies do not discriminate and cannot detect any of D1–D14.

No publication readiness, canonical completeness, installability, or relation approval is claimed or implied.

# 2. Material defects and exact deterministic remediation

### D1 — Factor sets do not exist; set containment is unrepresentable
The settled boundary requires "factor versions are contained by governed factor sets," and the study specifies FactorSet → Factor → FactorVersion. The Emission Factor Registry candidate has only `EmissionFactorRegistry` and `FactorVersion`. `FactorVersion.identity` is `["factorRegistryId","factorId","version"]` — registry-contained, not set-contained. `EmissionFactorRegistry.required.currentReleaseRef` points at an object type that is never defined.

**Remediation.** In the Emission Factor Registry candidate, add to `objects`:
`"FactorSet": {"identity":["factorRegistryId","factorSetId","setVersion"],"required":["releaseLabel","publisherRef","publishedAt","contentDigest","immutable"],"optional":["supersedesSetVersion","licenceRef","withdrawnAt"],"lifecycle":["draft","published","effective","superseded","withdrawn"]}`.
Change `FactorVersion.identity` to `["factorRegistryId","factorSetId","setVersion","factorId"]`. Define `currentReleaseRef` as a reference to one `FactorSet` identity triple. Add invariants: `"A factor version exists only inside one published factor set version."`, `"A published factor set version never gains, loses or alters a factor version."`, `"A calculation run pins factor set versions, never bare factor names."` Remove `version` as a standalone FactorVersion identity component so that factor history is carried by set version.

### D2 — The three boundary facets are collapsed into one revision, destroying independent versioning
The study requires "three separately versioned boundaries"; the settled boundary requires they "remain distinct." `BoundaryRevision` carries `consolidationApproach`, `operationalScopes` and `valueChainCoverage` as sibling fields under one `revision`, one `effectiveFrom` and one `contentDigest`. A value-chain coverage change therefore mints a new organizational and operational boundary identity. The invariant `"Organizational, operational and value-chain boundaries remain separate facets."` is unfalsifiable against this schema, and fixture `scope-collapse` passes trivially because nothing in the schema is a single undifferentiated scope field.

**Remediation.** In the Inventory Boundary candidate, replace the three fields with three contained facet objects, each independently versioned:
`"OrganizationalFacetRevision": {"identity":["inventoryBoundaryId","organizationalFacetRevision"],"required":["consolidationApproach","includedEntityRefs","effectiveFrom","contentDigest"],"optional":["effectiveTo","supersedesRevision"]}`,
`"OperationalFacetRevision": {"identity":["inventoryBoundaryId","operationalFacetRevision"],"required":["includedSourceRefs","scopeClassification","effectiveFrom","contentDigest"],"optional":["effectiveTo","supersedesRevision"]}`,
`"ValueChainFacetRevision": {"identity":["inventoryBoundaryId","valueChainFacetRevision"],"required":["categoryInclusion","coveragePercent","exclusions","effectiveFrom","contentDigest"],"optional":["effectiveTo","supersedesRevision"]}`.
`BoundaryRevision.required` becomes `["organizationalFacetRevisionRef","operationalFacetRevisionRef","valueChainFacetRevisionRef","recalculationPolicyRef","effectiveFrom","contentDigest"]`. Add invariant: `"A composite boundary revision pins exactly one revision of each facet, and a facet revision changes only when that facet changes."` Restrict `consolidationApproach` to the closed set `["equityShare","financialControl","operationalControl"]`.

### D3 — The activity quality-class enumeration differs in all four places it appears and is not closed
Four mutually inconsistent lists are in evidence: study — measured, metered, derived, allocated, estimated, modelled, spend-proxy (7); synthesis — measured, derived, allocated, estimated, modelled, spend-proxy (6); profile candidate constraint — the same 6; settled boundary — measured, estimated, modelled, allocated, spend-proxy (5); visible Grok — measured, estimated, spend-proxy (3, exclusive). No artifact declares the enumeration closed, and no artifact declares it exclusive. The settled boundary governs; Grok's three-term list is a strict subset and is subsumed, not authoritative.

**Remediation.** In the profile candidate, replace the quality-class constraint with: `"Every activity datum carries exactly one qualityClass from the closed set [\"measured\",\"estimated\",\"modelled\",\"allocated\",\"spendProxy\"]."` Add a second, non-exclusive, optional field `acquisitionMethod` with closed set `["metered","sensor","invoice","manual","derived"]`, and the constraint `"acquisitionMethod refines qualityClass and never substitutes for it; \"derived\" and \"metered\" are acquisition facts, not quality classes."` Propagate the identical five-term closed set, verbatim, into Inventory Boundary invariants, Environmental Impact Calculation Run invariant 3 (currently "Measured, derived, allocated, estimated and proxy inputs remain distinct"), and the three fixture sets. Add constraint: `"A new Activity Data root is forbidden; the profile allocates no identifier."`

### D4 — `characterizationSetRef` and `methodEditionRef` are required but have no declared target
`ImpactCalculationRun.required` includes both. The characterization set is explicitly excluded by the Emission Factor Registry and by the Inventory Boundary, is listed as an open hold in all three candidates, and has no allocated home — so a required field points at nothing. `methodEditionRef` is unexplained: the study names WM-ACT-009 for the method edition and WM-ACT-053 for generic execution, but neither appears in the run candidate's `references`. The settled boundary forbids allocating a new identifier, so the gap must be declared, not filled.

**Remediation.** In the Environmental Impact Calculation Run candidate add `"unallocatedDependencies":[{"localName":"GWPCharacterizationSet","requiredBy":["ImpactCalculationRun.characterizationSetRef"],"allocationState":"unassigned","outOfContour":true,"blocksRun":true},{"localName":"MarketInstrumentRetirement","requiredBy":["ImpactResult.marketInstrumentRetirementRef"],"allocationState":"unassigned","outOfContour":true,"blocksRun":true},{"localName":"AssuranceEngagement","requiredBy":[],"allocationState":"unassigned","outOfContour":true,"blocksRun":false}]`. Add to `boundary.references`: `{"target":"WM-ACT-009","purpose":"Pinned calculation method edition"}` and `{"target":"WM-ACT-053","purpose":"Generic execution semantics"}`, and `{"target":"WM-KNW-012","purpose":"Allocation rule and recalculation policy statements"}`. Add to the Inventory Boundary `references`: `{"target":"WM-KNW-012","purpose":"Recalculation policy statement"}` and `{"target":"WM-KNW-013","purpose":"Significance threshold"}`, since `recalculationPolicyRef` is required with no referenced master. Add invariant: `"A required reference whose master is unallocated is declared in unallocatedDependencies and blocks the run; no identifier is invented to satisfy it."`

### D5 — Non-additivity, non-comparability and the reconciliation bridge are asserted everywhere and representable nowhere
The invariant `"Location- and market-based results are non-additive."` appears in the run candidate; `"Boundary bridge distinguishes boundary, method, factor and activity effects."` and `"Original and recalculated results remain resolvable."` appear in the Inventory Boundary. The acceptance scenario in all three documents turns on the bridge. No object has a non-additivity marker, a comparability flag, a baseline reference, or a bridge. Nothing stops a consumer summing R1 and R2.

**Remediation.** Add to `ImpactCalculationRun.required` the field `comparability`, an object with required `additiveWithin` (closed set `["none","sameRunSameMethod"]`), required `nonAdditiveWithRunRefs` (array, may be empty), required `comparableWithRunRefs` (array, may be empty). Add a contained object:
`"ComparabilityBridge": {"identity":["calculationRunId","bridgeId"],"required":["baselineRunRef","effects","residualUnexplained"],"optional":["notes"]}` where `effects` is an array of `{"effectKind","quantity","unit"}` and `effectKind` is the closed set `["boundary","method","factor","activity"]`. Add invariants: `"Results whose runs differ in method, boundary revision or factor set version are never summed or averaged."`, `"A run that changes boundary revision, method or factor set version relative to a cited baseline carries one ComparabilityBridge whose effects plus residualUnexplained reconcile the difference exactly."`

### D6 — Market-based method has no required evidence binding
Run invariant `"Market claims cite unique retirement evidence."` has no corresponding field. `ImpactResult.required.method` is a bare value; there is no retirement reference, no residual-mix fallback reference, and no uniqueness scope for a claim.

**Remediation.** Restrict `ImpactResult.method` to the closed set `["locationBased","marketBased","processLca","spendProxy","supplierSpecific"]`. Add the conditional requirement: when `method == "marketBased"`, exactly one of `marketInstrumentRetirementRef` or `residualMixFactorVersionRef` is required and the other is forbidden; when `residualMixFactorVersionRef` is used, `limitationDisclosed` is required and true. Add invariant: `"One retirement or cancellation reference supports at most one market-based result across all runs and all organizations."` Declare `MarketInstrumentRetirement` in `unallocatedDependencies` per D4; do not allocate a root for it.

### D7 — The cost/impact separation has no schema enforcement
`"Financial cost never substitutes for impact quantity."` is invariant 12 of the synthesis, invariant 7 of the visible Grok response, and invariant 12 of the Emission Factor Registry — but it is absent from the run candidate's invariant list, and `ImpactResult.required` is `quantity` + `unit` with no quantity-kind restriction. A monetary amount in `quantity` with a currency in `unit` validates. Symmetrically, the spend-proxy basis (currency, currency year, deflator, EEIO factor) has no home on the activity profile.

**Remediation.** Add `quantityKind` to `ImpactResult.required`, restricted to the closed set `["massCo2e","massGas","energy","volume","mass","area","count"]`, and add the prohibition `"ImpactResult admits no monetary field; monetaryAmount, currency and cost are forbidden keys on ImpactResult."` In the profile candidate, require that a `spendProxy` activity datum carries a separate `proxyBasis` substructure with required `monetaryAmount`, `currency`, `currencyYear`, `deflatorRef`, `eeioFactorVersionRef`, and the constraint `"proxyBasis is never read as a physical quantity and never as an impact quantity."` Add the cost-separation invariant verbatim to the run candidate's invariant list.

### D8 — A counterparty's claimed share has no container, so netting is forbidden only in prose
The study is explicit that "a counterparty's claimed share is stored as an assertion, never as truth," and the settled boundary forbids netting supplier Scope 1 against buyer Scope 3. The only artifact mechanism is the invariant text plus fixture `cross-boundary-netting`. There is no field distinguishing an own-boundary fact from a counterparty assertion, so the shared-asset share-sum invariant (`≤ 1` within one consolidation approach) cannot be evaluated against anything.

**Remediation.** Add to `BoundaryRevision.optional` a `counterpartyAssertions` array with required `assertedByOrgRef`, `assertedShare`, `consolidationApproach`, `assertionStatus` from the closed set `["unverified","corroborated","disputed"]`, and `evidenceRef`. Add invariants: `"A counterparty assertion is never an input to a result quantity."`, `"Shared-asset shares held under one consolidation approach sum to at most 1.0, and any shortfall is exposed as a declared residual."`, `"Inventories held by different counterparties are never netted, eliminated or deduplicated against each other."` Add the last of these to the Inventory Boundary invariant list, where it is currently absent.

### D9 — Run lifecycle permits results outside `completed` and permits an unresolvable `retired` run
`ImpactCalculationRun.lifecycle` is `["planned","running","completed","failed","superseded","retired"]`, while `ImpactResult` is contained with no status predicate. Results may therefore exist on a `planned` or `running` run, which contradicts `"Runs are immutable and corrected by successors."` No transition set is declared. `retired` is undefined and, read plainly, would strand any disclosure that cites the run — contradicting `"Original and recalculated results remain resolvable."` and the Emission Factor Registry's analogous `"Withdrawn factors remain resolvable for historical runs."`

**Remediation.** Declare the exact transition set `planned→running`, `running→completed`, `running→failed`, `completed→superseded`, `superseded→retired`, `failed→retired`; all other transitions forbidden; no transition out of `retired`. Add invariants: `"ImpactResult instances exist only on a run whose status is completed, superseded or retired."`, `"A completed run is append-only: inputs, results and contained objects never change."`, `"retired closes a run to new citation and never makes it unresolvable; a run cited by any disclosure revision remains resolvable permanently."`, `"A failed run contains no results."`

### D10 — `baseYear` is optional but the base-year invariant is unconditional
`BoundaryRevision.optional` contains `baseYear`, while the Inventory Boundary invariant set asserts `"Base year is boundary-scoped and cites a recalculation policy."` and `"Structural change triggers recalculation and comparability review."` An effective boundary with no base year satisfies the schema and breaches the invariant.

**Remediation.** Move `baseYear` to required on `BoundaryRevision` and permit the explicit sentinel value `"notEstablished"`, allowed only while `InventoryBoundary.status` is `draft`. Add invariant: `"A boundary revision whose status is approved or effective carries an established base year and a recalculation policy reference; \"notEstablished\" is a draft-only sentinel and never reaches a calculation run."`

### D11 — Assurance mastership is unallocated and the "assurance cannot mutate a closed run" invariant is missing from every candidate
The study places assurance engagement identity outside the contour with no allocated home; Grok's visible invariant 14 is `"Assurance and filing cannot mutate a closed run."` That invariant appears in none of the three candidates. The profile candidate's constraint `"Approval, assurance, filing, publication and compliance remain separate states."` risks reading as if WM-ECO-034 owns assurance *engagement* identity, which it does not.

**Remediation.** In the profile candidate, amend the constraint to `"WM-ECO-034 owns only the disclosure-side approval, assurance, filing, publication and compliance states; assurance engagement identity, scope, level and conclusion are externally mastered, unallocated, and out of contour."` Add to the Environmental Impact Calculation Run invariants: `"Assurance and filing never mutate, re-open or re-state a closed run; they reference pinned run and result identities."` Declare `AssuranceEngagement` in `unallocatedDependencies` per D4. Allocate no root for it.

### D12 — WM-ORG-012 is referenced as if it carried approved consolidation semantics
The Inventory Boundary references `{"target":"WM-ORG-012","purpose":"Group and consolidation context"}` with no edge-state qualifier, while the study's own hold records WM-ORG-012 as Codex-only with proposed edges only, and Grok's blocker list names `"consolidation approach exists only as org hierarchy"` as a failure mode. As written, an implementer may resolve the consolidation approach by traversing WM-ORG-012.

**Remediation.** Qualify every reference entry with `edgeState`, valued `"unapproved"` for WM-ORG-012 and for any other target lacking an approved relation edge — which, per both the study and the synthesis, is currently all of them. Add invariant: `"consolidationApproach is a dated decision carried on the organizational facet revision and is never derived by traversing an organization or group master."` Add a hold: `"No approved relation edge exists for any reference in this candidate; all reference edges are declared unapproved."`

### D13 — Fixture format lacks four required keys and the three validation policies are identical and non-discriminating
The three fixture sets use `vercy-enterprise-allocation-fixtures/v1`, whose cases carry only `id`, `kind`, `input`, `expect`. All 21 lack `target`, `violates`, `closesDefect`; all 12 negatives lack `expectedCode`, so no negative is deterministically checkable — any rejection for any reason passes. The three validation policies are byte-identical, set `minimumFixtures: 3` against 7 actual, `minimumInvariants: 8` against 14 actual, and are all satisfied by the current artifacts, which is why none of D1–D14 is caught.

**Remediation.** Bump the fixture format to `vercy-enterprise-allocation-fixtures/v2` requiring exactly the key set `["id","target","kind","input","expect","violates","closesDefect"]` plus `expectedCode` on every `kind == "negative"`. Backfill all 21 prior cases with `target` set to the owning candidate name, `violates` set to the invariant text under test, `closesDefect` set to `null` (pre-existing coverage, not defect-closing), and an `expectedCode` on each of the 12 negatives drawn from the code vocabulary used in §3. The 21 prior ids are already globally distinct and are retained verbatim. Amend all three validation policies to add `"requiresNegativeExpectedCode": true`, `"requiresDefectLinkage": true`, `"forbidsRootsOutsideSettledSet": ["Inventory Boundary","Emission Factor Registry","Environmental Impact Calculation Run"]`, `"forbidsActivityDataRoot": true`, `"requiresClosedEnumerations": true`, `"requiresUnallocatedDependencyDeclaration": true`, `"requiresReferenceEdgeState": true`, `"minimumInvariants": 14`, and set `minimumFixtures` to the per-candidate post-remediation count rather than 3.

### D14 — Overloaded and open-valued enumerations survive inside the contour the rename was meant to disambiguate
The study's hold on the overloading of "impact" was resolved at the root level by the names *Inventory Boundary* and *Environmental Impact Calculation Run*, but the contained objects are still `ImpactCalculationRun` and `ImpactResult`, and `ImpactResult.required` includes the single conflated field `gasOrImpactKind`. A gas and an impact category are different axes: CH₄ characterized under AR6 GWP100 is one gas and one category, and the conflated field cannot express both or either unambiguously. Separately, `FactorVersion.methodClass`, `BoundaryRevision.consolidationApproach`, `ImpactResult.scope` and `ImpactResult.category` are all open-valued.

**Remediation.** Rename the contained objects to `EnvironmentalImpactCalculationRun` and `EnvironmentalImpactResult`, matching the settled root names. Replace `gasOrImpactKind` with required `impactCategory` from the closed set `["ghgEmissions","waterConsumption","wasteGenerated","landUse","acidification","eutrophication"]`, optional `gas` from the closed set `["co2","ch4","n2o","sf6","nf3","hfc","pfc","mixed"]`, and required `characterizationBasis` which is `"none"` when `gas` is absent and otherwise the pinned characterization set reference. Close the remaining enumerations: `FactorVersion.methodClass` to `["supplierSpecific","gridAverage","residualMix","eeioSpend","processLca","proxy"]`; `ImpactResult.scope` to `["scope1","scope2","scope3"]`; `ImpactResult.category` to the fifteen declared value-chain categories plus `"notApplicable"`. Add invariant: `"Every enumerated field is a closed set; an unrecognised value blocks the run rather than passing through."`

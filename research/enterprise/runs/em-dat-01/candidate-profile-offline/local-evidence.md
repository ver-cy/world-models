# EM-DAT-01 local evidence review

WM-DAT-001 remains authoritative for dataset, version and distribution identity. WM-DAT-004 remains authoritative for schema/contract versions, compatibility and producer-consumer obligations. EM-DAT-01 is a held profile that tightens their optional cross-model assertions and allocates no new identity.

The accepted scenario keeps one dataset identity, two immutable distribution identities and fixity records, one exact contract pin at each append-only conformance assertion time, and independent dataset/contract version clocks. A successor dataset preserves predecessor and provenance. Compatibility is evaluated against an explicit baseline set and covers structural, semantic, value-domain and declared content changes; the dataset breaking indication is derived solely from that outcome.

Catalogue records, data-product offerings, quality rules/runs and pipeline or deep-lineage graphs remain external masters. An ODCS dataProduct field is only a reference to the data-product authority. PID scheme, notice duration/channel, service levels, freshness, licence catalogue and designation policy remain deployment policy.

Grok independently selected PROFILE and rejected a fused root. The single Claude frozen audit confirmed the disposition and requested narrow repairs for immutable released bytes and conformance history, compatibility baseline/scope, NONE exceptions, exact fixity, and quality/product boundary statements. Candidate `0.1.0-candidate.3` implements those repairs without an audit rerun.

Canonical publication remains blocked because both bases are reviewable drafts with `publishableCanonical: false`; DataCite and ODCS pins, ISO/IEC 11179 clause evidence and multi-profile SHACL validation remain unresolved. A non-canonical reviewable candidate is appropriate.

# EM-LND-07 — Frozen semantic audit (final)

Evidence admitted: local synthesis, initial Claude study, exact Grok study (visible portion only — verdict, counterexample/scenario, thirteen invariants, minimum profile shape; the blockers paragraph is treated as ending at "WM-ACT-004 Service Definition" and is not reconstructed), profile candidate, existing fixtures. ## 1. Verdict

**Accept the decision; reject the artifacts as submitted.**

The adjudicated semantics are upheld and not reopened: Offering Portfolio Scope is a versioned governance/filter profile over the future Offering Catalogue; Offering Landscape is a non-authoritative projection; neither has independent business identity; no runtime/model identifier is allocated; `decision: "PROFILE"` and `newRuntimeId: false` are correct. All three evidence sources converge on this, and nothing in the submitted artifacts contradicts the reconciled decision — so no re-study is triggered.

The submitted artifacts are nevertheless **not freezable as written**. The profile candidate encodes 8 constraints against a reconciled 13-invariant surface, carries two base identifiers with no evidenced role and one duplicated obligation base, declares no enumerations or shape, asserts one unevidenced mechanism ("reproducible fingerprint"), and misstates the provider-review hold. The fixture set is machine-unverifiable: no negative carries an expected code, and the majority of the reconciled invariants have no case at all. Defects D1–D19 below are all mechanically closable from the admitted evidence; none requires new provider input.

## 2. Material defects in the submitted artifacts

Artifacts in scope: `profile candidate` (PC), `existing fixtures` (FX), `local synthesis` (LS). The Claude and Grok studies are evidence and are not audited.

**D1 — PC `bases` asserts one obligation base twice under two spellings.**
`bases` contains both `"WM-ACT-029"` and `"WM-XCT-029"`. Only `WM-XCT-029` is substantively evidenced (obligation mixin anchored on `WM-ECO-006`, discharge rule cited); `WM-ACT-029` appears only inside a prose holds enumeration. Two distinct bases are therefore asserted where the evidence supports one.
*Remediation:* delete the string `"WM-ACT-029"` from `bases`; retain `"WM-XCT-029"`. Append to `holds` exactly: `"The obligation mixin is carried as WM-XCT-029; the WM-ACT-029 spelling in prior prose is treated as a transcription variant and is not asserted as a separate base."` No identifier is created or re-allocated.

**D2 — PC `bases` lists two identifiers with no evidenced role.**
`"WM-SFT-001"` and `"WM-MAT-008"` appear in no part of the admitted evidence. LS names "measurement masters" and "product/service catalogues" generically but binds neither phrase to these identifiers, so the dependency is unverifiable from the frozen dossier.
*Remediation:* delete `"WM-SFT-001"` and `"WM-MAT-008"` from `bases`. Add object `"basisRoles"` mapping every retained base to exactly one evidenced role string drawn from the dossier (`WM-ECO-021` → quote/proposal; `WM-ECO-020` → order and delivered/outstanding accounting; `WM-ECO-022` → recurring economics and renewal; `WM-ECO-006` → contract; `WM-XCT-029` → obligation mixin; `WM-ACT-004` → service definition; `WM-ORG-014` → seller-scoped customer relationship). Append to `holds`: `"WM-SFT-001 and WM-MAT-008 adjacency is unevidenced in the frozen dossier and must be re-evidenced before reinstatement as a base."`

**D3 — PC constraint 2 calls unallocated subjects "masters".**
`"Product, Service, Offering, Quote and Entitlement remain distinct masters."` Offering Catalogue, Product Catalogue and Consumption Entitlement are unallocated candidates; the text asserts existing mastership they do not have.
*Remediation:* replace constraint 2 with: `"Product, Service, Offering, Quote and Entitlement remain distinct subjects, each on its own allocated master or, where unallocated, on its owning candidate master; none is a subtype of scope or landscape."`

**D4 — PC states no authorisation prohibition.**
Constraint 1 forbids mutation ("read-only projection") but not authorisation. Reconciled INV-04 forbids the landscape from authorising a quote, order, subscription, entitlement grant or retirement — a projection can be read-only and still be wrongly used as authority.
*Remediation:* insert constraint: `"Offering Landscape cannot authorise or evidence a quote, order, subscription, entitlement grant or retirement; it may only project state its source masters already hold."`

**D5 — PC states no citation rule.**
Grok INV-05 ("Decisions cite catalogue state plus scope version, not the landscape") has no counterpart in PC. Without it, the partner-exclusivity counterexample has no admissible resolution.
*Remediation:* insert constraint: `"Portfolio decisions and external citations reference catalogue state plus a frozen scope version; the landscape is never the cited source of authority, and an obligation that depends on an admitted set cites the frozen scope version and catalogue baseline."`

**D6 — PC omits the explicit-exclusion rule.**
PC constraint 3 covers derivation of membership only. Grok's boundary section requires exclusion, override and exception to be explicit predicate outcomes, never silent gaps; absence from output is otherwise indistinguishable from exclusion.
*Remediation:* insert constraint: `"Inclusion, exclusion, override and exception are explicit recorded predicate outcomes per evaluated candidate; a silent gap is never an exclusion."`

**D7 — PC constraint 5 is weaker than the adjudicated intent on value.**
`"Zero revenue never means zero value and missing price never means free"` states the negatives but omits both the positive requirement (typed non-revenue value is *required* for internal services) and the ranking prohibition (Grok: zero external revenue "must not be scored as such in landscape rankings").
*Remediation:* replace constraint 5 with: `"Zero revenue never means zero value and missing price never means free; every in-scope internal service carries at least one typed non-revenue value term with method and uncertainty; zero external revenue is never scored as zero value in any landscape ranking or ordering; cost-to-serve may be recorded but never substitutes for the typed value."`

**D8 — PC constraint 7 omits retirement record content.**
PC states only that retirement cancels nothing. The adjudicated intent, LS and Grok INV-11 additionally require the record's own content.
*Remediation:* insert constraint: `"Every offering retirement record states dated retirement states, support window, retention window, migration path and entitlement conversion, and either a successor reference or an explicit no-successor reason; a blank successor is invalid."`

**D9 — No comparability-verdict enumeration, and the artifacts carry two conflicting vocabularies.**
LS and the Claude study use `comparable | comparable-with-adjustment | not-comparable`; Grok INV-07 uses `comparable | partial | incomparable`. PC declares neither, yet fixture `mixed-value-classes` expects "comparability verdicts". An unenumerated verdict is unverifiable.
*Remediation:* add to PC `"enumerations": {"comparabilityVerdict": ["comparable","partial","incomparable"]}`; insert constraint: `"A comparability verdict is exactly one of comparable, partial or incomparable; partial requires a declared justified outcome class with class-specific criteria restated for that class and the adjustment stated; silence is not comparability; co-membership in a scope is never comparability."` In LS §"Time, scenario and acceptance", replace `"All are comparable only with declared adjustment"` with `"All are partial, with the declared adjustment recorded"`; in LS invariant 3 replace `"comparable-with-adjustment"` semantics with the `partial` label. No semantic reversal: Claude's "comparable-with-adjustment" maps onto Grok's `partial`, and the LS acceptance scenario (shared justified outcome class) remains admissible while Grok's cross-class price-per-unit case remains `incomparable`.

**D10 — No outcome-overlap claim-status enumeration, and the completeness rule is not stated.**
PC constraint 6 lists the four evidence classes but not the status vocabulary (LS: suspected/substantiated/refuted with method, evidence, contradictions, confidence) nor Grok INV-09's rule that missing any one evidence invalidates the claim.
*Remediation:* add `"enumerations".outcomeOverlapClaimStatus = ["suspected","substantiated","refuted"]`; replace constraint 6 with: `"A duplicate-outcome claim records status suspected, substantiated or refuted with method, evidence references, retained contradicting evidence and confidence; substantiation requires all four of semantic-equivalence, consumer-overlap, entitlement-overlap and substitution evidence, and missing any one caps the status at suspected; name similarity may open a review and is never evidence."`

**D11 — No retirement-state or value-type enumerations; value-type spelling is inconsistent across artifacts.**
LS fixes the dated state sequence and four non-revenue value types; the Claude study adds the two revenue types and spells them `realised-`, while LS spells `realized`. PC enumerates none, so fixtures cannot bind a value `type`.
*Remediation:* add `"enumerations".retirementState = ["announced","closed-to-new","closed-to-change","withdrawn"]` with each occurrence dated, and `"enumerations".valueType = ["realized-revenue","contracted-revenue","avoided-cost","capability-value","regulatory-necessity","strategic-option"]`; normalise all artifact prose to the `realized-` spelling.

**D12 — PC does not make catalogue existence a precondition of evaluation.**
Grok INV-13 and the visible blockers make the absent Offering/Product Catalogue allocation a blocker to operational use. PC records the absence only as a hold, so a scope evaluation or landscape release would pass the constraint set today.
*Remediation:* insert constraint: `"A scope version is evaluable only against an allocated, versioned Offering Catalogue baseline; while the catalogue candidates are unallocated, scope evaluation and landscape release are inadmissible and no evaluation result may be cited."` Append to `holds`: `"Offering Catalogue and Product Catalogue are unallocated, so the profile has no target master and no scope evaluation or landscape release is admissible; this blocks operational use and does not reopen the identity decision."`

**D13 — PC constraint 8 asserts an unevidenced mechanism and omits three evidenced elements.**
`"a reproducible fingerprint"` appears in no admitted evidence and reads as an implementation/conformance mechanism. The constraint also omits the source-as-of/computation-time distinction, in-place-rewrite prohibition and supersession addressability.
*Remediation:* replace constraint 8 with: `"Every released view pins scope version, scenario, horizon, source as-of and computation time, keeps source as-of distinct from computation time, and remains resolvable after supersession; a changed predicate, market scope or portfolio policy creates a successor scope version and never a rewrite in place; superseded scope and view versions remain addressable."`

**D14 — PC `holds` misstates the provider-review state.**
`"Independent Grok review, crosswalks, immutable pins and frozen audit remain pending"` is contradicted by the record: the provider study was returned (provider-truncated) and this is the frozen audit.
*Remediation:* replace that hold with: `"The independent provider study was returned and was provider-truncated inside its blockers paragraph after 'WM-ACT-004 Service Definition'; only the visible verdict, counterexample/scenario, thirteen invariants and minimum profile shape are admitted as evidence, and no missing provider text is reconstructed. This frozen audit is complete. Crosswalks, relationship contracts and immutable pins remain pending."`

*Remediation:* extend the LS shape paragraph with exactly those seven items, matching D15.

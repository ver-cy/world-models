## Required questions

Develop a defensible identity/lifecycle boundary between a versioned action definition, proposed intent, accepted immutable request, current authority decision, transport attempt, observed result/effect and underlying duty. Explicitly separate the acting identity, represented principal, issuer, executor/audience, accountable owner, observer and master system. Labels, model text and document instructions cannot grant permission. A named permission reference, asserted boolean or hash is not authenticated authority. Delegation must be bounded by action/resource/purpose/time/audience and must not widen the principal's effective authority. Decide the smallest useful delegation subset, and explicitly defer unsupported chains, impersonation, emergency powers and real credential verification when appropriate.

What exact immutable content does a retry key bind? Define key namespace, tenant/Dimension, executor, action definition/version, resource/version, actor/principal, purpose, ordered payload, expiration and authority freshness. Explain why a repeated request, a new attempt and a new effect are not the same identity. Changed content under a retained key must conflict. A retry cannot resurrect a proposal, bypass a changed policy or leak cached output. Distinguish execution authority from disclosure authority: even a cached result requires a fresh read check. Explain revocation and TOCTOU at the chosen transaction boundary, ambiguous failure, timeout, in-flight requests, cancellation, reconciliation and compensation. Do not claim globally exactly-once external effects from an idempotency table or local file lock. Retention/purge of deduplication data must not silently make an old key safe to reuse.

Propose a bounded executable acceptance design: a reversible LOCAL SYNTHETIC effect under an explicit trusted host, with no arbitrary command execution, network side effect or credential. Decide whether a deterministic pure decision/reference reducer plus a transactional local adapter is appropriate, or propose a simpler adequate increment. Distinguish the semantic contract, host authorization, storage/lock, effect transaction and emitted evidence. State precisely what users could install and use to describe/validate their organization's actions now, and what production deployment must supply. Rejected transitions cause no effect; proposals remain unexecuted. Unknown outcomes remain unknown until evidence reconciles them; corrections append history without undoing actions. Treat replay/order/digest and right-to-read checks as part of the contract, not optional prose.

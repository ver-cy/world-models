Review this Enterprise boundary independently using public organization, team, membership and access-control practice where useful. Separate standards evidence from design inference. Do not invent a Vercy identifier.

WM-ORG-003 Team and WM-ORG-006 Membership are existing non-canonical drafts. WM-ORG-003 contains team-scoped membership assignments; WM-ORG-006 owns governed belonging. WM-ORG-006 lacks independent external review.

Proposed decision: **PROFILE WM-ORG-003 and REUSE WM-ORG-006**, no new ID. WM-ORG-006 Membership has a scheme, admission decision/basis, standing, validity, terms, role and evidence; it may outlive an assignment.

Test: a community member must not gain access to products of other members. Acceptance: a temporary two-company team and a permanent community keep assignment, membership, employment and access independently dated.

## Decisions that must be explicit

1. Quantity kind, physical dimension, unit expression and magnitude are different. Equal dimensions do not prove compatible kinds.
2. Preserve asserted lexical decimal precision; a rational conversion factor is separate from measured uncertainty. Unknown/absent/masked do not become zero.
3. UnitReference includes system, definition revision and original expression. A code or glyph alone is insufficient. External tables remain governed evidence, not a new Vercy master.
4. Distinguish temperature point from difference. Point minus point may produce a difference; point plus difference may produce a point. The R1 Grok study's blanket prohibition on point-plus-delta is not accepted.
5. A unit conversion formula and its definition source must be independently pinned. State the exact arithmetic subset; nonlinear/logarithmic, arbitrary and procedure-dependent units require explicit refusal or a separately reviewed extension.
6. An affine conversion is not multiplication by a bare factor. Preserve original values, exact derivation and rounding policy in any retained result.
7. Field semantics remain with the host: measured physical quantity, contractual billable quantity and duration are different asserted meanings.
8. Quantity times a unit price requires a qualified price denominator and currency/context policy. Do not encode currencies as UCUM units or reuse a scalar multiplier as proof of pricing compatibility. Research the adjacent Price contract before claiming an invoice-line engine.
9. Locale formats the presentation only. Keep language-tag validity, localized labels and host identity separate; this increment need not implement translation.
10. Duration measured in seconds does not implement working calendars or availability. WM-XCT-009 remains the temporal neighbor; WM-XCT-010 location cannot choose a calendar automatically.

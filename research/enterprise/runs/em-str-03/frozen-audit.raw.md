# Frozen semantic audit — EM-STR-03 (single pass, closed)

## 1. Verdict

**REVISE.**

The fixed identity decision is sound and is not reopened here. The *artifacts* do not yet carry it: the outcome-class identity rule has no enforceable expression, the non-system-realizer invariant is vacuous as drafted, coverage/missingness invariants reference records that do not exist, both candidates lack any version or knowledge-time apparatus, the profile artifact re-imports the retired meaning through its own `bases` list, and the WM-ACT-001 narrowing is asserted with no migration mechanism. These are defects in the submitted artifacts, not consequences of the registry holds.

---

## 2. Material defects

### D1 — Outcome-class identity is declared but unenforceable
`allocation-candidate.json` (BusinessCapability): `"identityTest":{"stableIdentity":"An enterprise ability defined by an outcome class and acted-upon object remains identifiable…"}` and invariant `"Capability identity is outcome-defined…"`. But `"objects":{"BusinessCapability":{"identity":["businessCapabilityId"]}}` — identity is a bare surrogate. There is no uniqueness, equivalence or deduplication rule over `(outcomeClass, actedUponObject)`, and `outcomeClass` is an unstructured required field with no scheme, vocabulary or term reference. Two records with identical outcome class and object are both well-formed with different surrogates. grok-study names this exactly: `"Outcome-class naming, equivalence, and deduplication rules are undefined."` claude-study specifies an identity priority (`"capability-register master key → governed IRI → minted UUID/ULID marked surrogate"`) that the artifact does not carry — there is no master-key field and no surrogate marking.

### D2 — Two incompatible change mechanisms for the same trigger
`identityTest.versionIdentity`: `"Changes to outcome semantics, applicability or governed scope create immutable capability versions"`. Fixture `new-outcome-class`: input `"A governed ability changes to a materially different outcome class."` → expect `"A successor or distinct capability is created with lineage."` One says *version under stable identity*, the other says *new identity*. Neither names the boundary between them. The expectation is itself disjunctive (`"successor **or** distinct"`) and therefore unexecutable as a test. Compounding this: `objects.BusinessCapability` has no version field at all — no `versionId`, no effective interval, no `predecessorRef` — so the versioning limb cannot be represented even if chosen.

### D3 — Decomposition is owned but not modelled
`boundary.owns` includes `"capability decomposition edges"` and `"tier and specialization metadata"`; invariants require `"Tier labels are versioned edge attributes and never identity"` and `"Re-decomposition supersedes edges while capability identities remain stable."` `objects` contains one object, `BusinessCapability`, with no `parentRef`, no edge object, no edge validity interval, no edge supersession field. Three invariants have no bearer. Edge mastership is also undefined — an edge spans two capabilities and `owns` assigns it to the capability record without saying which end. claude-study's `"siblings are mutually exclusive at that tier"` is dropped from the reconciled invariants and from both fixture sets.

### D4 — BusinessCapability boundary leaks into realization
`boundary.excludes` contains `"capability realization binding"`, yet `boundary.owns` contains `"required realization and assessment references"` and `boundary.references` lists `{"target":"WM-ORG-002","purpose":"Organizational-unit **realizer**"}`, `{"target":"WM-ACT-003","purpose":"Process or workflow realization method"}`, `{"target":"WM-MAT-008","purpose":"Performance observation"}`. These are the realization-side referents, duplicated verbatim in the CapabilityRealization candidate. Two directed representations of the same binding exist with no declared authoritative direction, so they can diverge. `"accountability and stewardship bindings"` (BC `owns`) and `"accountable party"` (CR `owns`) duplicate accountability across both objects with no precedence rule.

### D5 — WM-ACT-001 is referenced by the wrong candidate; non-agent capacity has no referent
BusinessCapability references `{"target":"WM-ACT-001","purpose":"Agent-scoped function, competence, proficiency and capacity"}` while its own invariant states `"Agent Function or Competence and Business Capability never share identity or mastership."` The reference has no stated use on the capability and re-establishes the coupling the fixed decision severs. Conversely, CapabilityRealization — which carries `capacityRef` and whose invariant is `"Capacity is period-scoped and attaches to realization"` — does **not** list WM-ACT-001 in its references at all, although claude-study locates capacity in `WM-ACT-001 capacityStatement`. Additionally, `capacityStatement` is agent-scoped; realizers of kind system, resource or supplier have no capacity referent anywhere.

### D6 — The non-system-realizer invariant is vacuous; realizer granularity contradicts the acceptance scenario
CapabilityRealization `required` includes `accountablePartyRef`, so **every** well-formed binding has an accountable party. Invariant `"Full realization requires an accountable non-system realizer"` is therefore satisfied by construction on any record whose accountable party is a unit or position — including a system-only binding. The negative fixture `system-alone-full` (`"A system-only binding is labelled full realization"` → `"The conclusion is rejected."`) cannot be constructed as a failing case. The root equivocation: invariant 1 is `"Every realization binds one capability to one explicit realizer"`, but claude-study's acceptance tuple is `R1(C, P, primary, accountable U1)` — process realizer plus unit accountable, i.e. two participants — and the fullness invariant counts the accountable party *as* a realizer. grok-study records the open question: `"Realization granularity is undecided: one realization per binding tuple, or one realization with many participants."` Separately, fullness is a property of the binding **set** as-of a date; no object or field bears a fullness claim.

### D7 — Self-cancelling system-realizer rules; evidence sufficiency absent
Adjacent invariants in the same file: `"Full realization requires an accountable non-system realizer."` and `"A system-only binding is supporting realization **unless stronger governed evidence exists**."` The second voids the first, and contradicts local-evidence (`"A system is at most a supporting realizer"`) and claude-study (`"A system is at most a supporting realizer"`). The exception is unbounded because grok-study records `"Evidence-sufficiency rules are missing, including how supplier or system support counts."` `evidenceRefs` is optional and carries no digest and no criterion linkage, though local-evidence requires `"Evidence carries digest and criterion linkage."`

### D8 — Coverage share: required denominator has no field, overlap has no rule
Invariant: `"Coverage share declares basis, denominator and effective period."` The object offers `coverageShare` and `coverageBasis`, both optional, and **no denominator field**; the effective period is only the binding's own `validFrom`/`validTo`, which is not necessarily the share's measurement period. Fixture `partial-realization` (`"Two teams realize different shares of one capability"`) exercises concurrent shares with no summation, overlap or common-denominator constraint. Nothing declares coverage to be derived-only, so a stored coverage value on BusinessCapability is not prohibited — and BC `owns` `"required realization and assessment references"` gives it a landing place.

### D9 — The completeness assertion is relied on and does not exist
BC invariant `"Missing realization or measurement is unknown or absent, never zero"`; CR invariant `"Missing bindings mean unknown unless scoped completeness proves coverage"`; local-evidence `"unless a scoped completeness assertion proves coverage of the relevant population"`; claude-study `"Map completeness is an explicit scoped assertion, never implied."` No completeness-assertion object, field or referent appears in any artifact — no population, scope, as-of, asserter or method. The exception clause of a core invariant points at nothing, so the invariant has no decision procedure.

### D10 — Coverage-state vocabulary conflicts with the unknown rule and conflates dimensions
`profile-candidate.json`: `"Coverage projections distinguish unrealized, partially realized, realized-but-low-maturity and unassessed."` Four states, all determinate; **no `unknown`** — directly contradicting D9's invariants that missing bindings are unknown. Separately, `realized-but-low-maturity` fuses the realization dimension with the maturity dimension inside one state value, against the invariant `"Maturity, performance and capacity remain separate dimensions with separate evidence"` in the same file.

### D11 — Scenario separation has no discriminator
BC `excludes` `"scenario or target-state projection"`; both candidates assert `"Scenario values never become authoritative actuals."` CapabilityRealization has no `assertionMode`, no `scenarioRef` and no target-state flag. The only candidate discriminator is lifecycle state `proposed`, which is ambiguous between "draft record" and "planned future realization". A scenario binding and an actual binding are indistinguishable at the field level, so the invariant is unenforceable.

### D12 — No version or knowledge time; "never rewrites history" cannot hold
`objects.BusinessCapability` has **no temporal field of any kind** — no register effective interval, no assertion time, no source or asserter. CapabilityRealization has `validFrom`/`validTo` only: valid time, no transaction/knowledge time, no asserter, no provenance. claude-study requires keeping separate `"capability register effective interval; realization validity interval; assessment validity window; observation phenomenon/result/ingestion times; assertion knowledge time."` With valid time only, an in-place correction of an error is indistinguishable from a real-world change, and the invariant `"Transfer creates a successor binding and never rewrites prior accountability"` has no storage model to enforce it.

### D13 — Succession chain is breakable
`successorRef` is **optional**; there is no `predecessorRef`; no rule binds `R1.validTo` to `R2.validFrom`; no rule forces `R1.status` to a terminal state on transfer; `validTo` is optional even for terminal states. The acceptance result asserts `"R1 closes at transfer time and successor R2 opens with U2 accountable"` and fixture `unit-transfer` expects `"R1 closes and successor R2 opens"` — neither is derivable from the declared fields. A record with `status":"ended"` and no `validTo` is well-formed.

### D14 — Lifecycle states are undefined or overlapping
Both objects carry `constrained` with no definition, no entry/exit condition and no declared effect on coverage projections. BusinessCapability carries `deprecated`, `superseded` and `retired` with no distinction and no transition rules; `superseded` is permitted with `successorRef` absent. CapabilityRealization carries both `transferred` and `superseded` with overlapping meaning — the transfer fixture does not say which applies.

### D15 — Required level, actual level and scale have no bearer
local-evidence: `"Required level is a demand statement with driver, horizon and pinned scale."` claude-study's minimal set includes `"CapabilityRequirement / level statement (contained)"` — contained in nothing that exists. No requirement object, no `scaleRef`, no scale version pin appears in any artifact, while `profile-candidate.json` requires assessments to pin `"criteria, scale, aggregation, decision rule"`. The invariant `"Required level, actual level, maturity, performance and capacity remain distinct"` has no records to be distinct across. *Hold/defect split:* publication of a scale registry is a hold; the absence of a `scaleRef` slot and a designated scale-owner field in these artifacts is an artifact defect.

### D16 — Resolvability and non-cascade are asserted without mechanism
`"Retired capabilities remain resolvable for historical realizations and assessments"` and `"Ended realizations remain resolvable for historical coverage and assessment"` — no tombstone field, no prohibition on hard delete, no referential-integrity rule, although claude-study notes WM-ORG-002 does carry `"lineage and tombstones"`. grok's invariant 16 (`"Ending a realization does not retire the capability"`) is absent from the reconciled set, and nothing prevents a retirement cascade when the last binding ends.

### D17 — The profile artifact re-imports the retired meaning and has no governance shape
`profile-candidate.json` `"bases":["WM-ACT-001","WM-ACT-034","WM-KNW-011","WM-MAT-008","WM-ORG-002","WM-ORG-003","WM-ORG-004"]` while its first constraint reads `"WM-ACT-001 is narrowed … and never acts as the Business Capability master."` Under the fixed decision only WM-ACT-034 is profiled; listing WM-ACT-001 as a *base* of the capability-assessment profile conflates "profiled base" with "referenced model" and re-admits the retired enterprise-capability meaning into the profile's own derivation. The profile also carries no `allocationState`, no `canonicalPublishable`, no `holds`, no version, no fixtures and no validation policy — unlike both candidates.

### D18 — Validation policies are unbound and purely numeric
`candidate-allocation-offline-business-capability/validation-policy.json` and `candidate-allocation-offline-capability-realization/validation-policy.json` are byte-identical and contain **no** `candidateName`, `contourId` or target. Neither policy is bound to the artifact it governs; either can be applied to either, or to the profile, silently. Both candidates pass on counts (12 invariants ≥ 8; 6 references ≥ 3; 6 fixtures ≥ 3) while every defect above survives validation. The policy requires no `expectedCode` and no mapping from declared invariants to fixtures.

### D19 — Fixture defects
- `reorganization-survival` input: `"A capability moves from unit U1 to U2"` — under the fixed decision a capability does not move; a realization does. The input encodes the rejected ownership semantics, and contradicts the acceptance scenario where *process P* transfers.
- `system-is-full-capability` and `process-redesign` are realization-side rules filed under the **BusinessCapability** fixture set, so the rules they exercise are attributed to the wrong artifact.
- `system-support` expects `"coverage is evidence-based"` — no observable field outcome; untestable.
- No fixture in either set carries an `expectedCode`, so no negative case can be shown to fail for the declared reason.
- Uncovered declared invariants include: unknown-vs-zero on BusinessCapability; scenario leakage; edge supersession under re-decomposition; capacity misattachment; finalized-assessment immutability and non-recomputation against a later scale; required vs actual level authority; assessment not renaming a capability; retired capability resolvability; concurrent realizations.

### D20 — The WM-ACT-001 narrowing is unsafe as specified
local-evidence: `"Narrow legacy WM-ACT-001 to agent-scoped Function, Competence, Proficiency and Capacity."` grok-study: `"Double-count risk remains until enterprise-capability usage is explicitly retired from WM-ACT-001."` The artifacts assert the retirement and supply no mechanism: no crosswalk, no detection rule for existing `capability` / `capabilityAttribution` instances that carried enterprise meaning, no write-freeze at an effective date, no default-to-unknown for unmapped rows, and no prohibition on auto-promoting an agent competence record into a BusinessCapability — which is precisely grok's second named failure mode (`"treating agent competence … as the enterprise capability, so identity moves with skilled people"`). The narrowing is also a breaking change to a published legacy model recorded with no version bump, deprecation note or migration-boundary record in these artifacts.

### D21 — Adjudicated invariants dropped without disposition
The reconciled twelve-item list silently loses: grok 13 `"Assessment does not create or rename the capability"`; grok 15 `"Multiple realizations of one capability may coexist"`; grok 16 `"Ending a realization does not retire the capability"`; claude-study `"siblings are mutually exclusive at that tier"`; claude-study `"separate determiner from decision authority"`. Dropping adjudicated content with no accept/reject/defer record is a reconciliation defect independent of whether each rule is right.

### Treated as holds, not defects
Registry non-allocation for both candidates; WM-ACT-001 and WM-ACT-003 being legacy `described-previous-version` under migration-boundary-review; WM-ACT-034 / WM-KNW-011 / WM-MAT-008 single-provider-waiver reviewable drafts; WM-ORG-002/003/004 unresolved boundary items; empty relation contracts and absent capability edges in the frozen relations list; absence of a published scale registry, semantic crosswalk and source-master confirmation; no canonical completeness, installability or publication-readiness claim.

## 3. Required bounded fixes

1. **D1** — Add to `BusinessCapability`: `outcomeClass` as `{schemeRef, code|governedTerm}`; a declared natural key `(outcomeClassRef, actedUponObjectRef)` with a uniqueness rule and a stated equivalence/deduplication procedure; `identityBasis` enum `masterKey|governedIri|surrogate` with surrogates explicitly marked.
2. **D2** — Choose one mechanism and state the boundary test: refinement of definition/applicability → new immutable version under unchanged `businessCapabilityId`; replacement of the outcome class → successor capability with new identifier. Add `versionId`, `effectiveFrom`, `effectiveTo`, `predecessorRef`. Rewrite the `new-outcome-class` expectation as a single deterministic outcome.
3. **D3** — Add a `CapabilityDecompositionEdge` object: `parentRef`, `childRef`, `tier`, `validFrom`, `validTo`, `supersedesRef`, `edgeVersionId`; declare the parent as edge master; restore sibling exclusivity (or record an explicit deferral with reason).
4. **D4** — Remove `"required realization and assessment references"` from BC `owns`; declare `CapabilityRealization.capabilityRef` the single authoritative direction; retarget the BC WM-ORG-002 reference purpose to register stewardship only; remove WM-ACT-003, WM-MAT-008 and WM-ACT-034 realization-side purposes from BC; state that BC `owns` register stewardship while CR `owns` operational accountability.
5. **D5** — Remove WM-ACT-001 from BC references; add it to CR references with purpose "agent competence and period capacity of an agent realizer"; declare a non-agent capacity referent or mark `capacityRef` admissible only for agent-kind realizers.
6. **D6** — State in the artifact that `accountablePartyRef` is **not** a realizer; retain one-realizer granularity explicitly; introduce a `CapabilityCoverageClaim` bearer (`capabilityRef`, `scopeRef`, `asOf`, `claimGrade`, `basis`) and move the fullness test onto it, evaluated over the binding set.
7. **D7** — Delete `"unless stronger governed evidence exists."` Make `evidenceRefs` structured: `{evidenceRef, digest, digestAlg, criterionRef}`. Add an evidence-sufficiency rule, or record its absence as a hold that blocks any fullness claim.
8. **D8** — Make `coverageShare` a structure `{value, basis, denominatorRef, periodFrom, periodTo}`, required whenever a share is asserted; add a same-denominator reconciliation rule for concurrent bindings; state that coverage is derived-only and may not be stored on BusinessCapability.
9. **D9** — Add a `CompletenessAssertion` object: `scopeRef`, `populationDefinition`, `asOf`, `asserterRef`, `method`, `validTo`; make it the only licence for reading absence as unrealized.
10. **D10** — Replace the four-state list with an orthogonal pair: `realizationState ∈ {unrealized, partial, realized, unknown}` × `maturityState ∈ {assessed(level), unassessed}`.
11. **D11** — Add required `assertionMode ∈ {actual, scenario}` on CapabilityRealization and `scenarioRef` when `scenario`; restrict `proposed` to a record-draft meaning; exclude `scenario` from authoritative projections by rule.
12. **D12** — Add to BC: `effectiveFrom`, `effectiveTo`, `assertedAt`, `asserterRef`, `sourceRef`. Add to CR: `assertedAt`, `supersedesRecordRef`, `asserterRef`; forbid in-place field mutation; corrections become new knowledge-time records.
13. **D13** — Make `successorRef` required on transfer; add `predecessorRef`; require `R1.validTo == R2.validFrom` or a declared `gapReason`; require `validTo` whenever status is terminal.
14. **D14** — Define `constrained` (entry, exit, projection effect) or remove it; make `deprecated`/`superseded`/`retired` disjoint with declared triggers; forbid `superseded` without `successorRef`; assign `transferred` to realizer change and `superseded` to record replacement, disjointly.
15. **D15** — Add a `CapabilityRequirement` object (`capabilityRef`, `requiredLevel`, `scaleRef`, `scaleVersion`, `driverRef` → WM-KNW-011, `horizon`, `authorityRef`); add `scaleRef` + `scaleVersion` to the assessment profile; keep the scale registry's publication as a hold.
16. **D16** — Add `tombstone` semantics and a no-hard-delete rule to both objects; add the explicit invariant that ending the last realization does not change capability status.
17. **D17** — Reduce `bases` to `["WM-ACT-034"]`; move the rest to a separate `referents` list with WM-ACT-001 appearing only as a CR referent; add `allocationState`, `canonicalPublishable`, `holds`, `profileVersion`, a fixtures file and a bound validation policy.
18. **D18** — Add `target`/`candidateName` + `contourId` to each validation policy; add `requiresExpectedCodeOnNegativeFixtures: true` and `requiresEveryInvariantMappedToFixture: true`; add a third policy for the profile.
19. **D19** — Rewrite `reorganization-survival` so the *realization* transfers; move `system-is-full-capability` and `process-redesign` to the CapabilityRealization set; restate `system-support` as a field-level expectation; add `expectedCode` throughout; add fixtures for every currently uncovered invariant.
20. **D20** — Add a migration section: narrowing effective date; write-freeze on enterprise-capability use of WM-ACT-001 from that date; a detection rule for dual-meaning legacy instances; an explicit prohibition on auto-promotion, with unmapped instances resolving to `unknown`; a deprecation/version note on the WM-ACT-001 boundary record.
21. **D21** — Add a disposition table for every adjudicated invariant from both studies with accept/reject/defer and a reason; reinstate the five dropped rules or record the rejections.

Publication holds

  • Absence-of-external-review hold: one Claude Sonnet and one Grok attempt for WM-ECO-025 each timed out after 120 seconds; no external result was admitted.
  • Relation hold: WM-ECO-024 is only a provisional parent signal and the frozen relation ledger contains no settled WM-ECO-025 edge.
  • Boundary hold: return request, eligibility, authorization, reverse shipment, receipt, inspection, disposition, refund obligation, credit note, payment return or reversal, chargeback and dispute retain separate identities.
  • Profile hold: goods, services, digital content, access, consumer, business, marketplace, subscription, travel and regulated-sector cases require separate conformance profiles.
  • Quantity and condition hold: every requested, authorized, handed-over, received, inspected, accepted and dispositioned value requires subject, unit, lot or serial, source, method, time and uncertainty.
  • Financial hold: price, tax, fee, discount, deposit, deduction, allocation, currency and rounding require authoritative invoice, tax, accounting and payment references.
  • State-axis hold: request, eligibility, authorization, custody, receipt, inspection, disposition, credit, instruction, execution, settlement, customer receipt and closure states and clocks must not collapse.
  • Authority hold: legal eligibility, return authorization, custody, condition judgment, physical disposition, accounting adjustment, refund execution, disclosure and destruction require delegated authority.
  • Jurisdiction hold: CISG and EU consumer, goods, digital and product-safety rules are scoped profiles; national, sector, tax, payment-scheme and contract rules need validation.
  • Source-access hold: UNECE pages were indexed but direct automated access was publisher-blocked, and the official IFRS issued-standard document redirected to sign-in.
  • Interoperability hold: every UBL, UN/EDIFACT, ISO 20022, EU, IFRS, EPCIS, CBV, Schema.org, RFC, PROV, DQV, ODRL and OpenAPI projection needs release pins, conformance evidence and loss declarations.
  • Privacy hold: party, address, account, payment, item, serial, condition, fraud, dispute and evidence data require purpose-bound access, minimization and retention controls.
  • Independent external review was explicitly waived by the repository owner; this codex-only result remains a reviewable draft.

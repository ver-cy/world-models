Publication holds

  • Absence-of-external-review hold: one Claude Sonnet and one Grok attempt for WM-FLW-010 each timed out after 120 seconds; no external result was admitted.
  • Aggregate hold: every instance must declare route, itinerary or combined-plan, and a future major version may split the aggregate into linked models.
  • Boundary hold: journey execution, networks, places, schedules, services, people, vehicles, bookings, activities, shipments, incidents, routing engines, source datasets and evidence remain external.
  • Terminology hold: route, line, journey pattern, trip, itinerary, segment, leg, waypoint, stop, call and visit have profile-specific meanings.
  • Standards-status hold: OGC API Routes and Route Exchange Model are developing or proposed material as of 2026-09-06, not approved OGC standards.
  • Mode hold: pedestrian, bicycle, road, rail, maritime, aviation, indoor, private, public, shared, freight and autonomous-route profiles need independent validation.
  • Jurisdiction hold: safety, accessibility, traffic, border, passenger-rights, privacy, licensing and retention rules depend on region and operating context.
  • Computation hold: routing and optimization algorithms, traffic prediction, feasibility solvers and benchmarks remain external implementation concerns.
  • Feasibility hold: a computed or selected plan does not prove resource availability, reservation, legal permission, safety or successful execution.
  • Privacy hold: personal itineraries and precise locations need purpose limitation, minimization, consent or other authority, protected access and deletion profiles.
  • Interoperability hold: every Transmodel, NeTEx, GTFS, OGC, ISO, EU, Schema.org, GeoSPARQL, GeoJSON, OWL-Time, PROV, DQV and ODRL projection needs exact release pins, conformance evidence and semantic-loss declarations.
  • Review hold: transport planning, geospatial, accessibility, safety, privacy, optimization, data governance, records and independent external review are outstanding.
  • Independent external review was explicitly waived by the repository owner; this codex-only result remains a reviewable draft.

  • Create independently validated pedestrian, bicycle, road, rail, maritime, aviation, indoor, multimodal, freight, evacuation and autonomous-route profiles.

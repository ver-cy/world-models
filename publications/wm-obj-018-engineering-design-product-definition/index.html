Publication holds

  • No independent external research or independent reviewer: Claude and Grok each timed out once at 120 seconds. Source-grounded Codex research and a separate frozen no-tools Codex self-audit are used under owner authorization. This remains reviewable-draft, not canonical.
  • AP242 licensed clauses and sector-specific engineering/metrology standards were not inspected. ReqIF semantic mapping and source-to-field conformance are untested. No normative standards compliance is claimed.
  • NIST PMI evidence uses historical selected fixtures and cannot demonstrate current software capability or universal product completeness. NASA process guidance requires local applicability decisions.
  • No executable instance-validation schema, CAD interpreter, PMI adapter, real design fixture or verified round-trip translator is included. Nested field groups are proposed semantics requiring implementation and tests.
  • Physical capabilities, tolerances, recognition and safe-use claims require product-specific evidence and qualified engineering review. Information-model validity never authorizes physical action or certification.
  • The adopting Dimension must instantiate actual design authority, release roles, access controls and retention policy before live engineering records are created or released.
  • Independent external review was explicitly waived by the repository owner; this codex-only result remains a reviewable draft.

Deferred research

  • Executable field-level schema and failure fixtures for conflicting authority, stale baseline, unresolved requirement and concurrent change.
  • Sector-specific engineering geometry, metrology, material, interface and physical-assurance evidence.
  • Licensed standard inspection and version-specific CAD/PMI/ReqIF crosswalks with explicit unsupported constructs.
  • Native-versus-derivative real-product tests for geometry, units, tolerances, semantic annotations and requirement traceability.
  • Independent external research and adversarial review of the completed information contract.

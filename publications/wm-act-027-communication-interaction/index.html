Communication Interaction

Provide the format-neutral context an agent needs to open, conduct, evidence, classify, correlate and close a communication interaction - a bounded exchange of contact turns between identified parties across one or more channels - with its participant roster, channel and endpoint bindings, turn content, delivery evidence, lifecycle state, outcome and governance references.

Risk / Opportunity

Govern the identified risk or opportunity as a persistent, identified record of an uncertain future event or condition whose occurrence would affect stated objectives, carrying its articulation, classification, likelihood and consequence estimates, evaluation against criteria, response decision, lifecycle state, ownership and provenance.

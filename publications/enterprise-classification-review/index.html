Required adoption limits

Use only with a complete frozen scheme release, an exact finite profile and independently authorized host context. Preserve every applicable correspondence before profile filtering. Treat candidate-only as a proposal; read the capacity, custody and native-binding limits before installation.

Publication addendum and exact limits

Use in a Company Dimension

Start with a complete frozen scheme release, a finite slot profile and an assignment snapshot. Review restrictions without an assignment when needed. Three synthetic contexts and a separately tested native binding illustrate use.

Research assurance: reviewable-draft. This is a bounded original companion. Original offline assessment dialect. Scheme owners and live business subjects stay external. Scores, correspondence and a passing review confer no identity or write authority. Adoption limits.

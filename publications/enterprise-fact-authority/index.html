Enterprise Fact Authority

Describe who is accountable for a fact, who maintains it, which source is preferred, who may submit a change, and how conflicts and history are retained in your Company Dimension.

4 bundles · 6 layers · 18 findings · 18 questions. Open a layer to inspect its findings, questions, expected answers, artifacts and permitted actions.

Use in a Company Dimension

Start with one governed fact scope, an accountable party, a manual or connected source and an explicit write grant. Add stewardship, competing sources and history as needed. The downloadable package includes the schema, reference validator and three synthetic Dimension examples.

Research assurance: reviewable-draft. EM-XCT-02 remains partially covered. The host must authenticate actors and invoke the companion validator; native envelope validation alone is insufficient. Read adoption limits.

Bundles → Layers → Findings → Questions

Findings are information needs about the model, not additional entity types. Expected answers describe what to collect or inspect; they are not prefilled facts about your company. Listed actions confer no permission by themselves.

Question Q01

Is this a meaning, value assertion or ownership record?

Expected answer

  • Identify whether the record concerns meaning, values or ownership.
  • For FactAuthority, governs is values; consult the separate definitionAuthorityRef for meaning.

Artifact / evidence

boundary-decision + governs

Action and unknown handling

Explain; do not infer title or definition ownership

Finding · FA-identity

Stable identity

Question Q02

What survives rename/transfer?

Expected answer

  • Record ID, dimension, scope, predicate, revision and previousDigest.
  • For an observation, preserve subject/source; for a part, preserve its identity, kind, owner and party/source.

Artifact / evidence

record IDs, anchors, revision chain

Action and unknown handling

Follow exact lineage; transfer by new term, never merge parties

Finding · FA-meaning

Definition authority

Question Q03

Who defines this predicate?

Expected answer

  • The external definitionAuthorityRef and the predicate whose meaning it governs.
  • State whether the external reference was verified; this companion does not validate it.

Artifact / evidence

definitionAuthorityRef

Action and unknown handling

Resolve externally; insufficient context if absent/unverified

Finding · FA-accountable

Accountability for values

Question Q04

Who answers for values at T?

Expected answer

  • The exact fact-valid time and knowledge cut, matching authority IDs/revisions/digests and accountable party.
  • No applicable authority means unknown; overlapping authority IDs mean authority-contested.

Artifact / evidence

authority slice

Action and unknown handling

Read accountable; zero/multiple returns unknown/contested

Finding · FA-overlap

Overlapping authorities

Question Q09

Which of two authorities applies?

Expected answer

  • Every matching active authority ID and exact revision digest at the requested times.
  • Return authority-contested; do not choose by recency or silently merge policies.

Artifact / evidence

authority-contested outcome

Action and unknown handling

Governor investigates; no recency fallback

Finding · FA-transfer

Accountability transfer

Question Q12

What happens at responsible-party change?

Expected answer

  • Guarded closure of the old term, transfer cutoff and a new term with a new identity.
  • Preserve old effective coverage; a gap remains unknown and a correction is not an automatic transfer.

Artifact / evidence

closed old term + new term

Action and unknown handling

Governor records both; gap stays unknown

Question Q05

Who handles the disagreement?

Expected answer

Question Q07

Which source is preferred at fact time?

Expected answer

  • Applicable MastershipRule IDs, source URIs, priorities, valid terms and authority revision pins.

Question Q06

May this actor submit for source S now?

Expected answer

  • Authenticated caller, source, current trusted receipt time and matching WriteGrant.
  • Require one active source grant naming the caller plus current scoped authority/configuration; reject missing or ambiguous permission.

Artifact / evidence

Action and unknown handling

Question Q08

What if equal sources disagree?

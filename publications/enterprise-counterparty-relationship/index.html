EM-COM-01 · Enterprise profiles · Published 0.1.0

Enterprise Counterparty Relationship

Represent customer, supplier and partner roles for one Party without duplicating Party identity, upstream relationship records or contact endpoint values.

4 bundles · 8 layers · 16 findings · 32 questions

Explore structureSpecification and structure JSONDownload model packageAgent instructionsSemantic contract

Required adoption limits

Use only with authoritative Party resolution, pinned upstream records and the semantic evaluator. The profile does not own Party identity, relationship lifecycle, endpoint values, consent or classification schemes.

Publication addendum and exact limits

Use in a Company Dimension

Anchor one profile set to an authoritative Party and observing Dimension; bind existing customer/supplier/partner records; assign existing contact endpoints by purpose and time; retain sourced segmentation assertions.

Research assurance: reviewable-draft. This is a bounded original companion. No Party matching, endpoint ownership, consent decision, contract/order lifecycle or group-membership authority is supplied. Adoption limits.

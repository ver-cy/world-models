Goal / Objective

Represent a desired outcome as a governable record: what end state is wanted, for whom and by when, how attainment is measured, who is accountable, on what authority it stands, and how it is refined, changed and superseded — without owning the actions that pursue it, the metrics that measure it, or the systems that evaluate it.

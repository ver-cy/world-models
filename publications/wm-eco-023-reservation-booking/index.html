Reservation / Booking

Represent one governed request, hold and confirmation lifecycle for scarce or scheduled capacity so agents can understand what is sought, for whom, from which provider, under which terms, at what time and place, and with what operational evidence without confusing the reservation with inventory, contract, order, ticket, payment or fulfilment.

  • Cancellation hold: request, effective cancellation, expiry, no-show, capacity release, charge, refund, entitlement effect and record disposition require separate evidence and authority.
  • Authority hold: capacity commitment, term acceptance, charging, refunding, ticketing, transfer, cancellation, disclosure and disposition require delegated accountable authority.

  • Interoperability hold: every UN/EDIFACT, Schema.org, IATA, TM Forum, RFC, PROV, ODRL, DQV, ISO 4217 and OpenAPI projection requires version pins, conformance evidence and information-loss declarations.
  • Privacy hold: passenger, guest, attendee, patient, itinerary, location, accessibility, identity-document, loyalty, payment and contact data require purpose-bound access, minimization and retention controls.

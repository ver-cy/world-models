SBOM / Supply-chain Manifest

Model the software bill of materials as a governed, versioned, signable manifest record: its own identity and specification binding, the component inventory and identifier sets it carries, the asserted relationship graph, declared licensing and completeness, authenticity bindings by reference, and the manifest's publication, access and retention lifecycle.

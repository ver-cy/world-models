Health Care Delivery

Give an agent the system-side context needed to understand, populate, inspect and operate health care delivery: which provider organizations offer which services, through which authorized roles and at which physical, mobile, home or virtual locations; what capacity and readiness exist; how demand queues, allocations, referrals and transfers move people toward care; and how those records are stewarded, verified, measured and disclosed. Encounter lifecycle is delegated to the contained model WM-ACT-018; clinical, personal, workforce-qualification, organizational and estates facts stay with their owning models.

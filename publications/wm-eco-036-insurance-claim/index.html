Publication holds

  • Absence-of-external-review hold: one Claude Sonnet and one Grok attempt for WM-ECO-036 each timed out after 120 seconds; no external result was admitted.
  • Boundary hold: the aggregate owns claim identity, request-side assertions, lines and amendment lineage while policy, loss, evidence, handling, decision, settlement, payment, dispute and records masters remain external.
  • Relation hold: WM-ECO-003 CONTAINS WM-ECO-036 and WM-ECO-036 COMPOSE WM-ACT-045 remain candidate relations without approved cardinality or cascade semantics.
  • Insurance-line hold: life, health, disability, workers compensation, property, motor, liability, travel, cyber, marine, reinsurance, catastrophe and public-scheme claims need separate validation.
  • Jurisdiction hold: claim formation, deadlines, evidence duties, limitation, coverage, liability, remedy, privacy, retention and redress depend on jurisdiction, policy and insurance line.
  • State hold: notification, presentation, completeness, validity, coverage, liability, decision, settlement, payment, closure and finality remain independent.
  • Value hold: claimed, estimated, assessed, allowed, offered, reserved, settled and paid values remain separately sourced, timed and currency-qualified.
  • Authority hold: claimant assertion, observed fact, expert opinion, insurer position, settlement and regulator or court decision do not prove one another.
  • Identity hold: duplicate, related, parent-child, successor, multi-policy and multi-insurer relations require explicit evidence and must preserve predecessors.
  • Privacy hold: personal, health, financial, location, evidence, fraud and legal data require purpose limitation, minimization, access controls and auditable disclosure.
  • Source-access hold: ACORD licensed artifacts were not accessed; only official public overview material was used, and no ACORD conformance is claimed.
  • Maturity hold: HL7 FHIR R5 Claim-family resources are Trial Use and health-specific, not a universal claim model.
  • Interoperability hold: every FHIR, ACORD, code-list, temporal, provenance, quality and policy projection needs exact release pins, conformance evidence and loss declarations.
  • Review hold: insurance, claims, actuarial, legal, privacy, security, records and independent external review are outstanding.
  • Independent external review was explicitly waived by the repository owner; this codex-only result remains a reviewable draft.

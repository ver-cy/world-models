crs-origin-and-reference-point

CRS, site origin and reference point

Precise geospatial information shall be derived from project georeferencing entities relating the engineering coordinate system to a real-world CRS. IfcSite RefLatitude, RefLongitude and RefElevation, if asserted, establish the WGS84 point of local (0,0,0) for approximate use and are not a substitute for IfcMapConversion. INSPIRE recommends a reference point especially useful for small-scale portrayal. Observation time of a survey and event time of a legal boundary change are recorded separately.

    • orientation
  3. If approximate WGS84 latitude, longitude and elevation are stored on the site, what is their precision and are they consistent with the map conversion?spatial
    Expected answer
    • ref_latitude
    • ref_longitude
    • ref_elevation
    • precision_note

    Expected answer

Publication holds

  • Source and live-version verification is incomplete: the two providers cite IFC 4.3 from different hosts (ifc43-docs.standards.buildingsmart.org versus standards.buildingsmart.org) and different documentation builds, and grok-sourced Pset_SiteCommon, Pset_LandRegistration and IfcMapConversion claims must be re-fetched and pinned against the single host and version chosen for the base before merge.
  • Paywalled and blocked sources remain unverified at clause level: ISO catalogue entries (55001, 19650-1, 19650-3, 19152-1, 19152-2, 41011, 6707-1), IEC 62264-1 and the ENERGY STAR campus guide PDF were cited for title, edition and scope only; no clause-level wording may be published as quotation or requirement.
  • Multi-profile domain validation is not complete: the synthesised model must be exercised against at least three materially different site profiles (for example a university or hospital campus, a process or industrial site, and a port, data-centre or utility holding) before publication, since the evidence pack contains no instance-level validation.
  • Jurisdictional profile verification is outstanding: emergency planning (29 CFR 1910.38), regulatory site identity (EPA FRS), benchmarking (ENERGY STAR), physical security (NIST SP 800-53), contaminated land (CERCLA/RCRA) and construction wage envelopes (29 CFR 5.2) are all United States instruments; at least one non-US jurisdiction cross-check is required before the model is presented as jurisdiction-neutral.
  • No governed global location identifier scheme is asserted: GS1 GLN could not be fetched (HTTP 403) and ISO/IEC 81346 reference designation texts are paywalled, so any identifier-grammar claim must stay out of the published draft.

Deferred research

  • LADM SpatialUnitGroup binding for multi-parcel campuses, plus unregistered, customary and informal occupation, and condominium, strata and air-right fragmentation: decide whether the site model needs an explicit binding pattern or remains a reference to the land-administration sibling model.
  • Terminology collision between the statutory CERCLA/RCRA 'facility' (any site or area where a hazardous substance has come to be located, or all contiguous land under common control) and the ISO 41011 facility used by WM-BLT-006: an explicit overlay rule is needed so environmental-liability objects are never merged into the facility model or allowed to enlarge the managed extent.
  • IEC 62264 / ISA-95 role-based site alignment: confirm that a geometry-less logical enterprise site is an alignment target only and cannot satisfy canonical physical-site validation, and record where the enterprise hierarchy is authoritative for rough-cut planning.
  • Marine, subsurface, offshore and relocatable managed holdings: neither provider located a primary source defining a managed-site container for these cases; the vertical-extent and water-body accommodations are partial and the boundary is unresolved.
  • Non-US contaminated-land and major-hazard regimes (Seveso, IED, UK COMAH and equivalents) as overlays on the hazard and authorisation structure, to test whether the US-derived structure generalises.
  • Site-level accessibility requirements and biodiversity or ecological baseline accounting: both appear only as single questions or constraints, with no primary standard cited by either provider.

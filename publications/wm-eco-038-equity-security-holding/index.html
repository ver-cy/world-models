Equity / Security Holding

Represent one governed security holding as an identity-bearing, source-qualified position that binds a holder capacity and a security to an account or register context and preserves balances, rights, restrictions and change lineage without becoming the instrument, account, transaction, corporate action or legal ownership determination.

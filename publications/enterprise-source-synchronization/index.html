Enterprise Source Synchronization

Connect qualified source records to existing company subjects, preserve immutable intake evidence and explain synchronization progress without inventing identity or deletion.

5 bundles · 10 layers · 27 findings · 54 questions

Explore structureSpecification and structure JSONDownload model packageAgent instructionsSemantic contract

Required adoption limits

Start with SourceInstance, qualified acquisition scope and steward-reviewed aboutness to existing subjects. Metadata intake never creates or retires business subjects. Executable use needs trusted host identity, grants, time, evidence custody and current local-store ownership. Read the finite-capacity, shared-page, disclosure and recovery limits before installation.

Publication addendum and exact limits

Use in a Company Dimension

Begin with a declared source, qualified scope and reviewed aboutness mapping to an existing subject. The local reference and three synthetic profiles add atomic metadata receipts, snapshot coverage and a native restricted projection.

Research assurance: reviewable-draft. This is a bounded original companion. No live connectors, IAM, business fact application, distributed exactly-once, writable archive import, automated quarantine resolution or production-scale storage. Current host state and protected evidence custody are required. Adoption limits.
